# SIM-008 — Broker Negotiation Lab

## Employment outcome
The learner must demonstrate that they can take a verified, economically understood load opportunity and conduct a truthful, professional, authorized broker negotiation that results in one of four defensible states:

- `AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL`
- `VERIFY BEFORE DECISION`
- `DECLINE`
- `ESCALATE`

The learner must not confuse successful negotiation with completed booking. Module 09 owns formal booking and rate-confirmation/document controls.

## Locked operating framework
The simulation must operationalize:

**PREPARE → VERIFY FACTS → SET POSITION → OPEN → QUESTION → LISTEN → COUNTER → TRADE → CONFIRM → DOCUMENT → ACCEPT / DECLINE / ESCALATE**

This is a decision framework, not a script.

## Mandatory competency traceability
Every meaningful decision must map to one or more locked Module 08 competencies:

- **M08-C01** — Build a negotiation position from verified load/carrier facts.
- **M08-C02** — Convert Module 07 economics into supplied target/floor/walk-away positions.
- **M08-C03** — Distinguish asking rate, target, floor/minimum and walk-away.
- **M08-C04** — Open professionally and identify role/load without misrepresentation.
- **M08-C05** — Ask purposeful discovery questions and isolate missing/material facts.
- **M08-C06** — Listen for constraints, objections, assumptions and usable evidence.
- **M08-C07** — Construct an evidence-supported counteroffer.
- **M08-C08** — Use truthful leverage and reject fabricated leverage.
- **M08-C09** — Use conditional trades/concessions rather than uncontrolled concessions.
- **M08-C10** — Clarify agreement-specific accessorial/operating terms without inventing universal amounts.
- **M08-C11** — Maintain professional communication under resistance.
- **M08-C12** — Stay within supplied carrier/dispatcher authority and escalate when needed.
- **M08-C13** — Confirm agreed, unresolved and conditional terms accurately.
- **M08-C14** — Document negotiation and hand off to Module 09 without falsely representing booking completion.
- **M08-C15** — Decline professionally when the opportunity fails supplied carrier requirements.

Produce an explicit decision-to-competency coverage map. No important competency may exist only as decorative text.

## Simulation character — hard requirement
This must feel like a **live broker negotiation desk**, not a multiple-choice test with trucking words.

The learner must receive progressive information, make decisions before all later facts are known, experience broker responses/consequences, and adapt without losing first-attempt evidence.

Use fictional commercial/scenario data. Do not hard-code current market-rate claims as universal truth.

## Required progressive scenario conditions
The simulation must include a coherent sequence containing all of the following, either in one main case with branches or in tightly connected case segments:

1. A verified load opportunity with a broker's opening rate.
2. Supplied Module 07 economic output including target, floor/minimum and walk-away/approval condition.
3. A material fact that is initially missing and must be discovered rather than assumed.
4. A professional opening/role-identification decision.
5. A broker objection or resistance that requires listening/questioning rather than automatic concession.
6. A counteroffer requiring evidence-supported reasoning.
7. A real, verified leverage fact that may be used truthfully.
8. A tempting fabricated-leverage option that must be rejected.
9. A concession decision where uncontrolled movement is weaker than a conditional trade.
10. An agreement-specific term such as extra stop, detention, layover, TONU, lumper/reimbursement or another appropriate condition. Do not teach a universal accessorial amount.
11. A broker statement that is vague and must be clarified rather than converted into an agreed term.
12. A broker rate that reaches or approaches the supplied floor.
13. A later material fact change that alters the learner's prior negotiation position.
14. A broker-pressure moment pushing immediate commitment.
15. An authority-boundary event requiring the learner to ACCEPT within supplied authority, DECLINE, VERIFY or ESCALATE.
16. A closeout step requiring the learner to separate `AGREED IN PRINCIPLE`, `UNRESOLVED`, and `NOT ESTABLISHED` terms.
17. A documentation step recording counters, concessions/trades, broker statements, authority boundary, unresolved terms and next action.
18. A final Module 09 handoff decision where the learner must not mark the load formally booked merely because verbal negotiation aligned.

You may exceed 18 decisions when occupational depth requires it. Do not pad the count.

## Required critical-failure controls
Synchronize to the locked Module 08 master control. At minimum, the simulation must detect and preserve these six critical-failure families:

1. **Unauthorized below-floor acceptance** — knowingly accepts below a supplied carrier minimum/floor when supplied facts require authorization and no authorization is present.
2. **Fabricated leverage** — invents competing offers, market facts, truck position, driver facts or other leverage.
3. **Material misrepresentation** — materially misrepresents carrier/truck/driver/load facts.
4. **Unauthorized carrier commitment** — commits the carrier outside supplied authority.
5. **False agreement state** — treats an unresolved material term as agreed.
6. **Premature booking claim** — represents verbal negotiation/agreement-in-principle as completed booking before Module 09 controls.

Critical failures must affect competency/mastery state and remain visible after remediation. A corrected later answer must not erase first-attempt failure evidence.

## Consequences and remediation
Wrong decisions must create meaningful instructional consequences, not merely red/green feedback.

Examples:
- fabricated leverage damages professional/reputation state and triggers remediation;
- premature concession weakens negotiation position;
- failure to clarify a material term creates an unresolved-term consequence;
- unauthorized acceptance triggers critical failure and escalation remediation;
- false booking status triggers a Module 09 boundary remediation.

Remediation must diagnose the competency failure and require the learner to demonstrate corrected reasoning.

## Professional-language requirement

Do not reward theatrical aggression, fake certainty, threats, bluffing or memorized “broker killer” scripts.
