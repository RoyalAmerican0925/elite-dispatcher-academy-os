# Elite Dispatcher Academy — Module Completion Definition

**Effective:** 2026-09-02  
**Applies to:** Modules 01–15 and any future academy module.

The word **COMPLETE** is a controlled status. It may not be used for a module that is mostly built, substantially drafted, content-gate passed, regular-closeout passed, simulation-build passed, or merely represented in the master architecture.

A module is complete only when its required instructional competency chain is complete and verified:

**TEACH → DEMONSTRATE → PRACTICE → ASSESS → SIMULATE → REMEDIATE → RECORD COMPETENCY**

Required module-level controls include, as applicable to the competency:
- premium-depth student instruction;
- professional language and terminology;
- usable student tools/decision frameworks;
- realistic worked examples and occupational practice;
- knowledge check;
- exam and controlled answer/rationale layer;
- practical assessment and scoring/critical-failure logic;
- full module-specific instructor guide;
- diagnostic remediation and reassessment;
- aligned simulation with competency evidence;
- source register/currentness controls for regulated or date-sensitive claims;
- cross-module prerequisite/transfer alignment;
- commercial value review using Google, Employment, and Buyer tests;
- fresh completion verification.

No module may be advanced as complete while a required item above is missing or unresolved.

Academy-wide release-system controls—including LMS/account integration, durable production records, final browser/device QA, manual accessibility QA, assembled branding/rendering, commerce/delivery integration, and final publication/current-source review—are tracked separately. They must be finished before the academy is called publication-ready, but they must not be used to disguise an incomplete module-level instructional chain.

When reporting status, state the actual incomplete layer instead of substituting the word complete.
