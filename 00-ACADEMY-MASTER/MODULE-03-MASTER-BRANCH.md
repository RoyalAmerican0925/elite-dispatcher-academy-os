## Central control principle
**DO NOT BOOK FROM WEIGHT ALONE.**

A nominal equipment label, cargo-weight statement, or gross-weight figure does not by itself prove physical or legal compatibility.

## Source / regulatory controls
Primary-source reconciliation was refreshed during the premium closeout.

Current federal instructional anchors:
- 20,000 lb single axle on the Interstate System;
- 34,000 lb tandem axle on the Interstate System;
- Bridge Formula controls for other axle groups and the 80,000-lb gross framework on the Interstate System, subject to applicable exceptions/grandfather provisions;
- 102-inch vehicle-width framework on the National Network;
- 48-foot minimum semitrailer length states must generally permit for the relevant National Network tractor-semitrailer configuration, subject to grandfather provisions;
- H = hazardous materials, N = tank vehicle, X = combination tank vehicle and hazardous materials endorsement.

Do not teach 80,000 lb as a cargo-only or universal legality test. State, route, permit, configuration, commodity, and other applicable requirements remain separate controls.
