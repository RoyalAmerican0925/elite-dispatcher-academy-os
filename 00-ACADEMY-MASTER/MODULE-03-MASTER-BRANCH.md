# Module 03 Master Branch — Trucks, Trailers, Freight & Equipment

**Control status:** VERIFIED EXISTS / EARLIER COMMERCIAL GATE PASSED / PREMIUM-DEPTH RE-AUDIT REQUIRED

## Employment outcome
Student evaluates whether a load is compatible with a specific carrier, driver, and equipment combination and distinguishes good fit, bad fit, needs-more-information, and specialized/escalate conditions.

## Core competency families
- equipment identification and limitations
- commodity / equipment fit
- temperature requirements
- dimensions and overwidth recognition
- gross versus axle/tandem weight
- driver qualification / endorsement recognition
- facility/loading constraints
- missing-information discipline
- STOP / VERIFY / ESCALATE

## Professional language layer
Dry van; reefer; flatbed; payload; tare; GVW/GVWR; axle; tandem; bridge formula; legal width; oversize; permit; H/N/X endorsements; placarded hazmat; live loading; facility constraint; washout; TWIC where contextually relevant.
