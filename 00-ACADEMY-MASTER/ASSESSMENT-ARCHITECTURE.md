# Academy Assessment Architecture

## Purpose
Assessments must measure occupational understanding and performance, not reward memorization detached from dispatcher work.

## Module Evidence Stack
1. Embedded checks/practice — formative.
2. Knowledge check — diagnostic/formative.
3. Scenario/application work — judgment practice.
4. Module exam — independent knowledge/application evidence.
5. Practical assessment — integrated task performance.
6. Simulation — progressive work-context performance.
7. Remediation/reassessment — targeted repair where required.
8. Competency record — durable evidence of completion/mastery.

## Design Rules
- Build from a competency blueprint before adding questions.
- Every scored item traces to taught material.
- Use plausible beginner errors as distractors where appropriate.
- Include reasoning, classification, sequencing, document reading, calculations, verification, and next-action decisions according to module scope.
- Do not force multiple choice where an operational response is the competency.
- A correct answer reached through unsafe/incorrect reasoning may require instructor review when the reasoning itself is material.
- Critical failures are reserved for decisions whose consequences justify them; they are not drama devices.
- Scoring guides, student instructions, answer keys, and instructor guidance must use the same thresholds and logic.
- Professional-language precision may be scored when misuse materially affects understanding or work quality.

## Separation of Purpose
Knowledge check ≠ exam ≠ practical ≠ simulation. They may share competencies but must provide different evidence.

## Release Control
Assessment counts, thresholds, critical items, rationales, and keys must be recalculated and synchronized whenever content changes.
