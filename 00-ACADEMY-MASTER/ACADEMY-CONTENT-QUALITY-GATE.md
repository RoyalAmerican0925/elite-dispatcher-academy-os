# Academy Content Quality Gate

Every major Academy component must pass these gates before it can be treated as premium instructional content.

## 1. Occupational necessity
Does this teach knowledge or skill a working dispatcher needs, prevent a realistic beginner error, prepare a later competency, or provide a usable workflow/tool? If none apply, remove it.

## 2. Google test
Could a student obtain essentially the same value from a quick generic web search? If yes, add the missing occupational application: workflow, judgment, documents, consequences, exceptions, verification, realistic scenarios, tools or practice.

## 3. Employment test
What can the student now DO reliably that they could not do before this component? The answer must be observable.

## 4. Buyer test
Would a reasonable paying student see professional training rather than compiled internet information?

## 5. Language test
Are industry terms used precisely, taught in context and distinguished from common misuse? Can the student communicate the decision professionally?

## 6. Evidence test
Are legal/regulatory/date-sensitive claims controlled by appropriate authoritative sources? Are fictional training facts labeled as fictional?

## 7. Workflow test
Does the content show what information arrives, what the dispatcher checks, what decision is made, what can go wrong, what must be verified and what happens next?

## 8. Practice test
Does the student practice the competency before being graded on it?

## 9. Assessment trace test
Can every scored competency be traced to instruction and practice? Can every major course outcome be traced to evidence of mastery?

## 10. Simulation test
Where simulation is required, does it recreate meaningful work with incomplete information, competing considerations, progressive events, consequences, remediation and competency tracking rather than functioning as a disguised quiz?

## 11. Presentation test
Is the material clear, consistent, usable, accessible and worthy of the Academy brand? Visual polish cannot compensate for weak instruction, and strong instruction cannot excuse careless presentation.

## 12. Contamination test
No AI chatter, internal production notes, stale TODO/TBD markers, unsupported claims, accidental private correspondence, copied competitor language, or irrelevant project material may appear in student-facing content.

## Verdict
A component that fails a material gate is not complete. Length is never the criterion; occupational value is.
