# Student Journey Map

## Entry
1. Enroll/access Academy.
2. Complete orientation and technology check.
3. Understand program outcomes, assessment rules, simulation rules, professional-language expectations, and support/remediation process.
4. Establish student progress/competency record.

## Per-Module Learning Cycle
1. **Orient** — what the competency is and why dispatchers need it.
2. **Learn** — concepts, terminology, documents, workflows, boundaries.
3. **See** — worked examples, freight files, communication examples, demonstrations.
4. **Practice** — guided exercises and job aids.
5. **Check** — formative knowledge check and feedback.
6. **Apply** — scenarios and independent exercises.
7. **Prove** — exam/practical evidence.
8. **Simulate** — realistic progressive dispatcher work.
9. **Remediate** — repair specific gaps rather than simply repeat content.
10. **Record** — competency/progress evidence and unlock next appropriate work.

## Fluency Journey
Professional vocabulary accumulates rather than resets each module. Later modules should expect earlier terminology while adding new language in context.

## Graduation Journey
After required module competencies are demonstrated, the student completes the verified capstone/final integration requirement, resolves required remediation, and receives a final completion/competency record and certificate according to release policy.

## Customer Experience Standard
The student should never have to guess:
- what they are learning;
- why it matters;
- what to do next;
- what a document/tool is for;
- how work will be scored;
- why an answer/decision was wrong;
- which skill needs remediation;
- whether they have completed the requirement.
