# Reputation & Craftsmanship Standard

## Reputation Standard
Every released Academy component must demonstrate the level of accuracy, instructional value, professional presentation, and craftsmanship appropriate for a product whose creator publicly stands behind it.

## Ten-Part Release Quality Lens
1. **Accuracy** — claims, calculations, keys, workflows, and source-sensitive statements are correct.
2. **Depth** — instruction reaches occupational application rather than stopping at definitions.
3. **Professional Language** — terminology is precise, contextual, and used as the industry competency requires.
4. **Practical Usefulness** — students can apply the material to dispatcher work.
5. **Clarity** — a novice can follow the instruction without hidden assumptions.
6. **Consistency** — manuals, tools, assessments, simulations, keys, and instructor guidance agree.
7. **Presentation** — customer-facing materials look deliberate, readable, branded, and commercially credible.
8. **Competency Evidence** — the Academy can show what the student demonstrated, not merely what the student viewed.
9. **Commercial Value** — the product organizes knowledge into workflows, judgment, tools, realistic practice, assessment, and simulation that free information does not provide by itself.
10. **Craftsmanship** — details are checked; nothing is included merely to make the course look larger.

## No-Filler Rule
Every addition must materially do at least one of the following:
- teach knowledge a new dispatcher needs;
- build a skill used later;
- prevent a realistic beginner mistake;
- prepare for assessment/simulation;
- establish a necessary later-module foundation;
- provide a usable workflow, decision framework, example, artifact, or tool.

If it does none, remove it.

## Premium Tests
**Google Test:** If the section provides essentially the same value as ordinary top-level search results, deepen professional application, workflow, judgment, artifacts, consequences, exceptions, verification, tools, or practice.

**Employment Test:** State what the student can now reliably DO.

**Buyer Test:** The component must feel like professional training, not compiled internet information.

## Pride in Production
Craftsmanship is expressed through the quality of the work: accurate language, careful reasoning, clean documents, useful exercises, realistic simulations, verified answers, coherent visual presentation, and respect for the student's time and money.

## Prohibited Release Conditions
Do not release material containing filler, unsupported categorical claims, stale cross-module promises, unresolved contradictions, broken scoring, answer-key mismatches, fake proprietary documents, unlabeled fictional data, AI/process chatter, TODO/TBD placeholders, or presentation that has not passed visual QA.
