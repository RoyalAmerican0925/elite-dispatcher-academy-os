## Module 15 — Dispatcher Business Operations, Client Service, Systems, and Capstone Practice
There is a **control-map scope conflict that must be resolved before production**:
- The Authoritative Near-Term Module Map defines Module 15 as **Dispatcher Business Operations, Client Service, Systems, and Capstone Practice**.
- The Project-Control Module Map defines Module 15 as **Capstone and Integrated Dispatch Operations**, applying the full workflow in realistic end-to-end scenarios.

These are compatible only if Module 15 is intentionally designed as a combined business-operations + integrated-capstone module. That decision must be explicit. Do not silently drop the business-operations competency or silently overload a capstone with unrelated business-startup content.
