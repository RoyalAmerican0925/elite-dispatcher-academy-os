## Subpar-content purge standard for subsequent module passes
A file does not survive merely because it exists. During module-by-module premium review, remove or replace content that is:
- generic first-page-search material without operational transformation;
- repetitive filler;
- unsupported or stale regulatory/platform instruction presented as current;
- disconnected from a competency or workflow;
- assessment content not taught by the module;
- a tool with no real occupational use;
- a simulation choice that rewards guessing, trivia, fabrication, unsafe conduct, or false authority;
- an answer key mislabeled or used as an instructor product;
- a placeholder that can be mistaken for completed production;
- contradictory to the current Academy professional-language/authority framework.

## Verification requirement
Every future purge action must record exact path, disposition, reason, and replacement/current authority where applicable. Completion percentages are updated only after the affected production gate is reverified.
