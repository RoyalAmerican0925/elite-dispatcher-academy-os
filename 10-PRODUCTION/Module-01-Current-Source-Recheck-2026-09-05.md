## Findings
- FMCSA describes the **USDOT Number as a unique identifier** used to identify an employer/person/entity's operations in the registration/safety environment.
- **Operating authority is distinct from the USDOT Number** and relates to the authority/registration required for specified regulated operations where applicable.
- FMCSA continues to use **MC / FF / MX docket-number prefixes** in the Motus environment; new operating authorities receive their own docket numbers under current Motus changes.
- Motus became available to new/existing registrants in May 2026 and is the current registration environment; legacy registration tools were sunset/limited during the May transition.
- Therefore Module 01 should teach the conceptual distinction without telling learners to follow obsolete URS/legacy-portal procedures.

## Curriculum effect
The premium M01 exam/runtime uses conceptual wording such as:
- USDOT number = entity identifier in FMCSA safety/registration systems;
- operating authority = separate permission/registration concept for specified regulated operations;
- presence of one does not automatically prove the other.

This wording remains suitable for foundational training.
