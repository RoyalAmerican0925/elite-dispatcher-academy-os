## Occupational Outcome
The learner must independently determine whether a proposed dispatcher action remains within a represented carrier relationship or presents facts requiring verification, return/decline, or escalation. The learner must analyze actual conduct rather than rely on labels, payment methods, carrier count, tax status, or a contract as a universal safe harbor.

## Required Operating Model
**STOP → IDENTIFY → TRACE → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE**

For every material boundary event the learner must be able to identify:
- represented principal;
- freight source;
- who controls carrier selection;
- whether discretionary allocation exists;
- known facts versus assumptions;
- missing facts and verification source;
- applicable scope/authority document;
- defensible operational action;
- required documentation or handoff.

Required disposition language:
**PROCEED WITHIN SCOPE / MORE FACTS REQUIRED / STOP-VERIFY / RETURN OR DECLINE / ESCALATE FOR AUTHORITY OR LEGAL REVIEW**

6. Compensation and tax-status myths.
