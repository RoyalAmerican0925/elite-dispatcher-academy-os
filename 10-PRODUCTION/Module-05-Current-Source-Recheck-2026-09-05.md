# Module 05 Current-Source / Platform-Currentness Recheck — 2026-09-05

**Scope:** M05 sourcing, first-pass broker/entity verification and vendor-platform boundary language.

## FMCSA 2026 controls
Authoritative FMCSA material confirms:
- Motus is FMCSA's new registration system and public users can search an entity's registration record there.
- FMCSA's legacy Licensing & Insurance system is now historical-only for review; current Motus filings are not reflected there as of the agency's May 2026 FAQ.
- Broker/freight-forwarder financial responsibility remains tied to BMC-84 surety bonds or BMC-85 trust funds at the statutory/regulatory $75,000 level.
- Effective January 16, 2026, updated financial-responsibility rules limit acceptable BMC-85 trust assets and create suspension consequences when available financial security falls below $75,000 and is not replenished within the required period.

## Instructional consequence
M05 should teach **current-source verification**, not a memorized legacy website click path. Any lesson that says to use an older FMCSA system as the universal/current lookup method is superseded by the current-source control.

M05 remains first-pass sourcing verification. M06 owns deeper fraud/authority/financial-security verification.

## Load-board platform currentness
Official vendor materials continue to show that load search is platform-specific:
- Truckstop's current developer/search documentation exposes vendor-defined criteria such as origin/destination, equipment, age (`HoursOld`) and sorting.
- Truckstop's user documentation states its own search workflow and required fields; these are Truckstop behaviors, not universal rules.
- DAT's current official material describes searching through DAT One/mobile/desktop and its own search/post tools; its interface and subscription packaging are DAT-specific.

## Premium wording control
The Academy may teach transferable concepts such as:
- origin/destination/radius;
- equipment type;
- posting age;
- availability verification;
- deadhead;
- commodity/weight/appointment details;
- shortlist status;
- entity/contact verification.

It must **not** teach any vendor's exact UI, subscription tier, proprietary mileage value, credential-sharing permission, filter name or Terms-of-Service rule as universal freight-industry law or permanent workflow.

## Broker/entity versus contact authentication
An FMCSA registration/authority record verifies information about the registered entity. It does not, by itself, authenticate a caller, email address, domain, phone number, payment instruction or transaction instruction. M05's `VERIFY THE ENTITY → VERIFY THE CONTACT → VERIFY THE TRANSACTION FACTS` doctrine remains correct and should remain controlling.

## Fraud-indicator restraint
A duplicate or similar posting is evidence requiring further verification, not sufficient proof of double brokering or fraud. The controlled M05 wording remains appropriately restrained.

## Recheck disposition
**PASS WITH CONTROLLED CURRENTNESS BOUNDARY.** No wholesale curriculum rewrite is warranted. Preserve concept-level instruction and require a final pre-publication platform/current-source recheck because vendor behavior can change.
