# AI Context Packet Standard

## Purpose
Ensure every AI production assignment receives enough authoritative project context to produce compatible work without guessing about prior decisions.

## Required Packet Fields
Assignment ID and asset ID; target file/path; producing AI role; asset type and audience; purpose; approved learning objectives or system requirements; prerequisite modules/assets; downstream dependencies; controlled terminology; required facts/sources; date-sensitive items requiring verification; required structure; examples or prior patterns to preserve; exclusions and legal/professional boundaries; formatting/output requirements; acceptance criteria; unresolved questions; completion-report requirements.

## Curriculum Writing Structure
Where relevant, instructional packets should require treatment of WHAT, WHY, WHEN, HOW, APPLICATION, FAILURE MODES, DETECTION, RESPONSE, and MEASUREMENT. This structure is a coverage control, not a requirement to force unnatural headings into every student-facing page.

## Source Rule
A packet must provide actual prior definitions and necessary facts, not merely tell another AI to check earlier work it cannot access. If source material is missing, the assignment must identify the gap rather than silently inventing continuity.

## Completion Report
The producing AI should report what was completed, objectives covered, dependencies used, new terminology introduced, factual/regulatory claims needing verification, unresolved gaps, files/assets produced, and recommended downstream updates.

## Integration Rule
AI output remains DRAFT_COMPLETE until integrated against terminology, dependencies, sources, acceptance criteria, and neighboring assets. High-quality prose alone does not make an asset verified.
