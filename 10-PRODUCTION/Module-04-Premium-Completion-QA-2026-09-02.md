## 7. Current-source refresh — September 2, 2026
Primary FMCSA sources were rechecked for the two unusually time-sensitive 2026 topics.

### Motus / legacy registration systems
FMCSA's current Registration Modernization material states that Motus replaced registration functions in legacy systems in May 2026. Registration functions in legacy systems were impacted beginning May 14, 2026; all other customers could create Motus profiles beginning May 19, 2026. FMCSA also states that L&I is now available only for reviewing historical motor-carrier records and Motus filings are not reflected there.

Instructional control: teach the learner to identify the fact being verified and select the current authoritative source. Do not teach a historical interface or legacy system as the permanent registration workflow.

### NRII temporary paper-MEC exemption
FMCSA's current NRII Learning Center and April 10, 2026 bulletin confirm a temporary exemption effective **April 11, 2026 through October 11, 2026**. It permits covered interstate CDL/CLP holders and motor carriers to rely on a paper copy of the MEC as proof of medical certification for up to **60 days after issuance**, subject to the exemption's terms.

Instructional control: this temporary rule is date-sensitive and is not a permanent Academy rule. A paper MEC within the stated window does not authorize a dispatcher to make the carrier's final qualification determination. Reverify before any delivery after October 11, 2026 or if FMCSA changes the procedure earlier.
