## 3. Occupational controls inspected
The content includes controls against several common beginner errors:
- nominal trailer type is not treated as proof of exact unit-specific dimensions/tare weight;
- dry van is not treated as reefer capability;
- 104-inch width is not taught as automatically legal or automatically illegal without route/jurisdiction/permit analysis;
- a driver missing the stated required H/X qualification is treated as a do-not-dispatch condition for the fictional load;
- projected gross weight below 80,000 lb is not treated as proof that axle limits are satisfied;
- incomplete load information routes to verification rather than guessing.

These are appropriate Module 03 mental-model controls. Final legal/current-source wording remains governed by Module 03 source registers and assembled release QA.
