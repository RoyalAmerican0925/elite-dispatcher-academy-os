# AI Production Roles

## ChatGPT — Chief Architect and Integration Engine
Owns system architecture, repository organization, dependency control, context assembly, integration, acceptance criteria, and final synthesis across the academy, EAOS, and TKE.

## Claude — Senior Curriculum and Long-Form Writing Engine
Produces deep instructional content from complete context packets. Required structure should cover what, why, when, how, application, failure modes, detection, response, and measurement where appropriate. Claude must not assume access to prior sessions; context packets must carry required definitions, dependencies, and constraints.

## Gemini — Research and Cross-Check Support
Supports broad research, alternate synthesis, structured comparison, and secondary verification tasks when assigned. Outputs must still pass source and acceptance controls before entering production.

## Kimi — Consistency and Audit Support
Used for cross-document consistency checks, terminology drift detection, dependency conflicts, and large-context audit tasks where appropriate.

## Grok — Supplemental Review / Alternate Perspective
Used as an additional review and idea-generation channel where useful. No output is authoritative until integrated and verified.

## Control Rule
No AI may silently fill a missing dependency with an assumption. Missing context must be surfaced as CONTEXT REQUIRED, and factual uncertainty must be surfaced as VERIFICATION REQUIRED.
