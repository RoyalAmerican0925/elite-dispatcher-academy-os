## Confirmed defects to repair
1. `Student-Manual-Part-03.md` defines shipper as the party that owns the freight. This conflicts with the more accurate Part 01 treatment. Role and ownership must be separated.
2. Cross-module promises in the existing manual must be checked against the live curriculum before retention; older assumed Module 06/09 scopes cannot be trusted automatically.
3. Existing instruction relies too heavily on role definitions and one simplified lifecycle scenario; four-flow reasoning and dispatch-file interpretation are not sufficiently taught.
4. Existing Module 01 student tools cover role mapping and lifecycle sequencing but do not yet provide the complete four-flow, dispatch-intake, or verification framework required by the premium standard.
5. Existing practical foundation is useful but must demonstrate integrated workflow judgment, not only role identification.
6. SIM-001 is already functional and tested; it must be aligned after curriculum/assessment changes rather than rewritten indiscriminately.

## Premium-depth ruling
Module 01 is not complete when the student can define the parties. It is complete at this layer when a novice can inspect a basic fictional freight file, identify who is doing what, trace four separate flows, recognize the purpose of foundational documents, separate known facts from verification needs, determine the next foundational action, and know when not to guess.
