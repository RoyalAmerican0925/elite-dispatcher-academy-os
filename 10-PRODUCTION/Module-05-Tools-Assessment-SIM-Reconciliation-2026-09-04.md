7. `Module-05-Freight-Fraud-Red-Flag-Checklist.md` — converts indicators into verification work; does not declare fraud from a flag alone.
8. `Module-05-Rate-Confirmation-Review-Checklist.md` — compares written terms with confirmed terms and stops unresolved material discrepancies; Module 09 retains deeper booking/document control.
9. `Module-05-STOP-VERIFY-ESCALATE-Load-Decision-Tool.md` — turns uncertainty into an explicit proceed/verify/hold/escalate disposition.

### Tool purge decision
**NO TOOL DELETION.** The tools are not mere duplicates: they correspond to different stages/artifacts in the sourcing desk. Future edits should improve usability or synchronization, not collapse distinct evidence controls into one oversized worksheet.

### Six controlled critical-failure families

1. endorsement mismatch treated as viable;
2. shipper-first discretionary allocation treated as ordinary carrier-side sourcing;
3. duplicate-looking postings declared proven fraud/double brokering without verification;
4. verified entity treated as authentication of a new contact;
5. mismatched rate confirmation processed as though it matches;
6. signing authority assumed merely from document review.

No hidden critical failures are authorized.
