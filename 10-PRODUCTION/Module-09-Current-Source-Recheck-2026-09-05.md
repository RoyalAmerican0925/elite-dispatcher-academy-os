## Findings
- E-SIGN supports the narrow proposition that an interstate/foreign-commerce signature, contract, or record is not denied legal effect solely because it is electronic. It does not eliminate authority, assent, authenticity, or other substantive requirements.
- E-SIGN record-retention language supports accurate/accessibile/reproducible electronic records where another law requires retention; M09 does not invent a universal carrier-side retention period.
- Broker transaction-record rules are background broker obligations; M09 does not falsely teach them as a universal carrier rate-confirmation form requirement.
- FMCSA's broker-transparency proposal is treated as a proposal unless/until current final authority says otherwise.
- Agreement-specific detention, TONU, lumper, tracking, check-call, cancellation, payment, document-submission, and signature procedures remain agreement/carrier/platform specific in M09.
- No particular commercial e-signature, TMS, load-board, or document platform is taught as legally mandatory.
