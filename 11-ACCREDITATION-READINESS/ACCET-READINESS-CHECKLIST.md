# ACCET Readiness Checklist

Research baseline: August 2026. Reverify against current ACCET Standards, policies and forms before application because standards can change.

## Eligibility Preconditions
- [ ] Institution has an identified student body in a prescribed learning format/setting.
- [ ] Programs provide an interactive educational component appropriate to subject matter and expected outcomes.
- [ ] Applicable state licensing requirements are met.
- [ ] Applicable federal requirements are met where relevant.
- [ ] Institution/program clientele fit ACCET's eligible continuing/vocational education scope.
- [ ] Institution has continuously and successfully delivered education/training for at least two consecutive years immediately before application.
- [ ] At least one graduating class from the longest program has completed training.
- [ ] Institution has generally been under the same ownership/control for at least two years, subject to ACCET's waiver provisions.
- [ ] Institution offers eligible postsecondary awards within ACCET scope.
- [ ] Education/training has prescribed clock hours and, where applicable, credit hours or CEUs.
- [ ] Institution is prepared to comply with ACCET Bylaws, Principles of Ethics, Standards and policies.

## Institutional Evidence Areas
Build evidence for planning and management; human resources; financial stability and tuition practices; program/course design; practical experience where applicable; instructional delivery and supervision; recruitment; admissions/enrollment; student services; attendance/engagement; student progress and achievement; records; complaints; and continuous improvement.

## Distance-Learning Readiness
- [ ] Define the interactive distance-learning/asynchronous learning model using current ACCET terminology.
- [ ] Document meaningful instructional interaction and student support.
- [ ] Define clock-hour methodology and workload.
- [ ] Preserve LMS engagement/progress evidence.
- [ ] Document assessment integrity and identity controls where applicable.
- [ ] Maintain technical-support and accessibility processes.

## Program Documentation
Prepare current versions of program/course information, clock-hour breakdowns, admissions standards, curriculum, outcomes, assessments, practical exercises, instructor requirements, student policies, tuition/refund information and required disclosures.

## Initial Accreditation Process
- [ ] Create/use ACCET Accreditation Management System when eligible.
- [ ] Submit Inquiry Questionnaire and applicable processing fee.
- [ ] Confirm eligibility determination.
- [ ] Submit formal application and supporting documentation.
- [ ] Prepare the applicable Analytic Self-Evaluation Report for a vocational institution.
- [ ] Prepare for on-site/evaluation review and Commission action.

ACCET currently states that roughly 12–18 months is a reasonable estimate for the accreditation review for an eligible institution making the required investment, but eligibility operating history must exist first.
