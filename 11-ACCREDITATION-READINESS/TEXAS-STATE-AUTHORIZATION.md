# Texas State Authorization Readiness

## Why This Comes Before Accreditation
Both DEAC and ACCET require applicable state authorization/licensing/approval. Accreditation does not substitute for Texas authorization.

## Governing Texas Framework to Review
Texas Workforce Commission (TWC), Career Schools and Colleges, administers Texas Education Code Chapter 132 and TWC Chapter 807 rules for covered career schools/colleges. The rules provide for school certificates of approval, program approval, student enrollment documentation, conduct policies, tuition/fee disclosures, refunds, staff approvals where applicable, records and enforcement. TWC can issue cease-and-desist orders to unlicensed schools when licensure is required.

## Required Pre-Launch Determination
Before enrolling paying students, obtain a documented determination of whether the academy requires a TWC Career Schools and Colleges Certificate of Approval or qualifies for a statutory/regulatory exemption. Do not infer exemption merely because delivery is online.

## Authorization File to Build
- legal entity/ownership documents;
- assumed-name/brand records as applicable;
- school location and delivery model;
- program description and occupational objective;
- curriculum/module sequence;
- total clock hours and methodology;
- tuition and all fees;
- catalog;
- enrollment agreement;
- cancellation/refund policy;
- conduct/dismissal/reenrollment policy;
- admissions requirements;
- student complaint/grievance procedure;
- instructor/director qualification records as applicable;
- advertising and website claims;
- student recordkeeping plan;
- financial documentation and any required security/bond information;
- distance-education/LMS description;
- evidence of approval or exemption determination.

## Consumer-Protection Controls
Texas rules require accurate disclosure of tuition, fees and other charges for covered schools, and student enrollment documentation must be retained. The academy should implement these controls even while the precise regulatory classification is being resolved because they also support accreditation readiness.

## Red Flag
Do not market the school as TWC-approved, licensed, exempt, accredited, or accreditation-pending unless the relevant authority has actually granted that status and the wording is legally permitted.

## Verification Rule
Texas requirements and forms can change. Recheck the current Texas Education Code Chapter 132, TWC Chapter 807, and current Career Schools and Colleges forms immediately before launch/application.
