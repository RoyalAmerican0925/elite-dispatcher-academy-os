# DEAC Readiness Checklist

Research baseline: August 2026. Source of truth for application requirements remains the current DEAC Accreditation Handbook.

## Eligibility Preconditions
- [ ] Institution/program model remains within DEAC scope.
- [ ] Each offered program is predominantly distance or correspondence education (51% or more).
- [ ] Applicable state educational authorization, license, exemption or approval is documented.
- [ ] Current programs have enrolled students for at least 12 consecutive months before initial application.
- [ ] Institution has operated under current legal status, ownership and control for two consecutive years before initial application.
- [ ] Comparative audited or reviewed financial statements demonstrate financial soundness.
- [ ] Institutional name and public identity do not create damaging or misleading associations.
- [ ] Owners, board members, officials and administrators can demonstrate integrity and ethical conduct.
- [ ] Institution is prepared for DEAC background-check requirements applicable to owners/officers/managers.

## Standards Evidence System
Maintain evidence mapped to the current DEAC standards, including at minimum:
- institutional mission;
- institutional effectiveness and strategic planning;
- program outcomes, curricula and instructional materials;
- educational and student support services;
- student achievement and satisfaction;
- academic leadership and faculty qualifications;
- advertising, promotional literature and recruitment controls;
- admissions/enrollment and consumer information;
- financial responsibility/capacity;
- tuition/refund and business practices;
- facilities, equipment, technology and distance-learning infrastructure;
- records, policies, compliance and other requirements in the current handbook.

## Course-Level Evidence
- [ ] Published program outcomes.
- [ ] Module/course learning objectives mapped to program outcomes.
- [ ] Documented instructional hours/workload.
- [ ] Assessment blueprint and passing standards.
- [ ] Practical/capstone evaluation criteria.
- [ ] Version-controlled instructional materials.
- [ ] Source/provenance controls for regulatory content.
- [ ] Scheduled curriculum review and revision process.

## Operational Evidence
- [ ] Enrollment records.
- [ ] Student progress/engagement records.
- [ ] Completion and withdrawal data.
- [ ] Assessment and achievement data.
- [ ] Student satisfaction data.
- [ ] Complaint/grievance log and resolutions.
- [ ] Faculty qualification/evaluation files.
- [ ] Student support records.
- [ ] Advertising approvals and archived claims.
- [ ] Financial statements and refund records.
- [ ] Strategic-plan reviews and corrective-action evidence.

## Application Preparation
- [ ] Re-download the current DEAC Handbook immediately before gap assessment.
- [ ] Complete an evidence-by-standard crosswalk.
- [ ] Resolve all material gaps before initial application.
- [ ] Budget current DEAC fees and evaluation costs.
- [ ] Prepare institutional self-evaluation and required exhibits.
- [ ] Prepare staff, records and systems for expert review/evaluation.

No checklist item is proof of accreditation; DEAC alone determines compliance and accreditation status.
