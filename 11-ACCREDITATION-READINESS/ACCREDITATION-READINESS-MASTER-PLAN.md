# Accreditation Readiness Master Plan

## Objective
Design and operate the academy so evidence required by a legitimate institutional accreditor is created during normal operations instead of reconstructed years later.

## Candidate Routes
### DEAC
Best-aligned candidate if the institution remains predominantly distance education. Current DEAC eligibility includes: each program predominantly distance/correspondence education (51%+); applicable state authorization/licensure/exemption; 12 consecutive months enrolling students in current programs before initial application; two consecutive years under current legal status, ownership and control; evidence of financial soundness through comparative audited or reviewed financial statements; institutional integrity; and possible background checks of owners/officers/managers.

### ACCET
Potential candidate for a vocational/continuing-education institution, including interactive distance learning. Current eligibility includes applicable state licensing; interactive educational methodology appropriate to outcomes; two consecutive years of successful educational operation immediately before application; at least one graduating class from the longest program; generally two years under the same ownership/control; defined clock-hour and, where applicable, credit-hour/CEU requirements; and compliance with ACCET standards and ethics.

## Build-Now Workstreams
1. Governance and institutional mission: legal entity, ownership/control records, governing responsibilities, mission, strategic plan, annual review process.
2. State authorization: determine Texas Career Schools and Colleges status and secure approval or documented exemption before relying on accreditation eligibility.
3. Curriculum: measurable outcomes, module objectives, clock-hour methodology, curriculum maps, instructional materials, practical exercises, assessments, capstone and revision history.
4. Student achievement: enrollment, attendance/engagement, completion, assessment performance, withdrawals, satisfaction, complaints, employment/outcome measures where claimed, and improvement actions.
5. Faculty/instruction: qualification standards, resumes/credentials, onboarding, evaluation, professional development and supervision records.
6. Student services: admissions, orientation, technical support, academic support, accessibility process, identity/authentication where required, complaint/grievance process, conduct, dismissal and reenrollment rules.
7. Consumer protection: truthful advertising, published tuition/fees, refund/cancellation rules, enrollment agreement, catalog, credential wording, disclosure controls and marketing review.
8. Financial capacity: separate accounting, budgets, financial statements, tuition/refund controls, cash-flow planning and records suitable for independent review/audit when required.
9. Technology and distance education: LMS controls, student access, interaction model, assessment security, records retention, privacy/security, uptime/support and change management.
10. Institutional effectiveness: defined KPIs, scheduled reviews, evidence-based corrective actions, annual effectiveness report and documented strategic planning.
11. Evidence preservation: every policy, approval, curriculum version, meeting record, outcome report, complaint resolution, faculty record and financial/control document receives a stable evidence ID and retention location.

## Accreditation Gate
Do not advertise the academy as accredited until an accreditor has formally granted accreditation. Before choosing an accreditor or paying application fees, perform a fresh eligibility review against the current official handbook and confirm state authorization.

## Recommended Sequence
Build accreditation-ready controls now; resolve Texas authorization; launch only when legally permitted; preserve operating evidence from day one; complete graduating cohorts and required operating history; conduct a formal gap assessment; select the accreditor whose scope matches the institution at that time; complete self-evaluation; apply; prepare for document review/site evaluation; remediate findings; await formal commission action.

## Key Timing Reality
The accreditation clock is institutional, not merely curricular. A completed course alone does not satisfy operating-history requirements.
