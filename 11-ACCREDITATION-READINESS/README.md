# Accreditation Readiness

Purpose: build the Elite Dispatcher Academy from the beginning so that institutional records, curriculum controls, student services, outcomes, finances, advertising, staffing, and compliance evidence can support a future legitimate accreditation application.

This folder is planning and evidence infrastructure. It does not claim that the academy is currently accredited or eligible to apply.

Primary candidate institutional accreditors researched: Distance Education Accrediting Commission (DEAC) and Accrediting Council for Continuing Education & Training (ACCET). State authorization/licensing must be resolved separately and before accreditation eligibility where applicable.

Files in this folder:
- ACCREDITATION-READINESS-MASTER-PLAN.md
- DEAC-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md
- ACCET-READINESS-CHECKLIST.md
- TEXAS-STATE-AUTHORIZATION.md
- EVIDENCE-REGISTER.md
- SOURCE-REGISTER.md

Research baseline: August 2026. Requirements must be reverified against current official standards before any filing or application.
