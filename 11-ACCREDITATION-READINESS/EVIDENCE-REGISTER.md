# Accreditation Evidence Register

Use this register from the first day of operations. Add one row/record in the eventual structured register for every material piece of evidence.

## Evidence Fields
Evidence ID; accreditation standard/control; document/record name; description; owner; effective date; review date; version; storage location; student cohort/program affected; confidentiality level; retention requirement; verification status; superseded-by link.

## Evidence Families
### Governance
Mission, legal entity, ownership/control, organizational chart, governing decisions, strategic plans, annual reviews.

### State Authorization
Applications, approvals, exemptions, correspondence, program approvals and renewals.

### Curriculum
Program outcomes, module objectives, syllabi, manuals, assessment maps, practical exercises, capstones, revision logs, source records.

### Students
Enrollment agreements, admissions records, progress, attendance/engagement, assessments, completion, withdrawals, certificates, complaints, support and satisfaction.

### Instructional Staff
Qualifications, resumes, credentials, role descriptions, onboarding, evaluations, professional development.

### Institutional Effectiveness
KPIs, completion/achievement data, satisfaction analyses, outcome reports, corrective actions, annual effectiveness reports.

### Financial
Budgets, financial statements, bank/accounting records, tuition receipts, refunds, accounts receivable, independent reviews/audits.

### Consumer Protection
Catalog versions, tuition schedules, enrollment forms, refund/cancellation policies, advertisements, landing pages, claims approvals and complaint resolutions.

### Technology
LMS configuration, access/security controls, privacy practices, support logs, assessment security, backups, incident/change records.

## Evidence Rule
Do not backfill evidence from memory if an original record should exist. Mark missing evidence as a gap and remediate prospectively.
