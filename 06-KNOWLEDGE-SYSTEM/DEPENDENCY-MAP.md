# Dependency Map

## Purpose
Track which concepts and assets must exist before later modules, assessments, simulations, and software can function correctly.

## Core Instructional Dependencies

Module 01 provides dispatcher role, workflow, ethics, communication, and course framework foundations used by all later modules.

Module 02 provides legal-role boundaries, authority distinctions, compliance awareness, and risk concepts used by Modules 04 through 15.

Module 03 provides equipment, freight, operating vocabulary, and trucking-operation foundations used by load search, planning, rate analysis, and exception handling.

Module 04 carrier/driver onboarding feeds Modules 05 through 15 because later dispatch decisions depend on knowing carrier, driver, equipment, authority, insurance, operational restrictions, and client requirements.

Module 05 load boards and market/lane research feeds Modules 06 through 10 and supports simulations involving load selection and market comparison.

Module 06 verification, risk screening, and fraud awareness feeds Modules 07 through 15 and all booking, negotiation, fraud, and dispatch-simulation work.

Module 07 rate math and profitability feeds Modules 08, 09, 10, 13, 14, 15 and any load-selection or negotiation simulator.

Module 08 negotiation feeds Module 09 booking/documentation and advanced simulation scenarios.

Module 09 booking and documentation feeds Modules 10 through 14 because trip execution depends on confirmed load terms and documentation.

Module 10 trip planning feeds Modules 11 through 13 and all dispatch-execution simulations.

Module 11 HOS/ELD awareness constrains Modules 10, 12, 13 and all route/time simulations.

Module 12 active load monitoring feeds Module 13 exception management and Module 14 closeout.

Module 13 exception management feeds Module 14 closeout and Module 15 capstone operations.

Module 14 billing/closeout feeds Module 15 business operations and capstone practice.

Module 15 integrates prior modules into end-to-end dispatcher-business and capstone performance.

## System Dependencies

Assessments depend on approved learning objectives and final module content.

Flashcards depend on the terminology ledger and module vocabulary.

Worksheets and SOPs depend on the operational workflow defined in curriculum.

Simulations depend on terminology, approved rules, scoring logic, realistic constraints, and required upstream module knowledge.

EAOS depends on stable content IDs, statuses, metadata, dependency records, assessment structures, and publication rules.

TKE depends on normalized terminology, fact/source records, regulatory-update tracking, dependency relationships, and reusable data models.

## Control Rule
Do not publish or mark a downstream asset VERIFIED if a required upstream dependency is unresolved, contradictory, or still materially changing.
