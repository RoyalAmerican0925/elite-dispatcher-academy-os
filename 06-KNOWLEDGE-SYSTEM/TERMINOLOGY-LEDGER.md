# Terminology Ledger

## Purpose
Create one controlled vocabulary for the academy so curriculum, assessments, simulations, software, and AI production use the same meanings.

## Entry Standard
Each controlled term should eventually include: term, approved definition, synonyms/aliases, disallowed or misleading uses, owning module/system, source basis if regulated or technical, date-sensitive flag, and notes.

## Initial Controlled Terms

### Dispatcher
A person or service coordinating freight movement on behalf of a motor carrier within the authority and contractual boundaries actually granted to that dispatcher. The course must not blur dispatcher activity with licensed property brokerage.

### Motor Carrier
An entity operating commercial motor vehicles under its own operating authority or other lawful operating arrangement, as applicable.

### Broker
A person or entity arranging transportation of property by authorized motor carriers for compensation under applicable federal brokerage requirements.

### Shipper
The party tendering freight for transportation.

### Consignee / Receiver
The party or facility receiving freight at destination.

### Rate Confirmation
A commercial document confirming agreed load terms between parties. It does not replace regulatory authority, insurance, or a broader contract.

### RPM
Rate per mile. The project must specify the calculation basis whenever used, including whether miles are loaded-only or total trip miles.

### Deadhead
Non-revenue miles traveled to reposition equipment, reach pickup, or move between revenue loads.

### Detention
Compensation or charge associated with excessive waiting time, subject to the actual agreement and load terms.

### TONU
Truck Ordered Not Used. A payment or fee that may apply when a truck is ordered for a load and the load is cancelled or otherwise not used, depending on the agreement.

### POD
Proof of Delivery. Documentation showing freight was delivered, typically subject to carrier, broker, shipper, factoring, or customer requirements.

### HOS
Hours of Service. Federal operating-time rules applicable to covered commercial drivers; dispatcher training must treat HOS as a planning constraint and compliance awareness topic, not as permission to direct unlawful operation.

### ELD
Electronic Logging Device. A system used by covered drivers to record required duty-status information under applicable rules.

## Control Rule
New terms should be added here before they are given specialized academy meanings in multiple modules. If a term has more than one legitimate industry meaning, the course must state which meaning applies in that context.
