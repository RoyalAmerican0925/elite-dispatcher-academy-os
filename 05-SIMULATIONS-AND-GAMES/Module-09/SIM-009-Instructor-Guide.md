## Facilitation notes

## Expected reasoning by stage

- **Stage 1–2:** Students should immediately recognize that an M08 agreement-in-principle is not a booking, and that detention — never discussed — is UNRESOLVED, not silently AGREED just because a deal was reached on everything else.
- **Stage 3–7:** Watch for students who let the matching $2,450 rate "average out" the two material mismatches (pickup time, driver assist). A common failure pattern is treating a same-day match ("both say Tuesday") as sufficient — the six-hour time difference is the actual material fact.
- **Stage 8:** This is the highest-value teaching moment. Students who accept RC v2 as fully resolved because the two originally-flagged problems are fixed have missed that the document introduced a *new* problem (the $2,400 rate). Flag this as anchoring on the original watch-list rather than re-reconciling the whole document.
- **Stage 9:** The Authority Matrix draws no exception for small dollar amounts, who introduced a change, or personal judgment. A student who signs "because it's only $50" is exhibiting the exact CF-01 pattern regardless of how reasonable it sounds.
- **Stage 10:** The document-fabrication trap (editing the PDF to show $2,450) is designed to feel harmless because the edited number happens to be "right." Reinforce: the document must reflect what was actually issued; a private backup copy of the original does not make altering the working file acceptable.
- **Stage 11–12:** A superseded document sharing a reference number, or a rushed colleague's convenience, are not justifications for using it. Similarly, matching commercial terms never substitutes for a still-pending required control (internal sign-off, owner authorization) at final closeout.

## Common misconceptions and diagnostics

| Misconception | Where it surfaces | Diagnostic question |
|---|---|---|
| "A negotiated deal is already a booking" | Stage 1 | "What has actually been verified in writing yet?" |
| "The important terms matching is enough" | Stage 7, 8 | "Did you re-check every field, or just the ones you were already watching?" |
| "A small/reasonable-sounding deviation doesn't need authorization" | Stage 9 | "Does the Authority Matrix actually carve out an exception for size or reasonableness?" |
| "Editing a document to show the 'right' number isn't really fabrication" | Stage 10 | "Does the edited document reflect what was actually issued?" |
| "A shared reference number means the document version doesn't matter" | Stage 11 | "Which version is actually current and authorized?" |
| "If the commercial terms match, it's basically booked" | Stage 12 | "Is every required control actually satisfied, or just the ones you can see easily?" |

## Critical-failure remediation and reassessment

A critical-failure trigger (M09-CF-01 through M09-CF-06) is preserved permanently — the exact triggering response, decision, and timestamp — and is never cleared by a later correct answer on the same decision screen. Students may continue through the rest of the main scenario after a trigger; resolution requires a separate reassessment scenario using **materially different facts** (different load, different numbers, different clause — see the Scenario and Evidence Packet). An incorrect reassessment attempt is recorded but does not resolve the family; only a correct reassessment response changes status to **`RESOLVED BY REASSESSMENT`**. This never rewrites history — the original trigger record remains visible even after resolution.

## Reassessment answer key (instructor use only — do not share with students)

- **M09-CF-01** (Load REF-334, damage deposit): **B** — escalate before accepting anything.
- **M09-CF-02** (Load REF-561, layover clause): **B** — classify as unverified/unauthorized; do not treat as fully accepted.
- **M09-CF-03** (Load REF-902, equipment mismatch): **B** — cannot represent as agreed while unresolved.
- **M09-CF-04** (Load REF-118, superseded v1 grabbed by mistake): **B** — stop it; only current v2 belongs in the packet.
- **M09-CF-05** (Load REF-763, backdating a timestamp): **B** — refuse; backdating is fabrication regardless of content accuracy.
- **M09-CF-06** (Load REF-449, pending internal sign-off): **B** — not yet BOOKED; a required control remains outstanding.
