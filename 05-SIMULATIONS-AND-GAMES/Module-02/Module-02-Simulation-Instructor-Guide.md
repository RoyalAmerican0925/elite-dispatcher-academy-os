# SIM-002 — Module 02 Agency Boundary Desk — Instructor Guide

**Status:** BUILD COMPLETE — RELEASE QA DEFERRED  
**Instructor use only.**

## Purpose and Operating Model
SIM-002 tests occupational boundary judgment, not legal-definition recall. Required Academy model:
**STOP → IDENTIFY → TRACE → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE**.
This is an Academy control model, not statutory text.

## Core Competencies
Learner identifies the represented principal, traces freight source/control, separates known from unknown, recognizes discretionary allocation, distinguishes carrier-side coordination from broader arranging activity, distinguishes return from reassignment, rejects false safe harbors, preserves insufficient information, selects a defensible action, documents it, and escalates a specific unresolved issue when qualified review is required.

## Locked Runtime Architecture
Seven stages / 15 decisions / competencies A–H. Final mastery requires 15/15.

## High-Stakes Families
- **M02-CF-01 / D06:** shipper-supplied freight plus dispatcher discretionary carrier allocation.
- **M02-CF-02 / D10:** redirecting a declined carrier-specific opportunity instead of returning it to source.
- **M02-CF-03 / D13:** actual allocation conduct contradicting a claimed agency label.

These are simulation high-stakes controls. They do not falsely claim one-for-one identity with the Practical's critical-failure wording.

## False-Safe-Harbor Guardrails
Never accept carrier count, one-carrier status, non-overlap, compensation method, contract title, tax status, or “not touching freight” as a universal safe harbor. Actual conduct and the full fact pattern control the instructional analysis.

## Premium Critical Remediation
For an ordinary error: identify the failed competency, route to the relevant manual/tool, require corrected reasoning, preserve first attempt, and record final mastery separately.

For a triggered M02-CF family:
1. preserve the original decision, response, timestamp and trigger;
2. allow same-screen correction for final-mastery evidence, but **do not clear the critical family**;
3. present the runtime's materially different alternate fact pattern for that family;
4. require transfer of the controlling principle, not answer-letter repetition;
5. record reassessment response/result/timestamp separately;
6. mark the family resolved only after the alternate reassessment is passed;
7. keep the original trigger visible in the history.

## Targeted Transfer
- CF-01: new shipper-direct overlapping-carrier allocation request.
- CF-02: new carrier-specific broker opportunity declined by the named carrier; learner must return/decline rather than independently redirect.
- CF-03: new contract-label-versus-actual-conduct comparison.

## Instructor Debrief
Ask: What fact controlled the decision? Who supplied the freight? Who would choose the carrier? What is unknown? What evidence verifies it? What action happens now? What would change the classification? What exact question requires qualified review?

## Runtime Evidence
Per decision: immutable first attempt, final response/mastery, timestamps, remediation/attempt count. Per high-stakes family: occurrence, family ID, trigger time, resolution state and alternate reassessment record. Overall: competency status and completion state.

Do not represent browser-local storage as durable LMS/student records.

## Completion Standard
SIM-002 instructional completion requires all 15 decisions at final mastery, no unresolved M02-CF family, no unresolved false-safe-harbor reasoning, and recorded remediation/reassessment evidence where used.

## Source / Legal Control
Keep simulation language within Module 02's source hierarchy: statute/regulation, FMCSA interpretive guidance, and Academy control models must remain distinct. When a real-world fact pattern cannot be resolved from verified facts, preserve uncertainty and route for qualified regulatory/legal review rather than improvising.
