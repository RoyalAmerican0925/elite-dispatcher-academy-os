# SIM-005 — Module 05 Live Load Search Desk

**Student Simulation**  
**Module:** 05 — Load Boards, Load Sourcing & Freight Opportunity Evaluation  
**Build status:** BUILD QA PENDING — release QA deferred

## Purpose
You are sourcing a load for fictional carrier **Meridian Cross Logistics**. Your job is to build a search profile, screen candidates against it, verify what's actually confirmed versus merely found, and issue a documented shortlist — not to book the first thing that looks close enough.

Use:

**PROFILE → SEARCH → SCREEN → VERIFY → COMPARE → PROTECT → DECIDE → DOCUMENT**

## What you will do
Across thirteen stages you will:
- build a search profile from the carrier/driver/equipment file and set search parameters;
- classify a phone-confirmed candidate correctly (GOOD CANDIDATE, not just FOUND);
- distinguish FOUND from VERIFIED AVAILABLE on a stale posting;
- turn an incomplete posting's gaps into specific verification questions;
- reject a candidate for a stated endorsement conflict;
- reject a candidate for a documented carrier-policy conflict;
- respond to duplicate-looking postings without declaring proven fraud;
- verify a new individual contact even though the brokerage entity is already confirmed;
- catch a material rate-confirmation discrepancy before signing;
- correctly locate signing authority rather than assuming it from document review;
- verify timing/HOS feasibility instead of calculating it from distance alone;
- classify a factor decline as a private risk signal, not proof of fraud or lost authority;
- recognize and escalate a shipper-first discretionary-allocation request;
- re-evaluate an already-confirmed candidate when new material information arrives, and issue a final documented disposition.

## Critical decisions
Six concepts require special review if repeatedly missed:
1. Do not treat a missing required endorsement as curable after booking.
2. Do not declare double brokering proven from duplicate postings alone.
3. Do not let a verified brokerage entity authenticate an unverified new individual contact.
4. Do not sign past a material rate-confirmation discrepancy.
5. Do not assume signing authority merely from reviewing the document.
6. Do not treat shipper-first discretionary allocation among represented carriers as ordinary sourcing — this is the Module 02 allocation-of-traffic boundary, carried into the sourcing context.
