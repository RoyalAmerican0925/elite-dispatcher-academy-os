# SIM-006 — Broker Verification & Fraud Command Desk — Student Instructions

**Module:** 06 — Broker Verification & Fraud Prevention  
**Role:** Dispatcher-side verification desk  
**Case:** progressive Kestrel Logistics Group transaction

## Employment task
Work an initially plausible broker/load opportunity through incomplete, conflicting, and changing evidence. The objective is not to be suspicious of everything. The objective is to make a defensible operational decision while preserving an evidence trail.

Use the Module 06 execution model:

**RECEIVE → ISOLATE CLAIMS → VERIFY ENTITY → AUTHENTICATE CONTACT → CROSS-CHECK LOAD/DOCUMENTS → VERIFY PAYMENT PATH → RECORD EVIDENCE → CLASSIFY RISK → PROCEED / VERIFY / HOLD / ESCALATE / DECLINE**

## Operating rules
- Keep **ENTITY, CONTACT, LOAD/DOCUMENT, and PAYMENT PATH** separate.
- A verified company record does not authenticate the person contacting you.
- Do not use a phone number/link supplied only by the questioned party as the sole source to verify that same party.
- A red flag is not automatically proof of fraud.
- Do not silently correct mismatched documents.
- Unexpected payment/remittance changes are HOLD/VERIFY events until independently authenticated.
- Urgency does not lower the verification standard.
- Record what evidence proves **and what it does not prove**.
- Use factual, non-accusatory language unless evidence supports more.

## Ten-stage case
1. Intake and claim isolation.
2. Entity verification.
3. Contact authentication.
4. Load/document cross-check.
5. Financial-security/current-status interpretation, including an inactive-authority mini-case.
6. Duplicate/re-brokering ambiguity.
7. Payment-path attack.
8. Urgency/social-engineering pressure.
9. Later evidence that changes case state.
10. Final disposition and escalation packet.

## Competency evidence
The runtime retains first-attempt evidence. Ordinary errors may be remediated and retried. A Module 06 **critical failure** is preserved and triggers instructor review; choosing the correct answer later on the same screen does not erase it. Critical remediation/reassessment uses a materially different fictional fact pattern.

At closeout, you must be able to state what is verified, unverified, conflicting/contradicted, what action is stopped, the current disposition, the evidence supporting it, and the escalation owner/next action.
