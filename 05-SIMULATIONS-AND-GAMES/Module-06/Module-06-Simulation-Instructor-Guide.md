# SIM-006 — Instructor Facilitation & Remediation Guide

**Simulation:** Broker Verification & Fraud Command Desk  
**Authority:** Module 06 practical/scoring guide + Module 06 Instructor Guide  
**Critical standard:** six controlling families; no invented seventh family

## Facilitation objective
Observe whether the learner can make evidence-bounded dispatcher decisions under uncertainty. Do not reward maximum suspicion. Reward **verification discipline**.

Release one stage at a time. Do not reveal the later Kestrel compliance findings early.

## Stage controls
### 1 — Intake
Require fact/claim/unknown separation. A posting field is not verified merely because it is specific.

### 2 — Entity
Require the learner to state both what entity/authority/financial-responsibility evidence proves and what it does not prove.

**CF2:** treating active/real entity status as proof of contact authorization.

### 3 — Contact
Require independently sourced trusted callback. Questioned contact data cannot authenticate itself.

**CF3:** proceeding through confirmed contact contradiction.

### 4 — Documents
Require field-by-field comparison. Do not award points for falsely flagging fields that actually match.

**CF6:** bypassing a material unresolved identity/document/payment mismatch.

### 5 — Financial/current status
Require limited-evidence interpretation:
- BMC status ≠ contact authentication;
- factor approval ≠ contact authentication or federal finding;
- 7-calendar-day, 7-business-day and 30-day contexts remain distinct.

Use Alder Ridge to test inactive-authority judgment.

**CF4:** knowingly proceeding while required authority is verified inactive.

### 6 — Duplicate ambiguity
Require verification questions about relationship/authorization rather than a fraud declaration.

**CF5:** declaring fraud/double brokering solely from inconclusive duplicate/similar-posting evidence.

### 7 — Payment path
Require HOLD, preservation, independent trusted verification and escalation when contradicted/unresolved.

**CF1:** acting on unverified/contradicted payment redirection.

### 8 — Pressure
Timed pressure is deliberate. Do not reward speed that bypasses controls.

### 9 — Evidence-state change
Learner must reclassify contact/document/payment evidence after trusted Kestrel compliance responds while keeping entity legitimacy separate.

### 10 — Final disposition/handoff
Require a factual closeout and reconstructable escalation packet.

## Six critical failures
Any occurrence triggers instructor review and prevents clean SIM-006 competency clearance:
1. acts on unverified/contradicted payment redirection;
2. treats active/real entity as proof unverified contact is authorized;
3. proceeds through confirmed impersonation/contact contradiction;
4. knowingly proceeds where required broker authority is verified inactive;
5. declares fraud/double brokering solely from ambiguous/inconclusive indicators;
6. bypasses a material unresolved identity/document/payment mismatch.

## Critical remediation
Do **not** erase a critical failure by allowing a corrected click on the same screen after remediation. Preserve the initial failure.

Then:
1. identify the failed mental model;
2. reteach only the failed control;
3. require the applicable Module 06 tool;
4. reassess on a **materially different fictional fact pattern**;
5. record original failure and retest result separately.

## Diagnostic remediation matrix
| Failure | Failed model | Required tool/retest |
|---|---|---|
| Entity treated as contact proof | Real company = real caller | Entity-vs-Contact Worksheet + alternate callback case |
| Questioned channel verifies itself | Any callback = verification | Trusted Callback Record |
| Document mismatch bypassed | Looks close enough | Load & Document Cross-Check Sheet |
| BMC/factor evidence overstated | Approval proves whole deal | Broker Verification Evidence Record |
| Duplicate posting called fraud | Red flag = proof | Risk Classification & Decision Record |
| Payment change acted upon | Same thread = same person | Payment-Instruction Change Verification Record |
| Pressure bypass | Losing load > verification | Timed HOLD/VERIFY alternate case |
| Weak escalation | “Ask somebody” is enough | Verification Evidence & Escalation Packet |

## Scoring boundary
SIM-006 produces competency evidence but does not replace the 30-point Module 06 Practical. The practical remains **24/30 plus no critical failure**.

Browser/device matrix, LMS persistence, manual accessibility review and assembled release QA remain deferred. Do not label SIM-006 `PUBLICATION_READY` based on the content/runtime gate.
