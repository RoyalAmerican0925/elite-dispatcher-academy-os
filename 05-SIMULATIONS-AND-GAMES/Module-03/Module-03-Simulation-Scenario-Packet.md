### Missing-information investigation
Before deciding that the load fits, identify the missing information that matters, including as applicable:
- commodity;
- actual equipment/tare information;
- axle-distribution implications;
- freight dimensions;
- loading method;
- temperature or special handling requirements;
- driver qualification/specialized requirements;
- route/jurisdiction information where relevant.

The correct professional behavior is not to convert missing information into assumed facts.

# Decision Discipline

Use this sequence throughout SIM-003:

1. **CHECK EQUIPMENT** — what equipment/driver facts are actually known?
2. **CHECK COMMODITY** — what is moving and does the equipment make sense for it?
3. **CHECK TEMPERATURE** — is active temperature control required?
4. **CHECK DIMENSIONS** — do dimensions create fit or specialized-movement questions?
5. **CHECK WEIGHT/AXLES** — distinguish cargo/gross/axle questions; do not rely on one number.
6. **CHECK DRIVER QUALIFICATIONS** — does the stated load require a qualification the assigned driver lacks?
7. **CHECK MISSING INFORMATION** — what has not been established?
8. **VERIFY** — identify the correct source/person/document for unresolved material facts.
9. **DECIDE** — PROCEED / REJECT / ESCALATE / VERIFY BEFORE DECIDING.

## STOP Rule

When a material fact needed to establish safe, operational or lawful fit is unresolved:

**STOP → IDENTIFY THE GAP → VERIFY → DO NOT ASSUME → DOCUMENT THE NEXT ACTION**

This rule is intended to build dispatch judgment, not merely test memorization.
