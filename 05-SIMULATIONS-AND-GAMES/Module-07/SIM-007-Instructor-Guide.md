# SIM-007 — Rate & Profitability Decision Desk
## Instructor Guide

**Module:** 07 — Rate Mathematics & Profitability

## Instructional intent
SIM-007 tests whether the learner can move beyond rate-per-mile slogans and perform a disciplined carrier economics review under incomplete and changing information. The learner should be rewarded for correct calculations, honest labels, verified inputs, appropriate HOLD decisions, recalculation when evidence changes and a carrier-facing recommendation that preserves carrier authority.

## Facilitation rules
1. Release evidence progressively by stage.
2. Do not disclose answer keys or later-stage numbers early.
3. Preserve first-attempt evidence before coaching.
4. Explain the principle behind an error rather than simply revealing the correct option.
5. Keep all case rates, fuel prices and percentages labeled fictional instructional assumptions.
6. Do not convert a material unknown into zero.
7. Do not call a partial-cost contribution margin “profit.”
8. Keep possible accessorial revenue outside current revenue until triggered and documented.
9. Keep positioning separate from guaranteed future revenue.
10. Preserve carrier final authority in every briefing.

## Stage guide
### Stage 1 — Carrier economic profile
Evaluate whether the learner can classify usable, unknown and irrelevant inputs. Pay special attention to the difference between a confirmed zero and an unknown.

### Stage 2 — Competing loads
The learner should resist ranking loads from headline revenue or loaded RPM alone. Delta intentionally requires HOLD FOR DATA.

### Stage 3 — Mileage normalization
Require total trip miles and effective RPM. The headline ranking should reverse once deadhead/repositioning is included.

### Stage 4 — Fuel and trip costs
Fuel must be based on total trip miles and the supplied truck MPG. Trip-specific tolls/fees must be included only when documented in the case.

### Stage 5 — Margin labeling
The learner must use the phrase or concept **estimated contribution margin under stated included-cost assumptions**. If only selected costs are included, the result is not net/accounting profit.

### Stage 6 — Accessorial uncertainty
A written clause is not earned revenue. The learner should retain the clause in the record while excluding untriggered/unverified revenue from the current calculation.

### Stage 7 — Payment/cash flow
Quick-pay is a cash-timing option with a cost. Faster payment does not create higher profitability.

### Stage 8 — Positioning
Positioning is a legitimate planning factor but not guaranteed revenue and not a number to add into current margin.

### Stage 9 — Changed evidence / counter threshold
The verified fee changes from 5% to 7%. Every affected comparison must be recalculated on the corrected basis. Then apply the carrier’s stated effective-RPM criteria and derive Alpha’s counter threshold from total trip miles.

### Stage 10 — Final carrier briefing
Require calculations, assumptions, unknowns, excluded costs, earned vs possible revenue, positioning, recommended disposition and explicit carrier final authority.

## Five critical-failure families
Exactly five families control SIM-007:
- **CF-01 Unsupported revenue**
- **CF-02 Profit mislabeling**
- **CF-03 Loaded-mile blindness**
- **CF-04 Fabricated input**
- **CF-05 Carrier-authority override**

CF-01 and CF-02 inherit the current M07 assessment chain. CF-03–CF-05 are simulation-specific occupational controls. Do not falsely describe all five as inherited from the existing Practical Scoring Guide.

## Critical remediation protocol
A critical failure is historical evidence and may not be erased by clicking the original question again.

When triggered:
1. preserve the triggering response, family and timestamp;
2. allow the simulation to continue so additional competencies can still be observed;
3. explain the occupational principle that failed;
4. do not relabel the family as “no failure observed” after same-screen correction;
5. after the main case, administer the approved materially different reassessment;
6. record reassessment evidence separately;
7. mark the family **RESOLVED BY REASSESSMENT** only after successful performance on the different fact pattern.

## Approved reassessments
- CF-01: new layover trigger/documentation case.
- CF-02: new $2,400 partial-cost margin labeling case.
- CF-03: new Red/Blue/Green effective-RPM ranking case.
- CF-04: new missing-origin-deadhead case.
- CF-05: new Load X/Load Y carrier-authority briefing case.
