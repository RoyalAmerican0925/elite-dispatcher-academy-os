# SIM-004 — Module 04 Carrier Onboarding Command Desk

**Student Simulation**  
**Module:** 04 — Carrier & Driver Onboarding  
**Build status:** BUILD QA PENDING — release QA deferred

## Purpose
You are onboarding fictional carrier **Lone Star Ridge Transport**. Your job is to decide whether the dispatch desk is operationally ready — not merely whether a folder contains documents.

Use:

**COLLECT → CLASSIFY → VERIFY → RECONCILE → PROTECT → DECIDE → DOCUMENT → TRACK**

## What you will do
Across ten stages you will:
- review an intake packet;
- separate authority verification from COI evidence;
- reconcile a W-9/entity-name difference without unsupported accusations;
- distinguish a driver dispatch profile from the carrier's DQ/compliance program;
- evaluate a stated endorsement conflict;
- handle a current medical-certification event using the then-current procedure;
- protect an existing payment path from an unverified redirect request;
- respond to a request to take over compliance functions outside ordinary dispatch scope;
- classify expiration/reverification triggers and carrier policy correctly;
- issue a final onboarding disposition that preserves unresolved controls.

## Critical decisions
Three concepts require special review if repeatedly missed:
1. Do not dispatch through a stated required driver-qualification/endorsement conflict.
2. Do not change payment instructions from an inbound request alone.
3. Do not silently assume authority to administer the carrier's DQ/drug-alcohol compliance program merely because you maintain operational driver information.

## Current-date event
One stage contains **CURRENT-DATE TRAINING MATERIAL** about the 2026 NRII/paper-MEC transition. Follow the procedure shown in the runtime only for the controlled training date. The Academy must reverify that event before use after October 11, 2026 or if FMCSA procedure changes earlier.

The enduring lesson is: verify the current procedure, certificate/driver match and carrier process; do not turn the dispatcher into the final medical-qualification authority.
