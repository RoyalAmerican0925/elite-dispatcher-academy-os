# SIM-004 — Scoring & Mastery Rubric

**Instructor use only**

## Mastery model
- FIRST-ATTEMPT PERFORMANCE: X / 20
- FINAL MASTERY: 20 / 20 required for COMPLETE
- REMEDIATION COUNT: decisions retried
- COMPETENCY STATUS: Mastered / Mastered After Remediation / Needs Instructor Review / In Progress

A lower first-attempt score does not automatically fail a learner who successfully remediates ordinary errors. First-attempt evidence must remain visible.

## Critical escalation
Exactly three decisions carry repeated-error escalation:
- SIM04-D09 — stated endorsement/qualification conflict;
- SIM04-D13 — unverified payment redirect;
- SIM04-D15 — automatic compliance-program scope takeover.

After two incorrect attempts, runtime flags instructor review. No additional critical failures should be created during administration.

## Completion
COMPLETE requires:
1. 20/20 final mastery; and
2. no unresolved instructor-review state.

## Current-date control
SIM04-D11 is not a permanent rule-memory item. It must be current-source checked before use after October 11, 2026 or earlier if FMCSA procedure changes.

## Interpretation
Look for the Module 04 reasoning pattern:

**COLLECT → CLASSIFY → VERIFY → RECONCILE → PROTECT → DECIDE → DOCUMENT → TRACK**

The learner must explain why an item is verified, unresolved, stopped, out of scope or ready rather than merely selecting a label.
