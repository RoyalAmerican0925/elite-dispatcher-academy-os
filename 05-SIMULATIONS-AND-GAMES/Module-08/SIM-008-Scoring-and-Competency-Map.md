# SIM-008 — Broker Negotiation Lab
## Scoring & Competency Map

## Commercial scoring rule

The runtime contains **24 meaningful decisions** across 12 stages. First-attempt performance is converted to the existing Module 08 practical scale:

**Scaled Practical Score = round((First-Attempt Correct ÷ 24) × 30)**

Commercial simulation completion requires:
1. **24/30 or higher** scaled first-attempt score;
2. final mastery across all required simulation evidence, including approved reassessment equivalents when a critical decision was failed;
3. **zero unresolved critical failures**.

A learner may improve through remediation, but the first-attempt score remains visible. Critical failures are not cleared by same-screen correction.

## Critical history rule

When a critical trigger occurs, record:
- family;
- triggering decision;
- first triggering response;
- timestamp;
- whether the original screen was later corrected;
- reassessment scenario ID;
- reassessment first response and timestamp;
- final reassessment result.

A later correct response on the original screen may improve final-response accuracy but **cannot change the critical family to NO FAILURE OBSERVED**. After a successful materially different reassessment, the family becomes **RESOLVED BY REASSESSMENT**.

## Competency status vocabulary

- **In Progress** — not enough evidence yet.
- **Mastered** — required evidence correct without remediation.
- **Mastered After Remediation** — ordinary error corrected.
- **Needs Reassessment** — critical failure occurred and remains unresolved.
- **Mastered After Reassessment** — materially different critical reassessment passed.
- **Needs Instructor Review** — scenario is complete but commercial score/final mastery gate is not fully satisfied.

## Boundary rule

No successful Module 08 outcome may be labeled `BOOKED`. Approved successful language is **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL** until Module 09 formal rate-confirmation review is completed.
