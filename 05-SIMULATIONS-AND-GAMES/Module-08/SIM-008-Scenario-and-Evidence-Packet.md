# SIM-008 — Broker Negotiation Lab
## Scenario & Evidence Packet

**Instructional status:** FICTIONAL CASE DATA — NOT CURRENT MARKET GUIDANCE

This packet defines the durable scenario truth behind the SIM-008 browser runtime. Evidence must be released only when its stage becomes available. Instructors and developers must not expose later-stage facts early.

## Carrier profile

**Carrier:** Crestline Freight Solutions  
**Equipment:** verified 53-foot dry van  
**Current truck position:** Chicago, Illinois  
**Supplied Module 07 economics:** target $2,650 all-in; floor/minimum $2,300 all-in; anything below $2,300 requires carrier authorization  
**Authority rule:** dispatcher may negotiate within supplied parameters but may not invent authority over driver conditions or carrier commitments.

## Broker/load setup

**Broker:** Apex Transit Brokerage — fictional  
**Opening rate:** $2,050  
**Initial file gap:** live unload versus drop-and-hook is not stated.  
**Noise in the file:** an unrelated historical lane rate and an assumed pattern from prior broker loads. Neither is verified for this specific negotiation.

## Progressive stage evidence

### Stage 1 — Case Intake & Position Build
Available:
- verified equipment and truck position;
- carrier target and floor;
- broker opening rate;
- unrelated old rate and assumed historical pickup pattern as distractors.

Learner must build a position from relevant verified/supplied facts without promoting assumptions to facts.

### Stage 2 — Missing-Fact Verification & Truthful Opening
New evidence:
- unload mode is missing;
- broker asks whether the truck can run reefer freight if the commodity changes;
- truck is verified dry van only.

The learner must verify the missing operational fact and represent equipment truthfully.

### Stage 3 — Discovery & Broker-Statement Analysis
New evidence:
- broker states: “Everyone is running this lane for $2,100.”
- no independent verification supports the statement.

The learner must distinguish a broker statement from established market truth and ask a purposeful discovery question without accusation or automatic concession.

### Stage 4 — Counter & Truthful Leverage
New evidence:
- internal economics support the $2,650 target;
- no competing offer exists.

A counter may be concise or briefly grounded. The learner is not required to disclose internal economics. Any invented competing offer is fabricated leverage.

### Stage 5 — Conditional Trade & Agreement-Specific Terms
New evidence:
- broker moves to $2,250, still below supplied floor;
- broker raises drop-and-hook as a possible guaranteed condition;
- broker vaguely mentions detention coverage.

The learner should use conditional trade logic and must not treat detention as specific until TRIGGER, AMOUNT/METHOD, EVIDENCE and PROCESS are established.

### Stage 6 — Vague Terms & Floor Discipline
New evidence:
- broker says: “We usually take care of our good carriers on stuff like that.”
- rate reaches $2,300 exactly.

The general assurance remains unresolved. The rate is at the supplied floor, not below it.

### Stage 7 — Material Change & Position Recalibration
New evidence:
- pickup becomes a fixed 6:00 AM appointment;
- carrier confirms that appointment is feasible;
- carrier explicitly reaffirms no willingness to move below $2,300;
- target remains $2,650; floor remains $2,300.

The learner must re-evaluate rather than blindly keep the old logic, while avoiding invented authority.

### Stage 8 — Broker Pressure & Professional Resistance
New evidence:
- broker imposes a two-minute deadline without new load facts;
- later, broker goes silent after a counter.

Pressure and silence are not themselves evidence requiring a concession.

### Stage 9 — Authority Boundary & Escalation
New evidence:
- broker offers $2,275;
- no below-floor authorization exists;
- broker separately asks the dispatcher to promise the driver will accept any unspecified overnight layover if the receiver changes the appointment.

The correct professional control is decline/escalate for the below-floor rate and refuse to commit unspecified operational conditions without authority.

### Stage 10 — Closeout Classification & Negotiation Record
New evidence:
- $2,275 was declined and escalated;
- carrier reauthorized holding at $2,300 and no lower;
- broker later agrees to $2,300;
- 6:00 AM appointment is confirmed;
- detention specifics remain unresolved;
- drop-and-hook was mentioned but never established.

Closeout buckets:
- **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE:** rate $2,300; 6:00 AM appointment.
- **UNRESOLVED:** detention specifics.
- **NOT ESTABLISHED:** drop-and-hook availability.

### Stage 11 — Agreement-in-Principle & Module 09 Handoff
New evidence:
- verbal agreement-in-principle exists at $2,300;
- no written rate confirmation has been received or reviewed.

The state is **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL**, not BOOKED. The handoff must carry agreed, unresolved and unestablished terms to Module 09.

### Stage 12 — Professional Decline & Final Disposition
Separate opportunity:
- delivery appointment is infeasible for the verified driver;
- broker will not move it;
- no authority exists to accept an infeasible load.

Correct disposition is professional decline.

Final primary-load state remains agreement in principle pending Module 09 booking controls.

## Critical reassessment packet

A critical reassessment must use materially different facts from the triggering screen. The six approved reassessment patterns are:

- **CF-01:** new below-floor case using $2,450 floor / $2,425 offer.
- **CF-02:** new fabricated-leverage situation with no competing offer.
- **CF-03:** new equipment representation fact — no liftgate.
- **CF-04:** new unauthorized weekend-layover commitment request.
- **CF-05:** new closeout classification involving rate, unresolved TONU and never-discussed layover.
- **CF-06:** new verbal-rate-agreement case without reviewed written rate confirmation.

Do not reuse the original numbers and facts as the critical reassessment. A student must demonstrate transfer of the competency, not recognition of the same answer.

## Scenario continuity controls

- $2,650 remains the supplied target unless explicitly changed.
- $2,300 remains the supplied floor throughout the primary case.
- $2,275 is below floor and must not become authorized merely because the gap is small.
- The later return to $2,300 occurs only after escalation and carrier reauthorization to hold at the original floor.
- No detention revenue or universal accessorial amount is created by this scenario.
- No verbal agreement is promoted to completed booking inside Module 08.
