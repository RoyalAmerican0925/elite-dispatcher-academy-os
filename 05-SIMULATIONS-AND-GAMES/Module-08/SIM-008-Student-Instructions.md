# SIM-008 — Broker Negotiation Lab
## Student Instructions

**Module:** 08 — Rate Negotiation & Broker Communication  
**Simulation purpose:** demonstrate that you can prepare, conduct, control, document and close a broker rate negotiation without inventing facts, exceeding carrier authority or confusing an agreement in principle with a completed booking.

> All companies, rates, people and load facts in this simulation are fictional instructional records. They are not current market-rate claims.

## What this simulation measures

This is not a script-reading exercise and not a disguised multiple-choice quiz. You will make decisions as evidence changes across **12 progressive stages and 24 occupational decisions**. Later facts can change the meaning of an earlier position. Your job is to react to the evidence actually available at that moment.

The operating sequence is:

**PREPARE → VERIFY FACTS → SET POSITION → OPEN → QUESTION → LISTEN → COUNTER → TRADE → CONFIRM → DOCUMENT → ACCEPT / DECLINE / ESCALATE**

The simulation measures all 15 Module 08 competency families, including position building, discovery, truthful leverage, countering, conditional trade, agreement-specific accessorial clarification, professional resistance, supplied authority, closeout classification, documentation, Module 09 handoff and professional decline.

## Before you start

You are acting as a dispatcher supporting a motor carrier. You do not own the carrier's decision authority. Use only the authority and facts supplied in the case. When a material fact is missing, verify it. Do not silently turn an unknown into a favorable assumption.

The case begins with a supplied target, floor/minimum and broker opening rate. Those figures are different concepts. A target is not automatically a minimum, and a broker's asking/opening rate is not the carrier's floor.

## How evidence works

Evidence is released progressively. Do not assume that information visible later in the simulation was known earlier. The runtime records your first response before remediation. A later correction does not rewrite the first attempt.

Where an agreement-specific term is raised, use the four-part clarification test:

**TRIGGER → AMOUNT/METHOD → EVIDENCE → PROCESS**

A vague statement such as “we usually take care of our good carriers” is not automatically a specific agreed term.

## Critical-failure rules

Six behaviors are controlled as critical failures:

1. accepting below a supplied carrier floor/minimum without required authorization;
2. fabricating leverage or negotiation facts;
3. materially misrepresenting carrier, truck, driver or load facts;
4. committing the carrier outside supplied authority;
5. treating an unresolved material term as agreed;
6. representing agreement in principle as completed booking before Module 09 controls.

If you perform one of those actions, the runtime preserves the critical-failure evidence. Selecting the correct answer afterward on the same screen **does not erase the failure**. You must later demonstrate the same competency in a materially different reassessment scenario. The original evidence remains part of the record.

## Scoring and completion

The simulation preserves two performance views:

- **First-attempt performance** — what you did before feedback/remediation.
- **Final mastery** — whether you ultimately demonstrated the required competencies, including any required critical reassessment.

First-attempt performance is converted to the Module 08 practical scale of **30 points**. The commercial build gate requires:

- scaled first-attempt score of **24/30 or higher**;
- final mastery across the simulation evidence;
- **zero unresolved critical failures**.

A corrected score never deletes the original attempt.

## Negotiation closeout language

Use precise status language. A rate can be **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE** while formal booking is still pending. Module 08 does not authorize you to label a load `BOOKED` merely because the broker and dispatcher verbally agreed on a number.

Module 09 owns formal booking, rate-confirmation review and dispatch-document handoff.

## Runtime record limitation

The browser runtime stores attempt history in local browser storage and can print a competency summary. That is an instructional runtime record, not a durable LMS/student-account record. Production LMS integration, browser/device release QA and manual accessibility QA remain separate release controls.

## Standard expected from you

A strong dispatcher does not win by always getting the highest number or by always refusing risk. Strong performance means making a defensible decision from verified facts, communicating professionally, using truthful leverage, protecting supplied authority, documenting ambiguity and handing the next person an accurate record.
