## Decision language
Choose the least accusatory status justified by the evidence:
- ☐ **CLEAR** — no material unresolved mismatch.
- ☐ **CLEAR WITH DOCUMENTED QUALIFICATION** — minor inconsistency independently resolved and recorded.
- ☐ **VERIFY** — red flag/missing fact requires independent confirmation.
- ☐ **HOLD** — material mismatch unresolved; do not sign, dispatch, reroute, or change payment instructions.
- ☐ **ESCALATE** — issue exceeds dispatcher authority/competency or needs management/compliance/cybersecurity review.
- ☐ **DO NOT USE** — carrier/factor/internal policy prevents use; not automatically a fraud finding.
- ☐ **DO NOT BOOK** — verified facts make the transaction unsafe or impermissible to proceed.
