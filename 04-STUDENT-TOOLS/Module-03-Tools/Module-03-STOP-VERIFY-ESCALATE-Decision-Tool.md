## VERIFY

For the issue type checked above, confirm the specific facts needed:

**Weight/axle:** Total gross weight? Weight on each individual axle/tandem group? Does every group independently meet its federal limit (20,000 lb single / 34,000 lb tandem), regardless of the gross total?

**Dimensions:** Actual usable interior dimensions (not nominal)? Door/opening clearance? Does rigid freight physically fit through the opening, not just inside the space?

**Equipment type:** Does the assigned equipment type actually match what this freight requires (temperature control, loading method, deck configuration)?

**Hazmat:** What does the shipment's actual transportation information show — proper shipping description, UN/NA number, hazard class, packing group, placarding requirement? Does the assigned driver hold the specific endorsement required (H, N, or X — note that X is not satisfied by H alone)?

**Facility:** Does the pickup/delivery location's actual loading method, access, and any credential requirements (e.g., TWIC, washout, food-grade condition) match the equipment and load plan?

**Oversize/overweight/permit:** Which states and routes are involved? Does the load exceed federal/state thresholds? Is a permit required, and can one actually be obtained for this specific route — don't assume yes or no without checking.
