## Decision

Based on the checklist above:

☐ GOOD FIT — proceed with booking
☐ BAD FIT — do not book as described
☐ NEEDS MORE INFORMATION — request missing facts before deciding
☐ SPECIALIZED / ESCALATE — stop and get qualified review before proceeding

**Reminder:** Weight is one line on this eighteen-line checklist, not the whole checklist.
