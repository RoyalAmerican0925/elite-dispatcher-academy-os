## Student Instructions

For each load offer, do not decide first. Capture the information, classify each field, identify what is missing or unclear, and write targeted clarification questions. Use only the facts provided. Do not assume favorable conditions.

## Part F — Error Detection

For each statement, write **Correct** or **Unsafe/Unsupported Assumption** and explain.

1. “It says Houston, so I’ll assume the pickup is downtown.”
2. “The offer says van, so I already know all equipment requirements.”
3. “The broker said $2,100, so I should record $2,100 and ask what that amount includes.”
4. “No special instructions were listed, so there definitely are none.”
5. “The load may still be workable, but I need the pickup time and exact location before deciding.”

## Instructor Answer Guidance

Case 1 should be largely Verified. Mileage must be treated as an estimate/basis, not guaranteed actual mileage. A reasonable follow-up may confirm any carrier-specific or facility-specific condition not supplied in the scenario.

Case 2 must not be marked ready to proceed. Expected missing/unclear fields include exact facilities, pickup timing, delivery timing, commodity, detailed equipment requirement, mileage basis, compensation components, special instructions, appointment method, load/contact identifiers, and other material conditions.

Error detection: 1 unsupported assumption; 2 unsupported assumption; 3 correct controlled response; 4 unsupported assumption; 5 correct.

## Scoring Guide

**Meets Standard:** identifies all major material gaps, makes no unsupported assumptions, asks targeted questions tied to operational consequences, and records information clearly.

**Needs Remediation:** misses multiple material fields, uses vague questions only, or assumes missing facts.

**Critical coaching flag:** learner recommends proceeding while knowingly missing information that materially prevents reliable evaluation.
