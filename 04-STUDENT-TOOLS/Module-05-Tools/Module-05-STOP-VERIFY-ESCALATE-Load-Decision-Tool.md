## What kind of issue is this?

☐ Missing information (a field simply isn't known yet)
☐ Ordinary verification (a normal check hasn't been done yet, no red flag)
☐ Material inconsistency (something doesn't match what was expected/negotiated)
☐ Operational incompatibility (equipment, driver qualification, carrier policy conflict)
☐ Potential fraud/security concern (see Freight-Fraud Red-Flag Checklist)
☐ Potential legal-boundary concern (see Module 02 shipper-direct boundary)

## Classification

☐ **PROCEED** — no unresolved issue; verified and clear
☐ **VERIFY BEFORE PROCEEDING** — specific item(s) need confirmation: ___________
☐ **STOP / DO NOT BOOK** — confirmed incompatibility or unresolved material inconsistency: ___________
☐ **ESCALATE** — issue exceeds ordinary dispatcher judgment or verification did not resolve the concern: ___________

**REMINDER:** Not every imperfect posting is an automatic rejection — missing information and ordinary verification needs are normal and routine. Reserve STOP/ESCALATE for confirmed incompatibilities, unresolved material inconsistencies, or genuine fraud/legal-boundary concerns, not for every incomplete posting.
