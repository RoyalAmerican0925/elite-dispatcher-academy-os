## Notes

- Highest posted rate is not automatically the best candidate — weigh deadhead and positioning alongside it.
- A candidate with unresolved broker-verification status or missing information should not outrank a fully verified candidate just because its rate looks better.
- When a candidate looks financially interesting enough to pursue seriously, that's the signal to move it into the fuller profitability workflow — this worksheet's job is only to identify which candidate(s) deserve that next step.
