## Opening

Use this framing, adapted to the specific load:

*"Hello, this is [Name], dispatching for [Carrier Legal Name], MC [number]. I'm calling about load [reference number] from [origin] to [destination]. Is it still available?"*

**IMPORTANT:** Always accurately represent your actual role and the carrier you're working for. Never claim to be the carrier owner, the driver, the broker, or another person.
