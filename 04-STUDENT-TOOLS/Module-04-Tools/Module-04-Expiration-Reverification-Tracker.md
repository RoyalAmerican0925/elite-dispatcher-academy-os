## Keep These Categories Separate

Do not invent a universal legal reverification interval where none exists. Every item above should be classified into one of these four categories — they are not interchangeable:

| Category | Meaning | Example |
|---|---|---|
| LEGAL EXPIRATION | A hard regulatory date | CDL expiration, medical certification expiration |
| ANNUAL RENEWAL | A recurring registration cycle | UCR |
| LOAD-BY-LOAD CHECK | Verified fresh for each dispatch decision | Current operating authority status, current insurance status |
| COMPANY/BEST-PRACTICE REVIEW | An internal policy choice, not a legal requirement | Periodic COI re-request, periodic W-9 re-confirmation |

**REMINDER:** A load-by-load check (e.g., confirming active operating authority via a current FMCSA source) should be verified fresh for that specific dispatch decision — a "last verified" date from weeks ago does not substitute for a current check when it matters.
