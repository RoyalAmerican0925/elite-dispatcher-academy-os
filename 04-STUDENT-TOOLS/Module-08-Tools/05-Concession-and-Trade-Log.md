## Conditional Trade Control
Write the trade in IF/THEN form:

**IF** ______________________________________________

**THEN** ____________________________________________

- Condition verified/confirmed? YES / NO / PENDING
- Where must the term be documented/reviewed next?

> A conditional trade is not an unconditional promise. If the condition is not established, do not record the result as fully agreed.

## Uncontrolled-Concession Warning
Before moving your position, answer:
- Did the broker move?
- Did a material fact improve?
- Did a material uncertainty get resolved?
- Are we receiving a defined condition/value?
- Is the movement authorized?

If all answers are NO, explain why movement is still defensible or HOLD the position.

**Instructor/practical use:** Exposes students who negotiate against themselves, cross the supplied floor, or treat vague broker promises as consideration.
