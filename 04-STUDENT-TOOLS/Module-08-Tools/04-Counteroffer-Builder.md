# Module 08 — Counteroffer Builder

**Purpose:** Construct concise, defensible counters from the prepared carrier position without random numbers, fabricated market claims, or unnecessary disclosure of internal economics.

**Point of use:** After sufficient discovery and whenever a new counter is considered.

**Competency evidence:** M08-C07, C08, C11, C12.

## Build the Counter
### 1. POSITION
What are you asking for?

### 2. BASIS
Which verified/supplied fact makes this position defensible?

Examples may include total-trip requirement, verified truck position, schedule burden, a real alternative, or another supplied operating condition.

**Do not disclose internal carrier cost details merely to prove the counter is valid.** A concise counter can still be evidence-grounded.

### 3. BOUNDARY
What can/can't you commit to under supplied authority?

### 4. QUESTION
What concise question invites a useful broker response?

## Counter Quality Test
- [ ] Number comes from prepared/supplied position, not a guess.
- [ ] Basis is truthful.
- [ ] No fabricated competing offer.
- [ ] No fabricated “market rate.”
- [ ] No false truck/driver/equipment statement.
- [ ] No unnecessary internal-cost disclosure.
- [ ] Counter stays inside authority.
- [ ] Language is professional and concise.
- [ ] Question creates a path to information or movement.
