## Decision Controls
- Offer at/above floor and all material terms acceptable within authority → may reach **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL**.
- Offer below floor with no authorization → **DECLINE or ESCALATE**, not “close enough.”
- Material economic input uncertain → **VERIFY BEFORE DECISION**.
- Target not reached but floor met → do not confuse disappointment with lack of authority.
- Floor met but a material term changed → re-evaluate; rate alone does not settle the decision.
