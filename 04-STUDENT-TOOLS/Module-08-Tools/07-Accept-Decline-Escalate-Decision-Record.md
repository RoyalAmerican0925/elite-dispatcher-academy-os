## Decision
Choose the status supported by the record:

### AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL
Use only when negotiated material points meet supplied carrier requirements and authority. This is **not BOOKED**.

### VERIFY BEFORE DECISION
Use when missing/conflicting material facts prevent a defensible close.

### ESCALATE
Use when an otherwise viable decision exceeds authority or requires higher review.

### DECLINE
Use when the opportunity fails supplied requirements or the authorized decision is not to proceed.
