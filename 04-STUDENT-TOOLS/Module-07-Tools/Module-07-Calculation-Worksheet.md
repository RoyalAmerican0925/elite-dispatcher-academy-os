Use this worksheet to evaluate one proposed load. The purpose is not merely to produce a number; it is to produce a **traceable economic decision record**.

## Operating Rule

For every material input, classify its status before calculating:

- **P — PROVIDED / VERIFIED FOR THIS EXERCISE OR TRANSACTION:** supported by the scenario, carrier, applicable agreement, route source, or other identified source.
- **U — UNKNOWN / UNRESOLVED:** not yet known well enough to use.
- **A — ASSUMED FOR TRAINING ONLY:** an explicitly fictional instructional assumption supplied for practice.

**Never silently convert U to zero.** A true zero is a known numeric value and must be distinguishable from an unknown field.

If a material unknown could reasonably reverse TAKE / COUNTER / DECLINE, classify the load **HOLD FOR DATA** until the missing input is resolved or the carrier explicitly directs how to treat it.
