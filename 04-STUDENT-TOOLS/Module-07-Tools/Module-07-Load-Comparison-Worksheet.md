## Presentation Notes

- Confirm every load used the same included-cost categories before comparing contribution-margin figures directly. If they didn't (e.g., one load has a factoring fee and another doesn't), say so explicitly rather than letting the numbers imply a clean apples-to-apples comparison.
- Present effective RPM alongside loaded RPM every time — never loaded RPM alone.
- Note next-load positioning as a separate, qualitative factor. It is not part of the dollar calculation, but it belongs in the conversation with the carrier.
- The carrier makes the final decision. This worksheet exists to give them accurate numbers, not to make the decision for them.
