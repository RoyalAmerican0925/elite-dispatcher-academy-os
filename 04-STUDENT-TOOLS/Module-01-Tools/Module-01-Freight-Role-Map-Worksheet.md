## Scenario A — Standard Brokered Load

A manufacturer in Fort Worth, Texas needs a truckload delivered to a retail distribution center in Tulsa, Oklahoma. The manufacturer uses a licensed freight broker to arrange transportation. The broker tenders the load to an authorized motor carrier. The carrier's dispatcher coordinates the truck and driver. The driver picks up, transports, and delivers the freight. After delivery, the carrier sends its invoice and proof of delivery to its factoring company for accelerated payment.
