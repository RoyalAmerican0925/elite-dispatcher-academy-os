# Elite Academy Operating System (EAOS)

EAOS is the long-term operating layer for producing, managing, delivering, assessing, and improving academy content.

## Target Capabilities
- curriculum lifecycle management
- structured content and versioning
- assessment engine
- simulation and scenario engine
- student progress tracking
- instructor dashboards
- QA and publication gates
- AI-assisted content production
- dependency and terminology control
- reusable academy infrastructure

## Relationship to the Dispatcher Academy
The Elite Trucking Dispatcher Academy is the first commercial implementation. The near-term course must work independently, while its content, IDs, metadata, assessments, and simulations should be organized so they can later be ingested into EAOS.
