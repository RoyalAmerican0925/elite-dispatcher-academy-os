# Module 08 — Broker Negotiation Desk Practical

**Total:** 30 points  
**Pass:** 24/30 AND zero unresolved critical failures.  
**Evidence rule:** First-attempt decisions remain part of the record after remediation.

## Candidate Brief
You are the carrier-side dispatcher for **Blue Ridge Carrier LLC**.

Supplied file:
- 53-foot dry van; solo driver
- Truck empty near Charlotte, NC
- Initial pickup window is operationally workable as supplied
- Module 07 economic work has already established: target **$2,700**; floor **$2,450**
- Below $2,450 requires actual carrier approval
- You may negotiate but may not invent equipment, driver, competing-offer, accessorial, or booking facts

Broker opportunity:
- Charlotte, NC → Columbus, OH
- Opening offer: $2,200
- One pickup / one delivery initially represented
- Pickup initially described as “afternoon”
- Delivery “next morning”
- Other material details must be discovered

Use all applicable Module 08 operational tools. Instructor releases events progressively; do not read ahead in a live administration.

## Event 1 — Prepare
Build the negotiation position. Identify material unknowns, authority, target/floor/walk-away condition, truthful leverage, and opening objective.

## Event 2 — Open & Discover
Broker says:
> “It's a normal dry load. Pickup around 2-ish. Everybody is moving these for $2,300.”

Respond professionally and conduct discovery. Distinguish broker statements from established facts.

## Event 3 — Facts Clarified
Broker establishes pickup at 2:00 PM and delivery at 9:00 AM next day. Broker offers $2,300.

Build and deliver a concise counter from the supplied position. Do not disclose internal economics merely to justify it.

## Event 4 — False-Leverage Opportunity
Broker says:
> “If you've really got something paying better, tell me now.”

You have NOT been supplied a competing offer. Respond.

## Event 5 — Resistance & Movement
Broker moves to $2,375 and says it is “pretty much max.” Then remains silent after your response.

Demonstrate resistance handling without negotiating against yourself.

## Event 6 — Conditional Value
Broker says:
> “I might get $2,425 if delivery can take you right away. Should be a quick unload.”

Determine whether the offer is within authority and whether the operational statement is established. Build any conditional response carefully.

## Event 7 — Agreement-Specific Term
Broker says:
> “If they hold you, we pay detention.”

Clarify/record the applicable **TRIGGER → AMOUNT/METHOD → EVIDENCE → PROCESS**. Do not invent universal terms.

## Event 8 — Floor Reached
Broker moves to $2,450. Explain whether this is target or floor and whether the rate alone closes all material issues.

## Event 9 — Material Change
Broker corrects the load:
> “One more thing—the pickup added a second stop 35 miles away before heading north.”

The supplied position has not been validated for the changed trip. Show the required status/action before continuing.

Instructor then supplies: after re-evaluation, carrier authorizes a revised floor of **$2,525** for the changed load; target remains $2,700.

## Event 10 — Authority Test
Broker offers $2,500 and says:
> “That's final. Just say yes and I'll send it.”

Respond under the revised authority.

If learner does not commit a critical failure, instructor releases:
> “I got $2,525 approved. Both pickup stops are confirmed. Delivery stays 9:00 AM. Detention was discussed but the evidence/process requirement is still unresolved.”

## Event 11 — Closeout
Create the three buckets:
- AGREED IN PRINCIPLE
- UNRESOLVED
- NOT DISCUSSED / NOT ESTABLISHED

Do not invent missing terms.

## Event 12 — Module 09 Handoff
Choose the supported status and produce a concise broker close plus internal handoff. The negotiated opportunity is not BOOKED merely because verbal alignment occurred.
