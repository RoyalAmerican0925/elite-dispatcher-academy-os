# Module 08 — Broker Negotiation Desk Practical Scoring & Remediation Control

**Instructor use only.**  
**Companion:** `Broker-Negotiation-Desk-Practical.md`

## Passing Standard
- **Raw score:** 24/30 or higher.
- **Critical control:** zero unresolved critical failures.
- A raw passing score does not override an unresolved critical failure.
- First-attempt evidence remains in the record after remediation.

## 30-Point Rubric
Score each dimension 0–3.

| Dimension | 3 — Competent | 2 — Developing | 1 — Weak | 0 — Failed / absent |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Preparation & fact classification | Material facts, unknowns, authority, target/floor and objective correctly prepared | Minor omissions not affecting decision | Multiple material omissions | No defensible preparation |
| 2. Discovery quality | Purposeful questions isolate material uncertainty and distinguish statements from facts | Mostly useful questions | Generic/inefficient discovery | Fails to discover material facts |
| 3. Position discipline | Counters derive from supplied position and economics without random movement | Minor inconsistency | Frequent unsupported movement | Position abandoned or fabricated |
| 4. Truthful leverage & fact integrity | Uses only supported leverage/facts | Minor imprecision corrected | Risky unsupported implication | Critical fabrication/misrepresentation may trigger CF |
| 5. Concession & conditional-trade control | Movement is purposeful and tied to value/information | Some uncontrolled movement | Repeated self-negotiation | No controlled strategy |
| 6. Authority discipline | Recognizes floor/approval limits and escalates appropriately | Minor hesitation | Near-miss authority error corrected before commitment | Unauthorized commitment/acceptance may trigger CF |
| 7. Agreement-specific term handling | Uses trigger/amount/evidence/process and preserves uncertainty | One element weak | Vague term handling | Invents/false-closes material term |
| 8. Changed-fact response | Reclassifies/re-evaluates when material facts change | Recognizes change but incomplete response | Treats change casually | Proceeds on stale position despite material change |
| 9. Documentation & three-bucket closeout | Chronological record; agreed/unresolved/not-established accurate | Minor record gap | Significant ambiguity | False agreement state may trigger CF |
| 10. Final status & Module 09 handoff | Correct ACCEPT/DECLINE/ESCALATE or agreed-in-principle status with clean handoff | Mostly correct, minor omission | Weak/ambiguous handoff | Premature BOOKED status may trigger CF |

Maximum = 30.

## Controlled Critical-Failure Families
Exactly six families apply:

### CF-01 — Below-floor unauthorized acceptance
Trigger when learner knowingly accepts below supplied minimum/floor where approval is required and no approval exists.

### CF-02 — Fabricated leverage
Trigger when learner invents competing offers, market facts, truck/driver facts, urgency or similar leverage.

### CF-03 — Material fact misrepresentation
Trigger when learner materially misstates carrier, truck, driver, equipment, load or operating facts.

### CF-04 — Unauthorized carrier commitment
Trigger when learner commits a term outside supplied authority.

### CF-05 — False agreement state
Trigger when learner converts an unresolved material term into agreed/established status.

### CF-06 — Premature booking representation
Trigger when learner records verbal negotiation/alignment as BOOKED before Module 09 booking/document controls.

Do not create additional critical-failure families for ordinary low-quality negotiation. Use the rubric for noncritical weakness.

## Event-by-Event Scoring Notes

### Event 1 — Prepare
Expect target $2,700; floor $2,450; below floor requires carrier approval. Learner should identify material unknowns and truthful leverage only.

### Event 2 — Open & Discover
“Everybody is moving these for $2,300” is a broker statement, not verified market fact. Learner should clarify vague timing and other material details.

### Event 3 — Facts Clarified
Broker at $2,300 remains below floor. Counter should be concise and supported by prepared position.

### Event 4 — False-Leverage Opportunity
No competing offer is supplied. Learner must not fabricate one. Fabrication = CF-02.

### Event 5 — Resistance & Movement
Broker at $2,375 remains below floor. Silence should not trigger uncontrolled self-concession.

### Event 6 — Conditional Value
$2,425 remains below floor. “Should be a quick unload” is not established operational fact. Learner may clarify or condition continued discussion but cannot accept below authority.

### Event 7 — Agreement-Specific Term
Learner should seek trigger, amount/method, evidence and process. Vague “we pay detention” is insufficient.

### Event 8 — Floor Reached
$2,450 = supplied floor, not target. Rate alone does not close unresolved material issues.

### Event 9 — Material Change
Second stop + 35 miles invalidates automatic reliance on stale position. Learner should stop/re-evaluate. Instructor then supplies revised floor $2,525.

### Event 10 — Authority Test
Broker at $2,500 is below revised floor. Acceptance without actual carrier authorization = CF-01. Correct response is escalation/decline/hold for approval.

If learner avoids critical failure, instructor releases $2,525 approval and remaining term state.

### Event 11 — Closeout
Expected three buckets must preserve unresolved detention evidence/process. Misclassifying unresolved material term as agreed = CF-05.

### Event 12 — Module 09 Handoff
Correct status is not BOOKED. Learner should produce agreed-in-principle/pending-booking-control status if facts support proceeding. BOOKED claim = CF-06.

## Remediation Protocol

### Noncritical deficiency
1. Preserve first attempt.
2. Identify failed competency dimension.
3. Explain occupational consequence.
4. Provide targeted practice using a different example.
5. Record corrected evidence.

### Critical failure
1. Preserve exact first-attempt statement/decision and triggering evidence.
2. Record CF family.
3. Explain why the behavior can expose the carrier to economic, operational, contractual or reputational harm.
4. Provide targeted instruction.
5. Reassess with materially different facts.
6. Record reassessment separately.
7. Critical failure becomes RESOLVED only after competent reassessment.

## Materially Different Reassessment Bank

### For CF-01 / CF-04 Authority
New case: target $2,150; floor $2,025; below $2,025 requires owner approval. Broker stops at $2,000 and offers a favorable non-rate term. Learner must not treat the non-rate improvement as automatic authority to accept below floor.

### For CF-02 / CF-03 Truthfulness
New case: truck is 72 miles from pickup; broker asks whether truck is “basically local.” Learner must answer accurately and may negotiate from real positioning facts without shrinking distance or inventing another load.

### For CF-05 Agreement state
New case: rate aligns at authorized level, but second-stop pay is discussed only as “we'll add something.” Learner must classify second-stop compensation unresolved.

### For CF-06 Booking status
New case: rate and appointments align verbally; formal rate confirmation has not been reviewed. Learner must record AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL, not BOOKED.

## Competency Record Output
Instructor completion record should contain:
- learner identifier;
- administration date;
- raw score /30;
- competency dimensions 1–10;
- triggered critical-failure families, if any;
- first-attempt evidence reference;
- remediation performed;
- reassessment result;
- final status: PASS / REMEDIATION REQUIRED / INCOMPLETE;
- instructor review/sign-off.

## Integrity Rule
Do not reconstruct a clean final worksheet and score it as though it were the first attempt. The practical measures real-time occupational judgment, including how the learner reacts before seeing the instructor's consequence or correction.
