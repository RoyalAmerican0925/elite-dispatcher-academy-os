# Module 08 — Broker Negotiation Applied Lab Answer & Rationale Control

**Instructor use only.**  
**Companion:** `Broker-Negotiation-Applied-Lab.md`

## Administration Rule
The lab is guided practice, but first-attempt reasoning must be captured before coaching. Accept equivalent professional wording; score the decision logic, truthfulness, authority discipline and evidence handling.

## Stage 1 — PREPARE
Expected:
- PREPARED only if material facts are sufficient for an opening position; otherwise VERIFY BEFORE COUNTER.
- Target = $2,500; floor = $2,300; below floor requires carrier approval.
- Broker offer = $2,050, not carrier floor or market truth.
- “Morning,” “easy,” detention, layover and drop/hook are not established.
- Truthful leverage may include verified truck/equipment/availability facts supplied by the file; no competing opportunity is supplied.

Remediate if learner invents missing facts, treats broker language as verified truth, or has no authority boundary.

## Stage 2 — OPEN + QUESTION
Expected discovery should narrow exact pickup timing and other material operating/term uncertainty. Learner should record “everyone runs this lane for around $2,100” as a broker statement, not verified market evidence.

Accept different question wording if it is purposeful. Do not reward long generic scripts.

## Stage 3 — VERIFY + POSITION
Expected:
- Broker is now at $2,150.
- Initial appointment facts are more precise.
- Student should counter from the prepared carrier position, normally at or near the supplied target unless a stated case reason supports another authorized opening.
- Student may cite trip/position burden concisely without revealing internal carrier economics unnecessarily.

Critical if leverage/facts are fabricated or materially misrepresented.

## Stage 4 — RESISTANCE
Expected:
- Broker movement to $2,200 changes broker position, not carrier authority.
- “Need an answer now” does not change floor or authority.
- Silence does not justify automatic concessions.
- Professional response may hold, ask a purposeful question, restate supported position, or make a controlled authorized move.

## Stage 5 — CONDITIONAL TRADE
Expected:
- $2,250 remains below $2,300 floor; no acceptance authority.
- “Supposed to be drop-and-hook” is not established.
- Conditional response should tie any movement or continued discussion to actual confirmation/value, without promising acceptance below authority.

## Stage 6 — AGREEMENT-SPECIFIC TERM
Expected four-part control:
1. TRIGGER — when detention begins/applies.
2. AMOUNT/METHOD — compensation amount or calculation method.
3. EVIDENCE — documentation/timestamps/etc. required.
4. PROCESS — notice/submission/request procedure.

“After a while” is insufficient. Do not import universal detention assumptions.

## Stage 7 — FLOOR EVENT
Expected:
- $2,300 is floor, not target.
- Rate alone does not settle detention/drop-hook or other material unresolved terms.
- A floor-reaching rate can support continued closeout only if other material conditions are acceptable or clearly preserved as unresolved for the appropriate later workflow.

## Stage 8 — MATERIAL FACT CHANGE
Expected:
- Pickup shifts from 10:00 AM to 6:00 AM.
- Status becomes VERIFY BEFORE DECISION because supplied case does not establish that prior position remains valid.
- Learner identifies need to re-check operating fit/economic position/authority as applicable.

Critical risk: learner knowingly proceeds as though the changed fact did not matter when the scenario explicitly says position validity is not established.

## Stage 9 — AUTHORITY PRESSURE
After re-evaluation, floor remains $2,300 and 6:00 AM is workable.

Broker at $2,275:
- correct path: decline/hold/escalate for actual approval;
- incorrect: accept because difference is small.

When broker returns with $2,300, that movement must be recorded chronologically. Detention still unresolved; drop-and-hook never confirmed.

## Stage 10 — CONFIRM + DOCUMENT
Expected buckets should reflect only evidence actually established.

Likely AGREED IN PRINCIPLE:
- rate $2,300;
- 6:00 AM pickup confirmed;
- other explicitly confirmed core movement facts from the scenario.

UNRESOLVED:
- detention details.

NOT DISCUSSED / NOT ESTABLISHED:
- drop-and-hook.

Critical if learner places unresolved detention into agreed terms or invents drop-and-hook.

## Stage 11 — DECIDE + HANDOFF
Expected status if all supplied conditions support proceeding:
**AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL**

Module 09 handoff should include:
- negotiated rate/status;
- material terms to verify in formal documents;
- unresolved detention details;
- broker statements requiring confirmation;
- next responsible action.

Critical if learner marks BOOKED inside Module 08.

## Acceptable Variation
Do not require exact sentences. A response is acceptable when it is truthful, within authority, tied to supplied facts, clear on conditions, and accurately documented.

## Critical-Failure Mapping
- CF-01 Below-floor unauthorized acceptance
- CF-02 Fabricated leverage
- CF-03 Material fact misrepresentation
- CF-04 Unauthorized carrier commitment
- CF-05 False agreement state
- CF-06 Premature booking representation

## Remediation Rule
For noncritical errors, coach the specific reasoning gap after first-attempt capture.

For any critical failure:
1. preserve original response;
2. explain the occupational risk;
3. provide targeted instruction;
4. reassess with materially different facts;
5. record corrected performance separately.

Do not erase the first attempt and do not reassess by repeating the identical question.
