# Module 08 — Broker Negotiation Applied Lab

**Type:** Progressive guided practice — not the final graded practical.

**Purpose:** Make the learner operate the Module 08 workflow using the seven Module 08 desk tools while facts, broker positions, and authority pressures change.

**Framework:** PREPARE → VERIFY FACTS → SET POSITION → OPEN → QUESTION → LISTEN → COUNTER → TRADE → CONFIRM → DOCUMENT → ACCEPT / DECLINE / ESCALATE

## Supplied Carrier File
You represent **Northstar Motor Carrier** for this exercise.

- Equipment: 53-foot dry van
- Truck: empty near Fort Worth, Texas
- Driver: solo driver; availability supplied as compatible with the initial pickup window
- Negotiating target: **$2,500**
- Supplied floor: **$2,300**
- Below $2,300: requires carrier approval; you do not have standing authority to accept below it
- No authority to invent or commit accessorial terms not supplied/confirmed
- Module 07 output has already been completed for the initial facts and supports the supplied target/floor exercise position

## Initial Opportunity
Broker offers a dry-van load from Fort Worth to Nashville.

Initial broker information:
- Opening rate: $2,050
- Pickup: tomorrow; broker initially says “morning”
- Delivery: following day
- One pickup / one delivery
- Broker says the load is “easy”
- Detention, layover, exact appointment, and drop/hook status are not yet established

### Stage 1 — PREPARE
Use:
- `01-Negotiation-Preparation-Sheet.md`
- `02-Target-Floor-Walk-Away-Worksheet.md`

Produce:
1. Carrier position.
2. Material known/unknown list.
3. Target/floor/walk-away distinction.
4. Truthful leverage available from the supplied file.
5. Opening objective.
6. Conditions that would trigger VERIFY, ESCALATE, or DECLINE.

**Checkpoint:** Do not treat “morning” or “easy” as sufficiently precise operating facts.

### Stage 2 — OPEN + QUESTION
Use `03-Broker-Discovery-Call-Sheet.md`.

Draft a concise professional opening and the first 3–5 purposeful questions.

The broker responds:
> “Pickup is sometime in the morning. Everybody runs this lane for around $2,100, and I don't have much room.”

Record:
- what the broker actually stated;
- what remains unverified;
- the likely negotiation constraint(s) worth exploring;
- the next purposeful question.

**Required judgment:** Do not automatically concede and do not accuse the broker of lying.

### Stage 3 — VERIFY + POSITION
The broker clarifies:
- pickup appointment is initially listed as **10:00 AM**;
- delivery appointment is **2:00 PM the following day**;
- commodity/equipment facts supplied remain compatible with the exercise carrier file;
- broker increases offer to **$2,150**.

Use `04-Counteroffer-Builder.md`.

Prepare a counter that:
- comes from the supplied position;
- is concise;
- uses only truthful/supportable facts;
- does not unnecessarily disclose internal carrier economics;
- includes a useful next question.

### Stage 4 — RESISTANCE
Broker says:
> “Best I can probably do is $2,200. If you want it, I need an answer now.”

Record:
1. what changed;
2. what did not change;
3. whether urgency changes your authority;
4. your professional response.

Then the broker pauses without making another offer.

**Required judgment:** Do not negotiate against yourself merely because of silence.

### Stage 5 — CONDITIONAL TRADE
Broker says:
> “I can maybe get to $2,250, and it's supposed to be drop-and-hook at delivery.”

Use `05-Concession-and-Trade-Log.md`.

Determine:
- whether $2,250 is within acceptance authority;
- whether “supposed to be” establishes drop-and-hook;
- a controlled conditional-trade response;
- what must be confirmed before the claimed term has decision value.

### Stage 6 — AGREEMENT-SPECIFIC TERM
Broker adds:
> “We take care of good carriers on detention after a while.”

Do not invent a universal policy.

Build the clarification using:
**TRIGGER → AMOUNT/METHOD → EVIDENCE → PROCESS**

Record each element as established, unresolved, or not established.

### Stage 7 — FLOOR EVENT
Broker moves to **$2,300** under the initial facts.

Explain:
- whether this is target;
- whether it is floor;
- whether rate alone settles every material term;
- what still must be confirmed before a clean negotiation closeout.

### Stage 8 — MATERIAL FACT CHANGE
Before closeout, broker says:
> “Correction — pickup was moved to **6:00 AM**, not 10:00 AM.”

The supplied exercise file does not establish that the original position remains valid after this change.

Required action:
- classify the new appointment as material;
- stop treating the earlier position as automatically current;
- identify what must be re-evaluated/verified;
- record the status **VERIFY BEFORE DECISION** until the position is re-established.

### Stage 9 — AUTHORITY PRESSURE
For this lab, after re-evaluation the instructor supplies that the carrier's floor remains **$2,300** and the 6:00 AM appointment is workable.

Broker then says:
> “I can do $2,275 final.”

Record your decision.

**Critical control:** No authority exists to accept $2,275. The correct path is decline/hold/escalate for actual approval—not “close enough.”

The broker then returns with:
> “Okay. I got $2,300 approved. Six AM pickup is confirmed. Detention details still need to be clarified. Drop-and-hook was never confirmed.”

This event explicitly establishes the final rate movement; do not skip it in the evidence record.

### Stage 10 — CONFIRM + DOCUMENT
Use `06-Negotiation-Call-Record.md`.

Build the three-bucket closeout:

**AGREED IN PRINCIPLE**
- classify only what the progressive evidence actually established.

**UNRESOLVED**
- identify discussed terms that still require clarification/confirmation.

**NOT DISCUSSED / NOT ESTABLISHED**
- identify items never actually established.

Do not clean up uncertainty to make the file look complete.

### Stage 11 — DECIDE + HANDOFF
Use `07-Accept-Decline-Escalate-Decision-Record.md`.

Choose the supported Module 08 status and explain it.

If the supplied evidence supports proceeding, the strongest status is:
**AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL**

Then create the Module 09 handoff showing:
- negotiated rate/status;
- material terms requiring formal-document review;
- unresolved items;
- broker statements needing confirmation;
- next responsible action.

**Never mark this load BOOKED inside Module 08.**
