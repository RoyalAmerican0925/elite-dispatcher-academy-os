# Module 09 Practical — Booking Control Desk

**Purpose:** Demonstrate occupational booking-control performance using a multi-document freight file.

**Score:** 30 points (10 dimensions × 0–3)  
**Pass:** 24/30 **and zero unresolved critical failures**.

## Fictional case packet
Learner receives:
- M08 negotiation handoff;
- broker communication trail;
- RC v1 with two material conflicts and one clerical discrepancy;
- correction message;
- RC v2 that fixes one conflict but changes another material term;
- carrier-supplied acceptance/signature authority;
- driver/truck/equipment identity facts;
- one obsolete document from a different load;
- source documents for the eventual dispatch packet.

## Required tasks
1. **Handoff classification:** separate agreed, conditional, unresolved and unverified facts.
2. **Document identity:** establish broker/carrier/load/version/source before acting.
3. **Material-term extraction:** complete the rate-confirmation extraction record.
4. **Reconciliation:** compare written terms to M08 evidence field by field.
5. **Discrepancy classification:** material / clerical / missing / later-workflow.
6. **Correction loop:** draft or record the correct correction/clarification request.
7. **Authority control:** determine what may be accepted/signed and what requires approval.
8. **Version/integrity control:** preserve history, identify superseded documents, exclude wrong/obsolete evidence.
9. **Booking record and dispatch packet:** build a traceable booking record and current packet.
10. **Final decision/handoff:** issue BOOKED/READY, HOLD, CORRECT, or ESCALATE with evidence and correct M10 handoff.

## Student deliverables
Use all nine M09 desk tools or equivalent instructor-approved forms. Every conclusion must identify its evidence source.

## Critical-stop rule
If a learner commits the carrier outside supplied authority, accepts/dispatches from a materially conflicting RC, falsely resolves a material discrepancy, uses an obsolete/wrong/unverified RC for dispatch, fabricates/materially alters evidence, or falsely marks the file booked, the practical cannot pass until materially different reassessment resolves that family.

## Scope boundary
Do not grade detailed routing/fuel/HOS/active monitoring/billing as M09 performance. The learner may identify a later-workflow issue and hand it off.
