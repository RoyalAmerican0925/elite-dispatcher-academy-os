# Module 09 Knowledge Check — Booking Control

**Use:** diagnostic + coached application. Not the final graded gate.

1. What best describes an agreement in principle?  
A. A completed M09 booking  B. A negotiation outcome still subject to booking controls  C. A BOL  D. A paid invoice

2. Which document is most directly used to record broker/carrier booking terms for a specific load in this module?  
A. POD  B. Rate confirmation  C. Invoice  D. Fuel receipt

3. A matching dollar amount but conflicting driver-assist obligation should be classified:  
A. Ignore  B. Material mismatch  C. Automatically clerical  D. M10 issue only

4. A blank delivery time means:  
A. Guess from lane length  B. Use load-board time  C. UNKNOWN/MISSING until verified  D. Driver chooses

5. Why separate extraction from reconciliation?  
A. To reduce confirmation bias and preserve an audit trail  B. To make booking slower  C. Because rate never matters  D. Because written terms always win

A. Use newest download  B. Version-control and verify controlling document  C. Average times  D. Delete both

7. Dispatcher authority allows acceptance only when written terms match approved negotiation. RC adds a new material obligation. Correct status:  
A. BOOKED  B. HOLD/CORRECT or ESCALATE  C. Sign then ask  D. Ignore

8. Which is a M09 responsibility?

9. Which is a later M10 responsibility?

10. A later RC supersedes v1. What belongs in the dispatch packet?  
A. v1 only  B. Both without labels  C. Current controlled version, with history retained separately  D. Whichever prints clearer

11. A broker email says “fixed” but no corrected RC is supplied where the written conflict remains material. Status:  
A. Automatically resolved  B. Verify/correct controlling evidence  C. BOOKED  D. Delete old RC

12. Which is a critical integrity failure?  
A. Recording a typo  B. Fabricating or materially altering booking evidence  C. Asking for clarification  D. Logging a version

13. A wrong carrier legal name on the RC should be:  
A. Assumed harmless  B. Verified/corrected before controlled use  C. Changed by dispatcher without record  D. Ignored if rate matches

14. A term “detention per broker policy” should be handled by:  
A. Inventing a standard dollar amount  B. Recording exact language and identifying referenced policy when material  C. Ignoring it  D. Treating it as federal law

15. What does `BOOKED / DISPATCH PACKET READY` mean?  
A. Trip fully complete  B. M09 controls satisfied and packet ready for M10 handoff  C. Invoice ready  D. Driver HOS guaranteed

### Applied freight-file scenario
Use this fictional evidence for 16–20: M08 shows $2,600, pickup 08:00, no driver assist, dispatcher authority only for exact-match terms. RC v1 shows $2,600, pickup 08:00, driver assist required. RC v2 later removes driver assist but changes pickup to 06:00 without explanation.

16. RC v1 driver-assist status? **MISMATCH — MATERIAL.**  
17. Can dispatcher accept v1 under supplied authority? **No.**  
18. Does v2 automatically resolve the file? **No; pickup time now conflicts and requires verification/correction.**  
19. Which version belongs in the eventual dispatch packet? **The verified current controlling version only; prior versions remain in history.**  
20. Final M09 status before pickup conflict is resolved? **HOLD/CORRECT or ESCALATE, not BOOKED.**
