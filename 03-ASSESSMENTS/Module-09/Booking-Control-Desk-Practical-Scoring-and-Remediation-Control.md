# Module 09 Practical — Scoring & Remediation Control

**Assessment:** Booking Control Desk Practical  
**Maximum:** 30 points  
**Pass:** 24/30 AND zero unresolved critical failures.

## 1. Ten scoring dimensions
Score each dimension 0–3.

| Dimension | 3 — Job-ready | 2 — Competent with minor gap | 1 — Material coaching required | 0 — Unsafe / missing / unsupported |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 1. Handoff classification | Correctly separates agreed, conditional, unresolved, unverified facts with sources | One minor classification/source gap | Multiple unsupported classifications | Treats negotiation as completed booking or guesses material facts |
| 2. Document identity | Correct broker/carrier/load/version/source established before action | Minor nonmaterial omission | Identity control incomplete | Acts on wrong/unverified identity |
| 3. Material-term extraction | All material supplied terms extracted accurately | One minor omission | Several material omissions | Headline-rate scan or materially wrong extraction |
| 4. Reconciliation | Field-by-field verbal/written comparison is accurate and traceable | Minor traceability gap | Misses a material comparison | Treats conflicting evidence as automatically reconciled |
| 5. Discrepancy classification | Correct material/clerical/missing/later-workflow classifications with rationale | One defensible minor error | Materiality reasoning weak | Material conflict ignored or falsely resolved |
| 6. Correction/escalation | Correct issue, evidence, requested correction and status recorded | Minor wording/process gap | Correction request incomplete | Advances booking without required correction/escalation |
| 7. Authority control | Correctly identifies supplied acceptance/signature limits and approvals | Minor documentation gap | Authority reasoning incomplete | Unauthorized commitment/acceptance/signature |
| 8. Version/integrity control | Preserves history, identifies current/superseded versions, excludes wrong document | One minor log gap | Version control incomplete | Uses wrong/obsolete/unverified document or alters evidence |
| 9. Booking record/dispatch packet | Complete traceable booking record; only current verified packet documents included | Minor nonmaterial omission | Packet/record needs material correction | Dispatch packet materially unreliable |
| 10. Final decision/M10 handoff | Correct BOOKED/READY, HOLD, CORRECT or ESCALATE state with evidence and proper M10 handoff | Minor handoff wording gap | State rationale incomplete | False completion or performs unsupported later-module conclusions |

## 2. Critical-failure families
Exactly six families control this practical and SIM-009.

### M09-CF-01 — Unauthorized carrier commitment
Trigger: learner accepts, signs, commits, or represents carrier acceptance outside the supplied authority record.

### M09-CF-02 — Material rate-confirmation acceptance error
Trigger: learner knowingly accepts or dispatches from a written confirmation containing a material conflict with supplied agreement evidence without correction or authorized resolution.

### M09-CF-03 — Unresolved verbal/written discrepancy represented as resolved
Trigger: learner marks a material conflict as agreed/resolved/booked while the evidence remains conflicting or missing.

### M09-CF-04 — Wrong/obsolete/unverified booking document used for dispatch
Trigger: learner knowingly uses a superseded, wrong-load, wrong-carrier, or otherwise unverified booking document as the dispatch basis.

### M09-CF-05 — Document fabrication/material alteration
Trigger: learner fabricates, materially alters, backdates, deletes, or misrepresents booking evidence, approval, source, or change history.

### M09-CF-06 — False booking completion
Trigger: learner represents the file as `BOOKED / DISPATCH PACKET READY` while a required M09 booking control remains unresolved.

No additional critical family may be invented during scoring. Noncritical errors are scored in the ten dimensions.

## 3. First-attempt evidence
For every practical attempt preserve:
- original learner work product;
- original score by dimension;
- critical family triggered, if any;
- triggering artifact/action and timestamp or assessment-session marker;
- instructor rationale.

Do not overwrite first-attempt evidence after coaching.

## 4. Ordinary remediation
For noncritical deficiencies:
1. identify the missed rule or evidence field;
2. coach the reasoning process without supplying the finished answer;
3. assign a changed example or correction task;
4. record corrected mastery separately from first attempt.

Ordinary remediation may improve final mastery but does not rewrite the first-attempt score.

## 5. Critical reassessment
Same-file or same-screen correction does **not** resolve a critical family. Resolution requires a materially different fact pattern testing the same competency.

| Family | Required different reassessment |
|---|---|
| CF-01 | New carrier authority matrix with different acceptance/signature limits and a different broker request |
| CF-02 | New negotiation handoff + RC containing a different material conflict such as appointment, equipment, rate component, or service obligation |
| CF-03 | New evidence set with conflicting verbal/email/written terms requiring evidence-supported unresolved/resolved classification |
| CF-04 | New multi-version document set containing a different obsolete/wrong-load/current-document trap |
| CF-05 | New change-history scenario requiring correction without altering or backdating source evidence |
| CF-06 | New closeout file with a different unresolved booking control requiring HOLD/CORRECT/ESCALATE rather than false completion |

Resolution label: **RESOLVED BY REASSESSMENT**. The historical trigger remains visible.

## 6. Final result states
- **PASS:** >=24/30 and no unresolved CF.
- **REMEDIATION REQUIRED:** <24/30 with no unresolved CF.
- **CRITICAL REASSESSMENT REQUIRED:** one or more unresolved CFs regardless of raw score.
- **PASS AFTER REMEDIATION/REASSESSMENT:** final mastery requirements met; original evidence remains preserved.

## 7. Instructor evidence rule
Do not award credit for an unsupported conclusion merely because the final action happens to be safe. Job-ready performance requires the learner to identify the controlling evidence and produce a traceable booking-control record.
