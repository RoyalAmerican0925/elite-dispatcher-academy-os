## Critical-family crosswalk
- CF-01 unauthorized carrier commitment → C09
- CF-02 material RC acceptance error → C05/C07
- CF-03 unresolved verbal/written discrepancy represented resolved → C05/C06/C14
- CF-04 wrong/obsolete/unverified booking document used for dispatch → C10/C12
- CF-05 document fabrication/material alteration → C10
- CF-06 false booking completion → C14/C15

## Evidence standard
A definition-only response cannot independently satisfy an occupational competency. Graded evidence must require the learner to interpret supplied facts/documents, produce or complete a work product, make a defensible booking-control decision, or explain an evidence-supported escalation/handoff.

## Scope rule
M09 competency evidence may identify a later-workflow issue but must not grade detailed M10 trip planning, M11 HOS/ELD, M12 monitoring, M13 exception/claim, or M14 billing/closeout performance as if it belongs to M09.
