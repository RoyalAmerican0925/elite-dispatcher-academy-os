# Module 09 Applied Lab — Answer & Rationale Control

## Controlling conclusions
1. M08 rate/lane/equipment/time facts are agreement-in-principle evidence; formal booking remains pending M09 controls.
2. RC v1 driver-assist requirement conflicts with M08 “no driver assist.” This is **MISMATCH — MATERIAL** because it changes carrier service obligation.
3. Under supplied authority, dispatcher may not accept/sign v1. Correct state is **HOLD / CORRECT**; escalate if correction is refused or authority is insufficient.
4. Broker message is evidence of intended correction but does not itself erase the conflicting written v1. Preserve it in the correction history.
5. RC v2 supersedes v1 for continued review only after identity/source/version are controlled. v1 remains historical evidence and is not deleted.
6. v2 creates a new material pickup-time conflict: 12:00 written versus 14:00 M08 handoff. The learner must not assume the change is beneficial or harmless.
7. Required next evidence is broker clarification/corrected controlled document and, if the change creates a term outside supplied authority, documented carrier approval.
8. Dispatcher cannot sign v2 under the supplied exact-match authority while pickup remains unresolved.
9. Dispatch packet is not ready. v1 must not be used as current dispatch instruction; v2 remains under review. Supporting booking history stays office-side.
10. Correct final lab status before resolution: **HOLD / CORRECT** or **ESCALATE** depending the supplied communication/authority outcome, never `BOOKED / DISPATCH PACKET READY`.
11. M10 handoff occurs only after M09 has a controlled current booking document and required booking facts; M10 then evaluates detailed routing/time/fuel/appointment feasibility.

## Scoring rationale
Award credit for evidence-supported reasoning, not answer words alone. A learner who says “hold” but cannot identify the conflicting source/term has not demonstrated C05–C07. A learner who notices the driver-assist conflict but signs anyway under the supplied authority triggers CF-01/CF-02 exposure.

## Acceptable correction request
Must identify load NL-9041, the exact conflicting term, expected term from controlled M08 evidence, request a corrected written confirmation, and avoid inventing authority or threatening/fabricating leverage.

## Critical coaching flags
- signing/accepting outside supplied authority;
- representing a material discrepancy as resolved without evidence;
- deleting or overwriting v1 history;
- sending v1 or unresolved v2 as current driver instruction;
- marking the load booked before pickup-time conflict is controlled.
