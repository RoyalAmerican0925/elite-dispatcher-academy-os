## Purpose

Practice disciplined broker/contact/payment-path verification and escalation without making unsupported fraud accusations. This is a practice/instructional scenario lab, not a secure graded exam.

## Student Instructions

For each scenario:

1. Choose the best operational classification or next-step status.
2. Explain the facts that support your choice.
3. Identify the verification, hold, escalation, or stop action that should follow.
4. Do not diagnose fraud unless the facts independently establish it. Use professional operational language.

Suggested response format:

- **Classification / status:**
- **Reasoning:**
- **Next verification or escalation action:**
