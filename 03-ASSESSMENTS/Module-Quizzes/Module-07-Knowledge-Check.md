## Part A — Multiple Choice

1. Loaded rate per mile (Loaded RPM) is calculated as:
   a) Total gross load revenue ÷ total trip miles
   b) Gross load revenue ÷ loaded miles
   c) Gross load revenue ÷ origin deadhead
   d) Total trip miles ÷ truck MPG

2. Effective revenue per total mile (Effective RPM) is calculated as:
   a) Gross load revenue ÷ loaded miles
   b) Total gross load revenue ÷ total trip miles
   c) Estimated contribution margin ÷ total trip miles
   d) Loaded miles ÷ total trip miles

3. Total trip miles is calculated as:
   a) Loaded miles only
   b) Origin deadhead plus loaded miles plus relevant destination/repositioning deadhead
   c) Loaded miles minus origin deadhead
   d) Origin deadhead multiplied by loaded miles

4. Estimated contribution margin, as calculated in this module, is best described as:
   a) The carrier's net profit on the load
   b) Applicable load revenue minus the included variable trip costs, with those costs explicitly stated
   c) The same figure as cash flow
   d) A figure that already accounts for driver pay, insurance, and equipment costs

5. A carrier's true break-even cost per mile:
   a) Can be accurately calculated by a dispatcher from a single load
   b) Is the same for every carrier in the industry
   c) Depends on that carrier's own complete cost structure and accounting methodology
   d) Is published by the EIA
