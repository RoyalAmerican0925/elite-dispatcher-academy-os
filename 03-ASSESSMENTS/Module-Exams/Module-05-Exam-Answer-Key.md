## Answer Key

### Part A — Sourcing Concepts

1. **b** — A load board is a freight-opportunity marketplace/tool, not an authority database.
2. **a** — A broker portal is associated with a particular broker/network; it does not replace regulatory verification.
3. **a** — Spot freight is generally a one-time/short-term market movement rather than an ongoing committed lane agreement.
4. **b** — Contract/dedicated freight reflects a recurring/prearranged relationship; terms still vary.
5. **a** — Origin deadhead affects positioning distance and whether the opportunity is practical.
6. **b** — FOUND means a potentially relevant opportunity has been located.
7. **b** — VERIFIED AVAILABLE requires confirmation that the freight is actually still open.
8. **b** — FCFS still operates within real facility hours/conditions.

### Part B — True / False

9. **False** — Posting age is a priority signal, not proof of availability or unavailability.
10. **False** — Duplicate-looking postings may have several explanations; verify before concluding double brokering.
11. **True** — Missing material information must be surfaced before the posting can be treated as ready for later decision layers.
12. **True** — Deadhead is relevant during sourcing, while detailed profitability analysis is intentionally deferred to Module 07.

### Part C — Sourcing Screen Scenarios

13. **GOOD CANDIDATE** — Equipment, commodity, lane, timing, and current availability fit the supplied carrier profile.
14. **BAD FIT / REJECT** — Flatbed/open-deck requirement conflicts with reefer-only equipment.
15. **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY** — Critical timing/location facts are missing.
16. **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY** — Availability is not yet confirmed; FOUND is not VERIFIED AVAILABLE.
17. **BAD FIT / REJECT** on the supplied carrier-policy/home-time facts — delivery timing conflicts with the known home-time requirement.
18. **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY** — Too many material fields are missing to classify the load as a viable candidate.
19. **BAD FIT / REJECT** — The destination conflicts with the carrier's stated lane restriction.
20. **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY** — Duplicate postings alone do not establish fraud/double brokering; verify the relationship/status.
21. **GOOD CANDIDATE** — All supplied sourcing-stage facts fit and availability is confirmed; route to the next workflow stage.
22. **GOOD CANDIDATE as to endorsement fit** — Non-bulk bottled water in a dry van does not create a tanker-endorsement requirement on the supplied facts. Continue normal screening for all other factors.
23. **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY** — Weight is high enough that carrier acceptance/loading/axle feasibility should not be assumed from the "53V" label alone.

### Part D — Critical Scenario

24. **c — STOP / ESCALATE. CRITICAL.** General shipper solicitation followed by discretionary selection among unrelated represented carriers presents the Module 02 brokerage/allocation-of-traffic risk. Charging the carrier does not eliminate the conduct issue. **Any other answer fails the exam regardless of overall score.**

### Part E — Sample Reasoning

25. **Expected:** FOUND only means an opportunity was located. VERIFIED AVAILABLE means the posting party/platform has confirmed it is still open. Keeping them separate prevents wasted work, false assumptions, and premature pricing/booking decisions on freight that may already be covered.

26. **Expected:** Any three or more relevant facts such as exact origin/destination, pickup and delivery dates/times/windows, commodity, weight, equipment details, availability confirmation, special instructions, appointment method, carrier lane/home-time restrictions, broker/contact identity, or other material operating conditions. The answer should show the learner does not jump from lane/equipment fit directly to booking.

## Scoring

Passing standard: **80% = 21/26**, **and Item 24 must be correct.**

A student who reaches 21/26 but misses Item 24 does not pass and must complete legal-boundary remediation before retesting.
