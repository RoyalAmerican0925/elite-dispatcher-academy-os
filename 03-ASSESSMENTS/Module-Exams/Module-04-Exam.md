## Part C — Onboarding Decision Scenarios

For Items 13–22 choose the best operational classification: **READY / PROCEED**, **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY**, **DO NOT DISPATCH**, or **STOP / ESCALATE**.

13. A carrier's current authority is verified, insurance filing is appropriate for its operation, driver/equipment profile is complete, and no unresolved onboarding mismatch remains.

14. A clean-looking COI is supplied, but the current FMCSA authority source shows the carrier's required operating authority is inactive.

15. The W-9 lists the LLC's owner name while the FMCSA record lists the disregarded LLC. The tax/entity relationship has not yet been documented.

16. A factoring Notice of Assignment directs payment to a factor, but a new email from an unfamiliar address says future payments should instead go to a different bank account.

17. A driver's CDL class and equipment fit are verified, but the dispatch profile does not contain current contact information or the driver's actual availability/status.

18. A dispatcher is asked to keep the carrier's full DQ file, run the carrier's drug/alcohol compliance program, and make qualification determinations outside the dispatcher's approved scope.

19. During the current NRII exemption period, a newly issued paper MEC is 30 days old and appears facially valid under the exemption window, but the electronic medical-certification record has not yet updated. The dispatcher has not yet verified the certificate/driver match or confirmed the carrier's process.

20. A carrier's COI shows appropriate insurance information, but the dispatcher has not checked the current authority/registration source at all.

21. A dispatcher receives a text message from someone claiming to be the carrier owner directing that all factoring payments be rerouted immediately. The number is not one previously verified for the owner.

22. **CRITICAL-FAILURE ITEM.** The load is a placarded hazardous-material shipment requiring an H or X endorsement. The available driver does not hold the required endorsement. Classification:
   a) READY / PROCEED
   b) NEEDS MORE INFORMATION only — dispatch now and resolve later
   c) DO NOT DISPATCH
   d) Treat the endorsement as optional if the carrier agrees
