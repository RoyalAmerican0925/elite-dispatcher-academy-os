   a) A carrier's internal driver file
   b) A marketplace/tool where freight opportunities may be posted for authorized users to search

   a) A freight-access environment associated with a particular broker or brokerage network
   b) A state licensing system
   c) A replacement for FMCSA authority verification
   d) A driver's ELD

   a) Moved under a one-time or short-term market transaction rather than an ongoing committed lane agreement
   b) Always cheaper than contract freight
   c) Always posted publicly
   d) Exempt from carrier screening

   a) Freight that can never change rates
   b) An ongoing or prearranged transportation relationship/lane rather than a one-off spot transaction
   c) Freight that does not require appointments
   d) Freight that may be accepted without checking carrier fit

   a) It affects how far the truck must travel before pickup and can change whether the opportunity is practical

6. FOUND means:
   a) The load is definitely still open and booked
   b) A potentially relevant opportunity has been located
   c) The broker has passed every fraud check
   d) The rate has been negotiated

   a) The posting is less than 10 minutes old
   b) Availability has been directly confirmed through an appropriate source/channel
   c) The load appeared on two boards

## Part F — Rate Confirmation, Verification, Credit & Timing Judgment

   a) Sign it — minor scheduling differences don't matter once the document arrives

   c) Assume the rate confirmation is correct and the verbal conversation was wrong, without contacting the broker

28. A broker's MC number checks out as active. An email arrives from an address the dispatcher hasn't corresponded with before, requesting a change to remittance instructions. What is the correct next step?
   a) Proceed — an active MC number is sufficient to trust the email
   b) Verify the request through a previously trusted, established contact channel before acting on it
   c) Reply directly to the new email address to confirm

   a) The broker has been proven to have committed fraud
   b) A private financial-risk decision by the factor — not a legal or safety determination about the broker

   a) Calculate whether 700 miles is achievable using only distance and elapsed time, and reject automatically if it exceeds 11 hours of driving
   b) Verify the driver's actual current hours, duty status, and route/appointment feasibility before committing to the schedule
