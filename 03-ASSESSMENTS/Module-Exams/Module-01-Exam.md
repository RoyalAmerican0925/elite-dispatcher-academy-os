## Part A — Roles: Broker, Carrier, Dispatcher, Driver

1. A company that arranges transportation between a shipper and a carrier, without ever taking possession of the freight itself, is functioning as a:
   a) Motor carrier
   b) Freight broker
   c) Consignee
   d) Factoring company

2. Which of the following best describes the carrier-side dispatch model taught in this academy?
   a) The dispatcher works on behalf of the shipper, finding carriers to move freight
   b) The dispatcher works on behalf of the motor carrier, sourcing and managing loads for that carrier
   c) The dispatcher works on behalf of the receiving facility
   d) The dispatcher is legally the same role as a broker

3. Who physically transports the freight in a standard truckload shipment?
   a) The freight broker
   b) The shipper
   c) The motor carrier, through its driver
   d) The factoring company

4. A company's job duties in this academy's dispatch model would NOT include:
   a) Sourcing loads for a represented carrier
   b) Physically operating the truck
   c) Communicating with brokers about load details
   d) Tracking a driver's schedule and equipment

The carrier's driver picks up the freight at the Cascade Millwork dock and delivers it to the distribution center's receiving dock in Reno.

    a) Cascade Millwork

    c) The distribution center in Reno
    d) The dispatcher

    a) Cascade Millwork
    b) The broker

    b) The carrier's MC (or equivalent operating authority) number
