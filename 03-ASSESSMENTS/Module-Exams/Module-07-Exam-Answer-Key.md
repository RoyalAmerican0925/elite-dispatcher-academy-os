## Item 28 — Applied Reasoning Rubric

Award full credit when the response clearly includes all of the following:

1. Calculate loaded RPM as an initial metric, but do not stop there.
2. Identify origin deadhead and any relevant destination/repositioning deadhead.
3. Calculate total trip miles and effective RPM.
4. Estimate fuel using total miles, truck MPG, and an applicable current or exercise-supplied fuel price.
5. Include known trip-specific costs/fees such as tolls and contractually applicable dispatcher/factoring/quick-pay fees.
6. Verify accessorial eligibility and documentation rather than assuming future detention, TONU, layover, lumper reimbursement, or extra-stop revenue.
7. If calculating contribution margin, identify exactly which costs are included and do not call the result net profit.
8. Consider next-load positioning as a planning factor.
9. Present assumptions and calculations clearly to the carrier.
10. State that the carrier makes the final accept/decline decision.

### Suggested Scoring

- **Full credit:** 9–10 elements present, with correct terminology and no unsupported revenue/profit claims.
- **Partial credit:** 6–8 elements present, with no critical conceptual error.
- **Minimal credit:** 3–5 elements present or significant omissions.
- **No credit:** Fewer than 3 elements, or response treats unearned accessorial revenue as guaranteed or labels a partial calculation as net profit.

## Passing Standard

The student must score at least **26 of 28** and must answer **Critical Items 24 and 26 correctly**. A wrong answer on either critical item means the exam is not passed even if the numerical score is otherwise 90% or higher.

## Instructor Publication Controls

- Do not add a fixed detention threshold, TONU amount, dispatcher-fee percentage, factoring range, quick-pay range, or carrier break-even figure as a universal rule.
- Keep "revenue," "cash flow," "contribution margin," and "profit" distinct.
