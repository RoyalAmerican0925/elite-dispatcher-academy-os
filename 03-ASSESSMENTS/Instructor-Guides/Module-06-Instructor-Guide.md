# Elite Dispatcher Academy — Module 06 Instructor Guide

**Module:** 06 — Broker Verification & Fraud Prevention  
**Instructional status:** PREMIUM BUILD — RELEASE QA SEPARATE  
**Exam:** 28 items; pass 26/28 plus Critical Items 25 and 27  
**Practical:** Broker Verification Case Desk; pass 24/30 plus no critical failure  
**Simulation:** SIM-006 — Broker Verification & Fraud Prevention Desk

## 1. Employment outcome
Learner can receive a suspicious or incomplete broker transaction, isolate claims, verify the entity, independently authenticate the contact, cross-check load/documents, protect the payment path, preserve evidence, classify risk, and issue a defensible proceed/verify/hold/escalate/decline decision without unsupported accusations.

## 2. Memory model and desk model
Student memory model:
**VERIFY THE ENTITY → VERIFY THE CONTACT → VERIFY THE LOAD → VERIFY THE PAYMENT PATH**

Operational desk model:
**RECEIVE → ISOLATE CLAIMS → VERIFY ENTITY → AUTHENTICATE CONTACT → CROSS-CHECK LOAD/DOCUMENTS → VERIFY PAYMENT PATH → RECORD EVIDENCE → CLASSIFY RISK → PROCEED / VERIFY / HOLD / ESCALATE / DECLINE**

Do not collapse entity and contact verification.

## 3. Instructor preparation
- Reverify current FMCSA systems and date-sensitive broker financial-responsibility procedures before delivery.
- Prepare fictional records only; never use live credentials/private banking data in class.
- Ensure seven Module 06 tools are available.
- Keep answer/scoring controls instructor-only.
- Distinguish federal requirements, current agency procedure, carrier policy, factor/credit policy, cybersecurity practice and Academy control language.

## 4. Lesson facilitation
### Entity
Teach what an authoritative record proves and its limits. Active authority does not authenticate an email, caller, load, document or bank account.

### Contact
Trusted-channel verification must be independent of the questionable message. If the channel is the thing in doubt, it cannot authenticate itself.

### Load/document
Require field-level cross-checking. Professional appearance and logos are not authentication. A mismatch is a verification task; do not silently repair it.

### Payment path
Unexpected payment/remittance/factoring changes are HOLD/VERIFY events. Urgency does not lower the verification standard.

### Financial security
Teach the $75,000 BMC-84/BMC-85 framework and keep distinct regulatory clocks distinct. Do not present financial security as a transaction-specific payment guarantee.

### Factor/credit/DNU
These may control carrier behavior under policy. They are not automatically fraud findings or federal enforcement orders.

### Duplicate freight / re-brokering
Similar postings are evidence to investigate, not automatic proof of unauthorized re-brokering. Require neutral language until evidence supports more.

### Cyber-enabled impersonation
Explain look-alike domains, display-name spoofing, compromised legitimate accounts, thread hijacking and social-pressure tactics. Do not teach simplistic rules such as “free email = fraud.”

## 5. Tool facilitation
1. Broker Verification Evidence Record — source/evidence/currentness discipline.
2. Entity vs Contact Authentication Worksheet — prevents the central identity error.
3. Trusted Callback Verification Record — independently authenticates material claims.
4. Load & Document Cross-Check Sheet — exposes transaction/document mismatches.
5. Payment-Instruction Change Verification Record — protects remittance changes.
6. Risk Classification & Decision Record — converts evidence into operational action.
7. Verification Evidence & Escalation Packet — makes escalation a controlled handoff.

Require students to explain what evidence does **not** prove.

## 6. Professional language
Preferred:
- “The entity is verified; the incoming contact is not yet authenticated.”
- “Hold pending independent verification.”
- “The supplied document contains a material mismatch requiring correction/confirmation.”
- “Unable to proceed under carrier policy.”
- “The duplicate posting is a verification indicator, not proof of double brokering.”

Reject unsupported labels such as “scammer” or “confirmed fraud” when evidence is inconclusive.

## 7. Knowledge Check / Scenario Lab
Use diagnostically. Require status + controlling evidence + next action. A correct label with incorrect reasoning is not mastery.

The 12-scenario lab is guided practice, not the secure graded practical.

## 8. Exam administration
Passing standard: **26/28 (90%) and Critical Items 25 and 27 correct.**

- Critical 25: unverified payment redirection.
- Critical 27: unsupported fraud conclusion.

Remediate the failed mental model before retest. Use alternate items/fact patterns.

## 9. Practical administration
Use `Module-06-Practical.md` and its dedicated scoring guide.

Six critical-failure families:
1. acts on unverified/contradicted payment redirection;
2. active entity treated as proof of contact authorization;
3. proceeds through confirmed impersonation/contact contradiction;
4. knowingly books in a supplied alternate case with required authority verified inactive;
5. declares fraud/double brokering solely from inconclusive indicators;
6. bypasses material unresolved identity/document/payment mismatch.

Pass: **24/30 plus no critical failure.** Preserve first-attempt evidence.

## 10. Diagnostic remediation
| Error | Failed model | Required remediation evidence |
|---|---|---|
| active MC authenticates caller | entity = contact | complete entity/contact worksheet and trusted callback on alternate case |
| suspicious email verifies itself | channel = trusted channel | construct independent callback source |
| logo/document appearance trusted | appearance = authenticity | perform field-level cross-check |
| urgency bypasses controls | speed > verification | apply HOLD/VERIFY under timed alternate scenario |
| factor decline called fraud | private risk = fraud finding | classify evidence and carrier-policy consequence separately |
| duplicate posting called double brokering | indicator = proof | state verification questions and neutral status |
| payment change accepted in-thread | continuity = authentication | independent payment-change verification |

## 11. SIM-006 alignment contract
SIM-006 must be a progressive verification desk, not a quiz. It must include:
- initial apparently plausible broker/load offer;
- authoritative entity evidence;
- contact/channel inconsistency;
- trusted callback decision;
- document mismatch;
- financial-security/current-status interpretation;
- factor/credit signal;
- duplicate/re-brokering ambiguity;
- payment-change event;
- urgency/social-pressure event;
- later evidence that changes the case state;
- evidence preservation and escalation handoff.

Record separately: first-attempt decision, correctness, critical-failure state, remediation, corrected decision, final mastery, competency-family results, overall status and instructor-review state.

SIM-006 critical controls must align to the practical rather than invent contradictory standards.

## 12. Source/currentness control
Before publication and periodically thereafter, reverify current federal systems/procedures. Never teach platform interfaces, vendor risk scores, factor decisions or carrier DNU lists as federal law.
