## 1. Employment Outcome
By the end of Module 05, the learner must be able to run a disciplined freight-sourcing desk: translate a carrier/driver/equipment profile into a search strategy, locate opportunities, distinguish FOUND from VERIFIED AVAILABLE, screen incomplete or conflicting postings, compare viable candidates, separate entity verification from contact authentication, detect and route red flags without unsupported accusations, review a supplied rate-confirmation summary for discrepancies, and document a defensible next action.

The learner has not demonstrated competency merely by finding a load on a board. The occupational result is a screened, verified, documented opportunity that can safely move to the next workflow stage.

## 2. Prerequisite Integration

## 3. Core Sourcing Model
Teach the working sequence:

**PROFILE → SEARCH → FIND → SCREEN → VERIFY AVAILABILITY → FIRST-PASS VERIFY → COMPARE → SHORTLIST → DOCUMENT → ROUTE FOR NEXT-STAGE DECISION**

Use these statuses precisely:
- **FOUND:** potentially relevant opportunity located; availability not implied.
- **VERIFIED AVAILABLE:** current availability confirmed through an appropriate channel.
- **GOOD CANDIDATE:** no supplied sourcing-stage conflict prevents routing forward.
- **BAD FIT / REJECT:** a supplied fact conflicts with carrier, driver, equipment, lane, qualification or policy requirements.
- **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY:** material information is absent, stale, ambiguous or conflicting.
- **STOP / ESCALATE:** the learner encounters a scope, authentication, qualification, fraud-risk or unresolved-document issue that should not be bypassed.

Never allow `FOUND = BOOKABLE` or `POSTED = VERIFIED`.

## 5. Lesson Delivery Intelligence

### A. Search begins with the truck, not the board
Require a usable search profile before opening a board: current truck location, equipment, usable capability, driver availability, carrier lane/preferences, prohibited freight, home-time constraint, appointment constraints and known qualification limits.

**Failure pattern:** searching broadly and trying to force the truck into an attractive posting afterward.

### B. Search radius and deadhead
Teach radius as a search-control variable, not a magic number. A wider radius increases opportunity count but can also increase unpaid repositioning, schedule pressure and operational risk.

Deadhead matters during sourcing, but deadhead alone does not prove profitability. Module 07 owns deeper rate mathematics.

### C. Posting age and availability
Posting age is evidence, not a verdict. A fresh post can already be gone; an older post can remain open.

Require explicit separation of:
1. when the opportunity was observed;
2. whether current availability was verified;
3. when/how verification occurred.

### D. Incomplete information
Train the learner to convert missing fields into questions. Commodity, weight, exact facilities, pickup/delivery windows, equipment requirements, special handling and current availability can materially change the decision.

Professional response: **“The opportunity is not yet decision-ready; these facts require verification.”**

### E. Equipment and qualification fit
Import Module 03 reasoning. A board's shorthand equipment label is not proof that the exact truck, trailer and driver fit the load. A stated endorsement conflict is a stop condition for the supplied assignment.

### F. Carrier policy and driver constraints
Lane preference, NYC prohibition, home-time requirements and other carrier choices are operational constraints when supplied by the carrier. Do not relabel them as federal law.

### G. Duplicate-looking postings
Teach three separate propositions:
1. duplicate/similar posts can be a red flag;
2. a red flag creates a verification task;
3. a red flag alone does not prove double brokering or fraud.

Require neutral language until evidence supports a stronger conclusion.

### H. Entity verification versus contact authentication
This distinction is mandatory.

Confirming that a brokerage exists or has current authority does **not** authenticate a particular caller, email, domain, phone number, payment instruction or transaction instruction.

Use the rule:
**VERIFY THE ENTITY → VERIFY THE CONTACT → VERIFY THE TRANSACTION FACTS.**

### I. Broker communication
Students should be able to conduct a concise sourcing call that confirms availability and material facts without pretending to have authority they do not possess.

Require truthful role identification. The learner should record facts, not rely on memory.

### J. Rate-confirmation discrepancy control
Module 05 introduces operational document comparison; it does not replace later booking/documentation depth.

Require field-by-field comparison of the written document against confirmed terms. A material mismatch triggers:
**STOP → IDENTIFY DISCREPANCY → VERIFY → OBTAIN CORRECTED/CONFIRMED TERMS → CONFIRM SIGNING AUTHORITY → PROCEED ONLY WHEN RESOLVED.**

Never teach the learner to silently rewrite a document or assume that reviewing it creates signing authority.

### K. Timing and HOS feasibility
A tight schedule is a verification event. Distance divided by an assumed speed is not a legal HOS determination. Route forward only after actual driver hours/duty status and appointment feasibility are checked through the appropriate workflow.

### L. Commercial credit/factoring information
A factor's refusal to purchase an invoice is a private commercial-risk signal, not proof of fraud, loss of authority or a federal prohibition. Keep financial-security/identity fraud depth in Module 06.

### M. Boundary with brokerage activity
Reinforce Module 02. General shipper solicitation followed by discretionary allocation of freight among unrelated carriers can create a significant brokerage/allocation-of-traffic concern. The correct training behavior is STOP / ESCALATE, not invent a workaround.

## 6. Student Tool Facilitation

### Load Search Worksheet
Purpose: convert the carrier/truck profile into explicit search parameters. Reject vague entries such as “good lanes.”

### Load Search Activity Log
Purpose: preserve what was searched, when, what was found, and what happened next. It supports repeatability and prevents memory-based sourcing.

### Load Screening Checklist
Purpose: force a candidate through fit/currentness/missing-information controls before it is shortlisted.

### Load Comparison Worksheet
Purpose: compare candidates on operational facts rather than headline rate alone. Do not let students turn it into a Module 07 profitability worksheet.

### Broker Call Sheet
Purpose: structure a concise verification conversation and record the broker's supplied facts.

### Broker Verification Worksheet
Purpose: separate authoritative entity facts from contact/transaction authentication.

### Freight Fraud Red Flag Checklist
Purpose: recognize conditions requiring verification. It is not a fraud-conviction checklist.

### Rate Confirmation Review Checklist
Purpose: compare written terms with confirmed terms and stop on unresolved material discrepancies.

### STOP-VERIFY-ESCALATE Load Decision Tool
Purpose: make uncertainty actionable rather than allowing guessing or premature booking.

## 7. Scenario Facilitation Protocol
For every scenario require the learner to state:
1. what is known;
2. what is missing or conflicting;
3. whether availability is actually verified;
4. carrier/driver/equipment constraints;
5. entity/contact verification state where applicable;
6. decision classification;
7. next verification action;
8. professional documentation language.

Do not accept the correct label without the controlling reason.

## 8. Professional Language Coaching
Preferred:
- “The load is found but availability is not yet verified.”
- “The posting is incomplete; verify commodity, appointment and facility details before routing forward.”
- “The entity is verified; the new contact is not yet authenticated.”
- “The duplicate postings are a verification flag, not proof of double brokering.”
- “The written rate does not match the confirmed call terms; hold processing pending resolution.”
- “This request presents a brokerage/scope concern and requires escalation.”

Avoid:
- “It was posted, so it is available.”
- “The MC is active, so the email is legitimate.”
- “Two posts means double brokering.”
- “The factor rejected them, so they are fraudulent.”
- “I reviewed the rate con, so I can sign it.”

## 9. Assessment Administration

### Knowledge Check
Use diagnostically before the graded exam. Route misses back to the failed mental model rather than merely revealing the answer.

### Exam
Current controlled standard: **24/30 (80%) and Critical Item 24 correct.**

Critical Item 24 tests whether the learner recognizes the brokerage/allocation-of-traffic boundary when a dispatcher receives freight from a shipper first and then chooses among unrelated represented carriers without broker authority.

### Practical — Morning Load Board Shortlist
Current standard: **24/30 and no critical failure.**

Controlled critical failures:
1. treating Candidate E as viable despite the supplied required-endorsement mismatch;
2. treating shipper-first discretionary allocation among unrelated carriers as ordinary carrier-side sourcing;
3. declaring duplicate-looking postings proven fraud/double brokering without verification;
4. treating verified brokerage entity status as authentication of the new contact;
5. processing/signing the supplied mismatched rate confirmation as though it matches;
6. assuming signing authority merely because the dispatcher reviewed the document.

Do not invent hidden critical failures during grading.

## 10. Diagnostic Remediation
| Error | Failed mental model | Remediation evidence |
|---|---|---|
| FOUND treated as available | discovery = verification | learner records separate found/verified timestamps and channel |
| headline rate drives shortlist | rate = complete decision | learner screens fit, deadhead, schedule, missing facts and currentness first |
| stale post automatically rejected | age = availability | learner states verification question/action |
| duplicate post called fraud | indicator = proof | learner writes neutral verification note and next step |
| active entity authenticates email | entity = contact | learner demonstrates independently obtained trusted-channel verification |
| mismatch rate con processed | document receipt = agreement | learner performs field comparison and STOP/VERIFY workflow |
| factor decline called illegal/fraud | private risk = legal finding | learner correctly classifies evidence type |
| shipper-first allocation accepted | dispatcher role = broker discretion | learner identifies boundary and escalates |

Reassessment must use an alternate fact pattern.
