# Module 09 Instructor Guide
## Booking Loads, Rate Confirmations, and Dispatch Documentation

**Control status:** PREMIUM BUILD — INSTRUCTOR CONTROL  
**Student outcome:** Convert an M08 negotiation handoff into a traceable carrier-side booking record and controlled dispatch-document handoff without exceeding supplied authority or absorbing later-module work.

## 1. Instructor mission
Do not teach M09 as "get the rate con and send it to the driver." The occupational skill is evidence-controlled booking. Students must learn to detect when the written document does not match the negotiated understanding, when authority is insufficient, when a document version is unsafe, and when the correct professional action is HOLD/CORRECT/ESCALATE rather than pretending the load is ready.

## 2. Required prerequisite check
Before M09 graded work, verify the learner can:
- identify carrier-side dispatcher authority boundaries from M02;
- use M06 verification discipline without treating an indicator as proof;
- interpret M07 rate/economic evidence;
- distinguish M08 negotiation outcome from formal booking completion.

If those foundations are materially weak, remediate the prerequisite rather than lowering M09 evidence standards.

## 3. Controlled M09 workflow
**NEGOTIATION HANDOFF → VERIFY AGREEMENT → RECEIVE DOCUMENT → AUTHENTICATE / IDENTIFY → READ ALL MATERIAL TERMS → RECONCILE VERBAL vs WRITTEN → CLASSIFY DISCREPANCIES → CORRECT / ESCALATE → CONFIRM AUTHORITY → ACCEPT / SIGN WITHIN AUTHORITY → BUILD BOOKING RECORD → BUILD DISPATCH PACKET → ISSUE CONTROLLED DRIVER HANDOFF → RECORD COMPETENCY**

Require students to name the current stage when explaining a decision.

## 4. Progressive teaching sequence
### Demonstration 1 — Negotiation-to-booking handoff
Model how to classify M08 evidence into AGREED, CONDITIONAL, UNRESOLVED and UNVERIFIED. Show why an agreement in principle is not the same as a controlled booking.

### Demonstration 2 — Rate-confirmation extraction
Use a fictional full confirmation. Extract identity, rate, dates/times, locations, equipment, commodity/weight when supplied, accessorial/operating/document terms, source, version and acceptance/signature fields. Do not let students scan only the rate.

### Demonstration 3 — Verbal-vs-written reconciliation
Put the M08 handoff and RC side by side. Compare field by field. Require MATCH / MISMATCH-MATERIAL / MISMATCH-NONMATERIAL / UNKNOWN-MISSING / LATER-WORKFLOW.

### Demonstration 4 — Correction request
Model a professional correction record: identify the exact conflicting term, cite the supplied evidence, request the specific correction/clarification, preserve the original version, and hold the booking state until resolved.

### Demonstration 5 — Authority stop
Give a case where every commercial term matches but supplied dispatcher authority does not permit signature/acceptance. Correct outcome is escalation, not "everything else looks good."

### Demonstration 6 — Version-control exclusion
Show v1, v2 and an unrelated/obsolete document. Require source/version/current-status logging and explicit exclusion from the dispatch packet.

### Demonstration 7 — Booking closeout
Build the booking record, current packet, driver-facing handoff, final status and M10 transfer. Emphasize that `BOOKED / DISPATCH PACKET READY` is an M09 state—not proof of HOS feasibility, trip-plan completion, monitoring, or billing.

## 5. Nine tool coaching rules
1. **Handoff Sheet:** no unsupported facts; every material item has source/status.
2. **Extraction Worksheet:** full-document read; blank is UNKNOWN/MISSING, not assumed zero/no.
3. **Reconciliation Matrix:** compare evidence, not memory.
4. **Discrepancy Log:** one issue per traceable record where practical; record requested and actual resolution.
5. **Authority Control:** distinguish authority to negotiate, accept, sign, transmit, and escalate.
6. **Version & Integrity Log:** preserve history; never silently replace evidence.
7. **Booking Record Builder:** separate source facts, verified facts, unresolved facts, decisions and approvals.
8. **Dispatch Packet Checklist:** include current verified documents; exclude superseded/wrong/uncontrolled material.
9. **Decision Record:** final state must follow evidence; BOOKED/READY is unavailable with an unresolved required M09 control.

## 6. Assessment controls
### Knowledge Check
20 items. Use diagnostically. Incorrect answers should identify which decision rule needs reteaching.

### Applied Lab
The learner must reconcile a multi-document case. Score reasoning and work-product quality, not merely the final status.

### Final Exam
30 items; passing score 27/30. Critical items are mapped in the answer key only to the six controlled CF families. A raw passing score does not erase a triggered critical condition.

### Practical
10 dimensions × 0–3 = 30. Passing score 24/30 plus zero unresolved critical failures. Use `Booking-Control-Desk-Practical-Scoring-and-Remediation-Control.md` as the controlling rubric.

## 7. Exactly six critical-failure families
- **M09-CF-01:** unauthorized carrier commitment.
- **M09-CF-02:** material rate-confirmation acceptance error.
- **M09-CF-03:** unresolved verbal/written discrepancy represented as resolved.
- **M09-CF-04:** wrong/obsolete/unverified booking document used for dispatch.
- **M09-CF-05:** document fabrication/material alteration.
- **M09-CF-06:** false booking completion.

Do not create additional critical families ad hoc. Score other deficiencies through the normal rubric.

## 8. Critical remediation protocol
When a CF triggers:
1. preserve first-attempt artifact and trigger evidence;
2. identify the competency and unsafe reasoning;
3. coach the principle without handing over the reassessment answer;
4. use a materially different scenario for the same competency;
5. record reassessment response/result separately;
6. if passed, mark **RESOLVED BY REASSESSMENT** while retaining historical occurrence.

Same-screen correction cannot resolve a CF.

## 9. Common misconceptions and instructor responses
**"The rate matches, so the rate con is fine."**  
Response: require full material-term extraction. Money is one field.

**"Written always beats verbal."**  
Response: M09 does not invent a universal evidence rule. Reconcile supplied evidence, authority and current controlling documentation; correct conflicts before acting.

**"The dispatcher can sign because the carrier hired them."**  
Response: require the supplied authority record. Relationship does not prove unlimited acceptance/signature authority.

**"A newer timestamp means the document is automatically valid."**  
Response: establish source, identity, version relationship and controlling status.

**"I can edit the PDF so it matches what was agreed."**  
Response: prohibited. Obtain corrected source documentation or document authorized resolution; preserve evidence history.

**"Booked means ready to drive."**  
Response: M09 booking controls can close while M10/M11 work remains ahead.

## 10. Evidence-quality standard
Award top-level performance only when the learner:
- identifies the controlling evidence;
- states what is known vs unresolved;
- uses the correct tool/work product;
- makes an authority-safe action;
- preserves document history;
- records a defensible final state and handoff.

A lucky answer without evidence-supported reasoning is not job-ready evidence.

## 11. Scope boundaries
Do not grade detailed routing/fuel/appointment planning as M09; that is M10. Do not grade detailed HOS/ELD feasibility as M09; that is M11. Active monitoring belongs to M12; deeper exceptions/claims to M13; billing/closeout to M14. M09 may identify and hand off those issues.

## 12. Currentness and legal-claim discipline
- Treat accessorial amounts, tracking/check-call requirements, detention/TONU conditions, cancellation terms, signature methods and payment terms as agreement-specific unless a current authoritative rule controls the point.
- Treat platform/vendor workflows as date-sensitive.
- Separate Academy workflow controls from legal mandates.
- Federal legal/regulatory statements require primary-source verification before commercial release.
- Do not tell students a particular vendor, signature platform, or form is universally required unless current authority actually says so.

## 13. Instructor completion record
Record separately:
- first-attempt exam/practical/SIM evidence;
- corrected mastery;
- critical-family history;
- reassessment history;
- unresolved instructor review state;
- final module result.

## 14. Build/release discipline
M09 may reach **BUILD COMPLETE — RELEASE QA DEFERRED** only after the curriculum, tools, assessments, instructor controls, SIM-009 package/runtime, source registers and independent premium QA pass fresh verification. Do not label the module `PUBLICATION_READY` until release-layer QA is complete.
