# Module 06 Practical — Broker Verification Case Desk

**Assessment type:** Integrated graded occupational practical  
**Passing standard:** 24/30 (80%) **and no critical failure**  
**Purpose:** Demonstrate evidence-based broker/contact/load/document/payment verification from intake through operational disposition.

## Student instructions
You are the carrier-side dispatcher. Use the Module 06 tools. Do not invent missing facts. Separate what each source proves. Your job is not to prove fraud; your job is to determine whether the transaction is sufficiently verified to proceed and document the decision.

## Fictional case packet — Load BV-6206

**Carrier profile:** Northstar Ridge Transport LLC operates a 53-foot dry van. Carrier policy requires current broker authority, independently authenticated contacts for material changes, factor approval where factoring is used, and independent verification of payment changes.

**Incoming load offer:**
- Presented broker: Meridian Gate Logistics LLC
- Presented MC: MC-620600
- Contact: “Alex Mercer,” alex.mercer@meridiangate-logistics.example
- Origin: Columbus, OH
- Destination: Nashville, TN
- Commodity: packaged household goods
- Weight: 31,500 lb
- Offered linehaul: $1,450

**Authoritative entity evidence supplied for this exercise:**
- Current official lookup shows Meridian Gate Logistics LLC, MC-620600, required broker authority ACTIVE.
- Financial-responsibility record shows an effective BMC-84 at the time of the exercise.
- Official record does not identify or authenticate Alex Mercer.

**Established carrier relationship record:**
- Previously verified corporate domain: `meridiangatelogistics.example` (no hyphen)
- Previously verified main number: 555-010-6206
- Established accounting/remittance instruction: Factor A / account ending 1184

**Independent callback event:**
When the student calls the supplied previously verified main number, the brokerage's operations desk confirms the load number exists, but states that **Alex Mercer is not an employee or authorized agent** and that the company did not send the incoming offer from the hyphenated domain.

**Document event:**
A rate-confirmation-looking PDF from the incoming contact uses Meridian Gate branding and MC-620600, but changes the pickup reference number and instructs the carrier to send a $350 “expedite deposit” to a new account before dispatch.

**Payment event:**
The same contact says the old factoring information is obsolete and directs all transaction funds to a new account ending 7729. The contact says there is no time for another callback.

**Factor/credit evidence:**
Carrier's factor reports that it currently accepts invoices from the legitimate Meridian Gate entity. This is a commercial credit decision; it does not authenticate the incoming contact.

**Duplicate-posting evidence:**
A similar Columbus-to-Nashville load appears under another active broker. No evidence in the packet establishes whether the freight is duplicated, co-brokered, reposted, or unauthorized re-brokering.

## Tasks

### Task 1 — Isolate claims (3 points)
List at least five material claims in the incoming offer that require verification and identify which verification layer each belongs to: entity, contact, load/document, or payment path.

### Task 2 — Entity verification (4 points)
Record what the official entity evidence proves and at least two things it does **not** prove.

### Task 3 — Contact authentication (5 points)
Use the Entity-vs-Contact and Trusted Callback records. State the result of the independent callback and the operational consequence.

### Task 4 — Load/document cross-check (4 points)
Identify the material document/transaction mismatches and state what must happen before any document is relied on.

### Task 5 — Payment-path control (5 points)
Complete the Payment-Instruction Change Verification Record. State whether the new account/deposit instruction may be acted upon and why.

### Task 6 — Risk-signal interpretation (3 points)
Explain separately what the factor approval and duplicate posting do and do not establish.

### Task 7 — Final disposition and escalation packet (6 points)
Assign the least accusatory operational status justified by the evidence. Complete a concise escalation packet showing verified facts, contradicted claims, what is stopped, and the decision requested/required.
