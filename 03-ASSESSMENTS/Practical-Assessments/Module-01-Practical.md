## Scenario — Blue River Foods

Blue River Foods manufactures shelf-stable food products in Omaha, Nebraska. It sells a truckload to a regional grocery distribution center in Des Moines, Iowa. Blue River contacts Prairie Freight Brokers to arrange transportation. Prairie Freight Brokers tenders the load to North Star Transport, an authorized motor carrier. North Star's dispatcher coordinates the load for North Star, and North Star's driver physically moves the shipment. After delivery, North Star sends its invoice and POD to its factoring company for accelerated payment.

North Star has a USDOT number and a separate MC operating-authority number.

## Student Tasks

1. Identify the **shipper**, **broker**, **motor carrier**, **dispatcher**, **driver**, **consignee/receiver**, and **factoring company role** in the scenario.
2. Explain who physically transports the freight and who merely arranges transportation.
3. Explain the difference between North Star's USDOT number and MC operating authority.
4. Explain why the dispatcher does not become the broker merely by communicating with Prairie Freight Brokers.
5. Describe the factoring company's function after delivery.
6. State whether the movement is interstate or intrastate based on the supplied origin/destination facts and explain why.
7. Draw or write the freight/document flow in sequence from shipper → arrangement → carrier/driver → receiver → invoice/factoring.
8. Identify one fact that would require verification rather than assumption if it were missing from a real load record.

## Required Deliverable

Submit one completed role map plus written reasoning for Tasks 2–8.
