## Carrier Profile

Silver Route Transport:

- 53-foot dry van
- truck currently in Fort Worth, Texas
- prefers Texas/Oklahoma/Arkansas lanes
- no hazmat
- carrier policy: no NYC deliveries
- driver needs to be back in Dallas by Saturday evening

## Load Candidates

### Candidate A
Fort Worth → Tulsa; 28,000 lb packaged consumer goods; dry van; pickup Thursday 14:00–16:00; delivery Friday 08:00–12:00; availability confirmed 10 minutes ago.

### Candidate B
Dallas → Bronx; dry van; 20,000 lb; pickup/delivery details complete; currently available.

### Candidate C
Arlington → Little Rock; "van load, 40k, good money"; no commodity, pickup time, delivery time, or exact facility information.

### Candidate D
Houston → Oklahoma City; dry van; 25,000 lb; pickup Friday 18:00; delivery Saturday 10:00; availability has not been confirmed since the posting appeared several hours ago.

### Candidate E
Fort Worth → Shreveport; placarded hazmat requiring endorsement the current driver does not have.

### Candidate F
Two nearly identical Fort Worth → Austin postings appear under two different brokers. Both show active authority, but the relationship between the postings and current availability are not yet verified.

## Student Tasks

1. Mark each candidate **FOUND** first, then separately indicate whether it is **VERIFIED AVAILABLE**.
2. Classify each as **GOOD CANDIDATE**, **BAD FIT / REJECT**, or **NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY**.
3. Identify the missing-information questions for Candidates C, D, and F.
4. Identify carrier-policy or driver-profile conflicts.
5. Create a ranked shortlist of the best candidates to route to the next workflow stage and explain the ranking.
6. Explain why deadhead is relevant during sourcing but does not, by itself, establish profitability.
7. Explain why duplicate-looking postings should trigger verification rather than an automatic accusation of double brokering.
8. If a shipper bypasses the load board and asks you to accept its freight first and choose among your unrelated carrier clients later, state the Module 02 boundary concern and the correct next step.

## Critical-Failure Criteria

Any of the following is a critical failure:

- treating Candidate E as a viable load for the current driver despite the stated endorsement mismatch;
- treating general shipper solicitation plus discretionary carrier allocation as ordinary safe carrier-side sourcing;
- declaring duplicate-looking Candidate F postings to be proven fraud/double brokering without verification.

## Passing Rule

At least 80% of rubric points **and** no critical failure.
