## Verification Event 1 — Entity vs. Contact

You route Candidate A forward. The brokerage company name and active authority are confirmed in an appropriate current source. A person calling himself **Marcus** then emails from a new address that has not previously been used by the brokerage and tells you to continue the load through him.

Complete the Module 05 Broker Verification Worksheet and answer:

1. What has been verified about the **entity**?
2. What has **not** yet been verified about the **contact**?
3. Does confirming the brokerage entity prove that Marcus, his email address, or his instructions are authorized? Explain.
4. State the next safe action before relying on the new contact.

**Required decision:** treat entity verification and contact verification as separate controls. A legitimate entity record does not, by itself, authenticate a person, phone number, email address, domain, payment instruction, or transaction instruction.

## Verification Event 2 — Rate Confirmation Discrepancy Review

After the Candidate A call, the following terms were confirmed in your call notes:

- Carrier: Silver Route Transport
- Origin: Fort Worth, TX
- Destination: Tulsa, OK
- Pickup: Thursday 14:00–16:00
- Delivery: Friday 08:00–12:00
- Equipment: 53-foot dry van
- Commodity: packaged consumer goods
- Weight: 28,000 lb
- Agreed linehaul rate for this exercise: **$1,150**
- No extra deduction or tracking fee was discussed

The broker then sends a fictional training rate-confirmation summary showing:

- Carrier: Silver Route Transport
- Fort Worth, TX → Tulsa, OK
- Pickup: Thursday 14:00–16:00
- Delivery: Friday 08:00–12:00
- Equipment: 53-foot dry van
- Commodity: packaged consumer goods
- Weight: 28,000 lb
- Rate: **$1,050**
- Additional term: **$75 tracking-compliance deduction if the tracking link is not activated within 30 minutes**

Using the Module 05 Rate Confirmation Review Checklist:

1. Compare the written summary against the confirmed call information.
2. Identify every material discrepancy supplied in this exercise.
3. Mark the appropriate decision: **MATCH**, **DISCREPANCY**, or **NOT SHOWN** for the relevant fields.
4. State whether the dispatcher should sign/process the document as-is.
5. State the correct next action.
6. Explain why a dispatcher should not silently change the written document, ignore the discrepancy, or independently decide the document's ultimate legal effect.
7. Confirm whether the dispatcher actually has carrier authorization to sign/process the document before taking any signing action.

**Required decision:** STOP on the discrepancy, do not sign/process it as though it matches, verify the conflicting rate and added deduction directly with the broker, obtain corrected/confirmed terms through the carrier's authorized workflow, and remain within the dispatcher's actual signing authority.

## Critical-Failure Criteria

Any of the following is a critical failure:

- treating Candidate E as a viable load for the current driver despite the stated endorsement mismatch;
- treating general shipper solicitation plus discretionary carrier allocation as ordinary safe carrier-side sourcing;
- declaring duplicate-looking Candidate F postings to be proven fraud/double brokering without verification;
- treating a verified brokerage entity as proof that the new/unverified contact is authorized;
- signing or processing the fictional rate-confirmation summary as though it matches the confirmed call information despite the stated rate/deduction discrepancies;
- assuming signing authority merely because the dispatcher reviewed the document.

## Passing Rule

At least 80% of rubric points **and** no critical failure.
