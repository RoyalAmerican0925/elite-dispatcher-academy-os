## Scenario — Five Opportunities

You operate a carrier-side dispatch service. Review each opportunity separately.

### Opportunity A
You have a continuing agreement with one dry-van carrier. A broker offers a load that matches that carrier's equipment and lane. You present the opportunity only to that carrier.

### Opportunity B
A shipper emails you three loads and asks you to choose which carriers from your eight-client network should receive each load. Several clients have overlapping equipment and lanes.

### Opportunity C
You represent two carriers with non-overlapping operations: one reefer carrier in the Northwest and one dry-van carrier in the Southeast. You source only within each carrier's standing territory/equipment limits.

### Opportunity D
Carrier A rejects a shipper-direct opportunity. Without returning the opportunity to the shipper, you redirect it to Carrier B because Carrier B will pay a higher dispatch fee.

### Opportunity E
A business contract labels you a "bona fide agent," but the shipper sends you freight first and you decide later which unrelated client carrier will move it.

## Student Tasks

For each opportunity:

1. Classify it as **Consistent with Carrier-Side Agency**, **More Facts Required**, or **Significant Brokerage Risk**.
2. Identify the fact(s) that control the classification.
3. State whether allocation-of-traffic discretion is present.
4. State the operational next step: proceed within carrier scope, seek clarification, return/decline, or stop/escalate for authority/legal review.
5. Identify any fact that would materially change your classification and state how you would verify it.
6. Write a concise professional case note documenting the decision without making unsupported legal conclusions.

Then answer:

7. Explain why contract labels and compensation method cannot substitute for analysis of actual conduct.
8. Explain why cancellation/return to source differs from reassigning freight to another carrier.
9. Explain why “I never touch the freight” does not by itself resolve whether conduct is brokerage.
10. Draft an escalation handoff for one high-risk opportunity. State the known facts, unresolved issue, operational risk, and decision requiring authorized review.

## Critical-Failure Criterion

Treating **Opportunity B or D as ordinary safe carrier-side agency without recognizing discretionary allocation among competing carriers** is a critical failure.

## Passing Rule

At least 80% of rubric points **and** no critical failure.

## Competency Evidence Requirement

A classification alone is insufficient for full credit. The learner must show the controlling facts, distinguish known information from assumptions, select a defensible operational action, and document the reasoning. Remediation uses a materially different scenario rather than repetition of the disclosed answer pattern.
