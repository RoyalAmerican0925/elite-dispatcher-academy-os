# Module 01 — Freight Movement Desk Instructor Scoring Guide

**Instructor use only.**  
**Student practical:** `03-ASSESSMENTS/Practical-Assessments/Module-01-Practical.md`  
**Program policy:** `03-ASSESSMENTS/PROGRAM-ASSESSMENT-SCORING-AND-REMEDIATION-POLICY.md`

## Passing standard
**24/30** plus completion of any required remediation for material assumption/verification failures.

M01 has no designated automatic critical-failure family. However, a learner must not be recorded competent when the work demonstrates repeated unsafe assumption behavior even if the numeric score reaches 24.

## Dimensions — 30 points

1. **Party/function identification** — 0–3  
2. **Physical/information/document/payment flow separation** — 0–3  
3. **Document-purpose and lifecycle reasoning** — 0–3  
4. **KNOWN / UNKNOWN / VERIFY classification** — 0–3  
5. **Contradiction/missing-data handling** — 0–3  
6. **STOP → IDENTIFY → TRACE → VERIFY → HAND OFF application** — 0–3  
7. **Changed-evidence update discipline** — 0–3  
8. **Later-module handoff accuracy** — 0–3  
9. **Operational next-action selection** — 0–3  
10. **Professional case-note quality** — 0–3

### Scoring anchors
**3 — Meets premium standard:** correct and operationally usable; cites supplied evidence; separates knowns from unknowns; identifies verification source/next action; preserves boundary with later modules; documentation would be usable by another dispatcher/instructor.

**2 — Partially meets:** generally correct but misses one material evidence, verification, sequence, or documentation element.

**1 — Below standard:** recognizes part of the concept but relies on vague assumptions, definition-only reasoning, or an unsafe next action that requires instructor correction.

**0 — Not demonstrated:** missing/wrong response or reasoning is materially unsafe/unusable.

## Expected case treatment

### Parties
- Shipper: **Blue River Foods** — tenders/sends the freight in this scenario.
- Broker: **Prairie Freight Brokers** — arranges transportation.
- Carrier: **North Star Transport** — performs motor transportation through driver/equipment if the load advances.
- Dispatcher: **North Star's carrier-side dispatcher** — coordinates/supports North Star's freight operation.
- Driver: **Morgan / Unit 214** — carrier-side driver/truck resource.
- Consignee/receiver: **regional grocery distribution center in Des Moines** — receives the freight.
- Factor: later receivables/cash-flow participant if North Star factors an eligible invoice; not a physical freight participant.

### Four flows
- **Physical:** Blue River/Omaha origin → North Star driver/equipment → Des Moines receiver. Not yet begun in the intake state.
- **Information:** broker offer, facilities, truck location, copied 10:30 note, FCFS message, later correction, delivery appointment update.
- **Documents:** rate confirmation absent/pending; BOL/POD/invoice are later lifecycle documents.
- **Payment:** no payment event yet; later carrier billing/receivables/factoring may occur after transportation and required documentation.

### Evidence classification before correction
Expected core classifications:
- carrier — KNOWN from supplied case;
- driver/truck — KNOWN;
- truck location — KNOWN as supplied current training fact;
- equipment type — KNOWN as carrier-record fact but later fit analysis belongs to M03;
- commodity — KNOWN;
- origin/destination facilities — KNOWN as stated present in broker email; real-world authenticity/currentness would later be verified in applicable workflow;
- pickup timing — VERIFY because sources conflict;
- delivery timing — UNKNOWN/VERIFY because “tomorrow morning” lacks an exact operational window;
- stated weight — KNOWN as stated value, not an equipment-fit approval;
- stated rate — KNOWN as stated offer, not proof of booking/agreement/payment;
- special instructions — UNKNOWN;
- broker contact role — KNOWN as supplied training fact; deeper contact/entity authentication belongs to M06;
- rate confirmation — KNOWN ABSENT/PENDING.

### Later correction
The later broker message resolves the pickup contradiction for the training case and supplies a 09:00 delivery appointment. The record should preserve that there was an earlier contradiction and show how it was resolved. Special instructions and formal booking/document status remain unresolved.

## Assumption-control remediation
Mandatory remediation is required when the learner:
- silently converts UNKNOWN to a fact/zero;
- ignores a material contradiction;
- treats a document's presence as proof every fact is verified;
- performs a later-module conclusion instead of recognizing and routing the question;
- erases the history of a resolved contradiction.

Retest with a materially different fictional file. Preserve first-attempt work and record corrected evidence separately.
