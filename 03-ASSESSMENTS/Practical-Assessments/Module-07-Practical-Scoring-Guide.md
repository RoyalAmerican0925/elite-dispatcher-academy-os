# Module 07 Practical Assessment — Instructor Scoring Guide

**Instructor use only. Keep separate from the student practical.**  
**Student practical:** `03-ASSESSMENTS/Practical-Assessments/Module-07-Practical.md`

All fuel prices, fee percentages, and dollar amounts in this assessment are fictional instructional assumptions.

## Expected Calculations

### Load 1
- Revenue: $1,750
- Loaded miles: 620
- Origin deadhead: 55
- Relevant destination deadhead: 30
- **Total trip miles:** 705
- **Loaded RPM:** $1,750 ÷ 620 ≈ **$2.82**
- **Effective RPM:** $1,750 ÷ 705 ≈ **$2.48**
- Estimated gallons: 705 ÷ 6.7 ≈ **105.22 gal**
- Estimated fuel: 105.22 × $3.85 ≈ **$405.11**
- Dispatcher fee: $1,750 × 5% = **$87.50**
- Factoring fee: **$0**
- Tolls: **$0**
- **Estimated contribution margin under the included-cost assumptions:** $1,750 − $405.11 − $87.50 ≈ **$1,257.39**

### Load 2
- Revenue: $1,600
- Loaded miles: 590
- Origin deadhead: 10
- Relevant destination deadhead: 0
- **Total trip miles:** 600
- **Loaded RPM:** $1,600 ÷ 590 ≈ **$2.71**
- **Effective RPM:** $1,600 ÷ 600 ≈ **$2.67**
- Estimated gallons: 600 ÷ 6.7 ≈ **89.55 gal**
- Estimated fuel: 89.55 × $3.85 ≈ **$344.78**
- Dispatcher fee: $1,600 × 5% = **$80.00**
- Factoring fee: $1,600 × 2% = **$32.00**
- Tolls: **$35.00**
- **Estimated contribution margin under the included-cost assumptions:** $1,600 − $344.78 − $80 − $32 − $35 ≈ **$1,108.22**

### Load 3
- Revenue: $2,050
- Loaded miles: 500
- Origin deadhead: 200
- Relevant destination deadhead: 0
- **Total trip miles:** 700
- **Loaded RPM:** $2,050 ÷ 500 = **$4.10**
- **Effective RPM:** $2,050 ÷ 700 ≈ **$2.93**
- Estimated gallons: 700 ÷ 6.7 ≈ **104.48 gal**
- Estimated fuel: 104.48 × $3.85 ≈ **$402.24**
- Dispatcher fee: $2,050 × 5% = **$102.50**
- Factoring fee: **$0**
- Tolls: **$0**
- **Estimated contribution margin under the included-cost assumptions:** $2,050 − $402.24 − $102.50 ≈ **$1,545.26**
- **Do not add detention revenue.** The assessment states only that a fictional detention term exists; no detention has occurred or been earned at the time of evaluation.

## Expected Analysis

A strong student may recommend Load 2 or Load 3 depending on the reasoning presented, but the recommendation must be evidence-based and must not treat any one metric as conclusive.

- **Load 3** has the highest loaded RPM, highest effective RPM, and highest estimated contribution margin under the limited supplied costs, but it also has 200 deadhead miles and an unearned detention clause that must not be counted as revenue.
- **Load 2** has minimal deadhead and delivers into the stated high-freight-volume area, giving it the strongest explicit positioning advantage in the exercise, but it includes tolls and a factoring fee.
- **Load 1** has a lower effective RPM than Loads 2 and 3 and delivers into the stated low-freight-volume area, though its estimated contribution margin exceeds Load 2 under the supplied limited-cost assumptions.

The practical is designed to reward disciplined comparison, not a memorized "correct load." Full-credit reasoning must discuss both quantitative and non-quantitative factors and preserve carrier decision authority.

## 0–3 Scoring Dimensions

Score each dimension from 0 to 3.

### 1. Calculation Accuracy
- **3:** Total miles, loaded RPM, effective RPM, fuel, fees, and estimated contribution figures are materially correct for all three loads.
- **2:** Minor arithmetic errors that do not change the overall reasoning.
- **1:** Multiple errors or incomplete calculations.
- **0:** Calculations are largely absent or unusable.

### 2. Effective-RPM Discipline
- **3:** Correctly uses effective RPM and deadhead in the comparison and does not rely on loaded RPM alone.
- **2:** Uses effective RPM but analysis is incomplete.
- **1:** Mentions deadhead/effective RPM but still primarily relies on loaded RPM.
- **0:** Uses loaded RPM alone as the deciding metric.

### 3. Terminology / Profit Labeling
- **3:** Correctly labels partial calculations and never calls estimated contribution margin "profit" or "net profit."
- **2:** Terminology mostly correct with one minor imprecision.
- **1:** Repeated terminology problems without a critical profit claim.
- **0:** Calls the limited calculation guaranteed profit/net profit.

### 4. Positioning Analysis
- **3:** Correctly discusses the high-freight-volume versus low-freight-volume destination information and treats it as a planning factor, not guaranteed future revenue.
- **2:** Mentions positioning with limited explanation.
- **1:** Minimal positioning reference.
- **0:** Ignores positioning entirely or treats it as guaranteed revenue.

### 5. Accessorial Verification
- **3:** Explicitly recognizes Load 3 detention as unearned and does not include it in current revenue.
- **2:** Correct treatment with limited explanation.
- **1:** Ambiguous treatment but does not actually add detention revenue.
- **0 — CRITICAL FAILURE:** Adds the unearned detention amount to guaranteed/current revenue or otherwise treats the clause as money already earned.

## Completion Rule

Use the academy's current practical-assessment scoring/remediation policy. Regardless of total points, a student who triggers the Accessorial Verification critical failure must remediate and reassess before Module 07 practical completion.
