## Part E — Applied Freight-File Reasoning

### 16. KNOWN / UNKNOWN / VERIFY
Expected treatment:
- **Origin city — KNOWN:** Omaha is explicitly stated.
- **Commodity — UNKNOWN:** no commodity is supplied.
- **Pickup timing — VERIFY:** two supplied records conflict (08:00 appointment-like note vs. FCFS 07:00–11:00).
- **Delivery facility — UNKNOWN:** Des Moines city is known but the actual facility is absent.
- **Stated rate — KNOWN as a stated offer, not automatically verified/accepted/earned:** $1,250 is present in the broker message. Full-credit answers may say VERIFY if they clearly explain that operational reliance still requires reconciliation with the active opportunity/document flow. Do not penalize a student who distinguishes “known stated value” from “verified commercial agreement.”

### 17. Four flows
Expected framework:
1. **Physical:** Omaha → Des Moines movement; North Star driver/truck would physically move the freight if the opportunity advances.
2. **Information/communication:** broker message, copied pickup note, newer FCFS message, missing commodity/facility/time facts.
3. **Document:** no rate confirmation has been issued; document flow is incomplete.
4. **Payment:** no completed transportation/invoice/payment event exists yet; payment flow is not sufficiently developed from the supplied facts.

Full credit requires separation of flows rather than collapsing everything into “load details.”

### 18. STOP framework
Expected response: **STOP relying on the conflicting pickup detail; IDENTIFY the conflict; TRACE the source/newer message; VERIFY the controlling pickup instructions through an appropriate current/authorized source; then HAND OFF or move the file forward only after the discrepancy is resolved.** The learner must not simply choose whichever time looks more recent without verification.

### 19. Current status
**b — Verification/information gathering required.** Material operating facts are missing/conflicting. A city pair and rate are not a usable/complete freight file.

## Instructor Reference Controls
- Do not present the carrier-side dispatch model as the only occupational use of “dispatcher.”
- Do not collapse DOT and FMCSA into identical entities.
- Do not teach geography alone as the interstate/intrastate test.
- Do not imply a populated field is automatically verified.
- Do not convert a missing field to zero, “probably,” or assumed fact.
- M01 teaches recognition and handoff. Advanced legal/equipment/onboarding/fraud/economics/booking/HOS determinations belong in later modules.
