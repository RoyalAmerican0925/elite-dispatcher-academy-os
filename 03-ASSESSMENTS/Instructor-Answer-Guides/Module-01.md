## Instructor Reference Notes

- Program scope: This module teaches the carrier-side dispatcher model. Do not present that model as the only possible occupational use of the word dispatcher.
- DOT vs. FMCSA: FMCSA is an administration within the U.S. Department of Transportation. They are related but not identical.
- USDOT vs. operating authority: Treat these as distinct regulatory concepts. A USDOT number identifies a regulated entity for safety/registration purposes; operating authority authorizes specified regulated operations where applicable.
- Interstate vs. intrastate: Do not teach geography alone as the test. A movement within one state can still be part of interstate commerce when it is one leg of a broader interstate movement.
- For-hire vs. private: Do not imply every private carrier has the same USDOT or operating-authority requirements. Applicability depends on the operation and governing rules.
- Module boundary: Keep broker-versus-dispatcher legal analysis introductory here. The detailed legal boundary belongs in Module 02.
