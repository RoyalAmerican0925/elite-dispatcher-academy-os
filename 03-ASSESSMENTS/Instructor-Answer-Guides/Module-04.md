### Part D — Sample Reasoning

19. The DQ file is the carrier's required compliance record under applicable law; the dispatch profile is the narrower operational information a dispatcher actually needs to plan and communicate. Building a DQ file would mean taking on compliance recordkeeping duties that belong to the motor carrier, not the dispatcher — see Section 7.

20. The current exemption is temporary and scheduled to expire October 11, 2026. FMCSA has said it does not anticipate additional nationwide NRII waivers or exemptions after the six-month period, but that is not a guaranteed future procedure. The academy must reverify FMCSA's then-current process rather than assume a fixed post-expiration paper-certificate rule.

21. A COI is not proof of current operating authority, doesn't guarantee coverage hasn't changed, and isn't a substitute for current verification — see Section 5 and Scenario S-04-02, where a clean-looking COI coexists with inactive operating authority.

22. "Dated within 60 days" only confirms the paper MEC falls within the exemption's *time* window — it does not by itself confirm the certificate belongs to the correct driver, that the carrier's process is otherwise sound, or that no other disqualifying fact exists. Treating the date alone as sufficient skips the verification step Scenario S-04-13 is built around, and puts the dispatcher in the position of making an independent regulatory determination that isn't theirs to make — see Section 8 and Scenario S-04-13.

## Instructor Safety & Publication-Control Notes

- **Current FMCSA systems:** As of May 14, 2026, legacy L&I is historical-only and does not reflect current Motus filings. SAFER is supplemental; teach students to use the current FMCSA source appropriate to the fact being checked.
- **NRII medical certification:** The nationwide paper-MEC exemption is effective April 11 through October 11, 2026 and allows reliance on paper MECs for up to 60 days after issuance. **A paper MEC within this window is grounds for VERIFY / CONDITIONAL PROCEED, never a simple, unverified PROCEED** — see Scenario S-04-13. Reverify FMCSA procedure before publication after the exemption date.
- **Insurance:** Keep the $750,000 federal BIPD floor limited to the described non-hazardous for-hire property-carrier category. Cargo and other coverages may be contractual, state-specific, or business choices.
- **W-9 identity checks:** A name difference is not automatically fraud. Disregarded-entity tax structures may explain legitimate differences; verify the relationship.
- **DQ file boundary:** Do not turn dispatcher onboarding into motor-carrier DQ/Clearinghouse/drug-alcohol compliance administration.
- **NOA/payment disputes:** Teach payment-direction verification, not legal adjudication of competing receivable claims.
- **LPOA:** Authority is limited by the document and applicable law. Do not teach blanket signing authority.
- **Privacy/security:** Separate law, state-specific requirements, contract terms, and cybersecurity best practice. Consumer email is not automatically unlawful, but sensitive information should be minimized and protected.

## Module 04 Reference Ledger & Dependencies

### Motus
APPROVED DEFINITION: FMCSA's current, centralized registration platform as of the 2026 modernization, replacing legacy registration systems.
CLASSIFICATION: CURRENT-DATE MATERIAL / FEDERAL SYSTEM
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 2
RELATED TERMS: Legacy L&I, SAFER
NOTES: Reverify before publication — active modernization area.

### Legacy L&I (Licensing & Insurance)
APPROVED DEFINITION: FMCSA's former registration system; as of May 14, 2026, a historical record not reflecting current Motus filings.
CLASSIFICATION: CURRENT-DATE MATERIAL / FEDERAL SYSTEM
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 2
RELATED TERMS: Motus, SAFER
NOTES: Do not use as a source for current filing status.

### SAFER
APPROVED DEFINITION: A public FMCSA safety/entity snapshot tool, useful as a supplemental lookup.
CLASSIFICATION: FEDERAL SYSTEM
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 2
RELATED TERMS: Motus
NOTES: Not a complete substitute for current registration/authority/insurance verification.

### UCR (Unified Carrier Registration)
APPROVED DEFINITION: An annual registration framework applicable to covered interstate/international transportation entities.
CLASSIFICATION: FEDERAL LAW (registration requirement) / INDUSTRY PRACTICE (screening)
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 3
RELATED TERMS: Operating Authority
NOTES: Carrier's own responsibility; not automatically the dispatcher's filing duty.

### BIPD (Bodily Injury / Property Damage) Insurance
APPROVED DEFINITION: Federally required public-liability insurance for for-hire motor carriers, with minimums varying by commodity and vehicle weight.
CLASSIFICATION: FEDERAL LAW
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 4 (FACT-M04-003)
RELATED TERMS: COI, Cargo Insurance
NOTES: $750,000 general non-hazmat floor at ≥10,001 lb GVWR; higher for certain hazmat.

### COI (Certificate of Insurance)
APPROVED DEFINITION: A document summarizing represented insurance information.
CLASSIFICATION: INDUSTRY DOCUMENT
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 5
RELATED TERMS: BIPD, Operating Authority
NOTES: Not proof of authority or current filing status by itself.

### W-9
APPROVED DEFINITION: An IRS tax-identification/certification form commonly used in business payment setup.
CLASSIFICATION: TAX / INDUSTRY DOCUMENT
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 6
RELATED TERMS: Disregarded Entity
NOTES: Name differences from FMCSA registration are common for disregarded LLCs — verify, don't assume fraud.

### DQ File (Driver Qualification File)
APPROVED DEFINITION: The motor carrier's required driver-qualification and compliance record set under applicable law.
CLASSIFICATION: REGULATORY COMPLIANCE RECORD
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 7 (FACT-M04-004)
RELATED TERMS: Dispatch Profile
NOTES: Carrier-owned; distinct from the dispatcher's operational profile.

### Dispatch Profile
APPROVED DEFINITION: The dispatcher's operational record containing information reasonably necessary to dispatch a driver.
CLASSIFICATION: OPERATIONAL RECORD
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 7
RELATED TERMS: DQ File
NOTES: Narrower in scope and purpose than the DQ file.

### NRII (National Registry II)
APPROVED DEFINITION: The FMCSA framework for secure electronic transmission of medical-examiner certification data to SDLAs/CDLIS.
CLASSIFICATION: CURRENT-DATE MATERIAL / FEDERAL SYSTEM
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 8 (FACT-M04-005)
RELATED TERMS: Medical Examiner's Certificate
NOTES: Subject to an active temporary exemption through October 11, 2026 — reverify before/after that date. A paper MEC within the exemption window supports VERIFY/CONDITIONAL PROCEED, not an automatic PROCEED — see Scenario S-04-13.

### NOA (Notice of Assignment)
APPROVED DEFINITION: A notice commonly used to inform an account debtor that payment rights have been assigned to a factoring company.
CLASSIFICATION: INDUSTRY / LEGAL DOCUMENT
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 11 (FACT-M04-006)
RELATED TERMS: Factoring
NOTES: Does not by itself let a dispatcher conclusively resolve ownership disputes.

### LPOA (Limited Power of Attorney)
APPROVED DEFINITION: A document granting specifically defined agency authority according to its own written terms.
CLASSIFICATION: LEGAL DOCUMENT
FIRST-USE LOCATION: Module 04, Section 15
RELATED TERMS: Dispatch Agreement
NOTES: Scope-limited; not a blanket signing authority.
