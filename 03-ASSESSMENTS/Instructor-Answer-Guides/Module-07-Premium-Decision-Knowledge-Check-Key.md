## Remediation Standard
Items 2, 3, 5, 6, and 10 are simulation-entry controls. For a miss, require the student to explain the failure mechanism and then solve a materially different case before SIM-007.

Examples:
- Item 2 remediation: use unknown destination repositioning or unknown toll exposure rather than repeating unknown origin deadhead.
- Item 3 remediation: change both required effective RPM and total miles.
- Item 5 remediation: change a different material input, such as actual deadhead or a verified fee.
- Item 6 remediation: use layover/TONU/extra-stop conditional revenue rather than repeating detention.
- Item 10 remediation: use a scenario involving a dispatcher recommendation that conflicts with the carrier's stated acceptance criterion.
