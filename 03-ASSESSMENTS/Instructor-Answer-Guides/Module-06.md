**Broker Verification & Fraud Prevention**

Integrated Final — August 2026

## Scenario Lab — Instructor Guide

### S-06-01: Clean Verification
Classification: **CLEAR.** All workflow steps check out with no unresolved mismatch. Proceed to the next stage.

### S-06-02: Real MC, Email-Domain Mismatch
Classification: **VERIFY → HOLD IF UNRESOLVED.** A real MC number does not verify the specific contact. Callback through a trusted, previously verified channel.

### S-06-03: Callback Contradicts Incoming Contact
Classification: **DO NOT BOOK / ESCALATE.** The trusted channel directly contradicted the incoming contact. This is a confirmed contact mismatch requiring escalation.

### S-06-04: Changed Payment Instructions
Classification: **HOLD / VERIFY.** Do not act on new instructions until independently verified through a trusted channel.

### S-06-05: Rate Confirmation Name/MC Mismatch
Classification: **HOLD / VERIFY.** Do not sign until the mismatch is resolved; do not label it fraud from the document alone.

### S-06-06: Similar Duplicate Postings
Classification: **VERIFY.** Duplicate freight alone does not establish double brokering. Verify authority and relationships first.

### S-06-07: Factor Declines Broker
Classification: **DO NOT USE if carrier policy requires factor approval.** A factor decline is a financial-risk decision, not proof of fraud.

### S-06-08: Required Broker Authority Inactive
Classification: **DO NOT BOOK.** Required authority is not active. Do not assume this automatically resolves separate insurance questions.

### S-06-09: Financial-Security Cancellation Pending
Classification: **HOLD / ESCALATE / VERIFY CURRENT STATUS.** Pending cancellation is a risk event, not the same as already-cancelled security.

### S-06-10: Real Broker Email Appears Compromised
Classification: **HOLD / VERIFY.** Use trusted callback verification instead of relying on the apparently legitimate email account.

### S-06-11: Urgent Request to Bypass Verification
Classification: **VERIFY / HOLD.** Urgency does not change the verification standard.

### S-06-12: Multiple Small Inconsistencies
Classification: **ESCALATE / PASS IF RISK UNRESOLVED.** Accumulating inconsistencies should not be dismissed merely because none is conclusive alone.

### Part D — Sample Reasoning

18. A company can be genuinely registered and authorized while the specific individual contacting you is not actually an authorized representative — for example, because of impersonation or a compromised account. Confirming the company is real answers a different question from confirming who you are actually communicating with.

19. Duplicate-looking postings have several possible innocent explanations, including separate legitimate freight, authorized co-brokering, multi-channel tendering, or reposting. Jumping directly to “double brokering” skips the verify-first process and risks an unfounded accusation.

20. Fraud is a serious factual and legal conclusion that a dispatcher is not positioned to establish independently. Professional operational language accurately describes the dispatcher's decision not to proceed without making an unsupported allegation.

## Instructor Safety / Publication Controls

### Current-Date FMCSA Systems
Section 4 (Motus/SAFER/Legacy L&I) is an active modernization area; reverify before publication.

### BMC-85 2026 Rules
The 30-day trustee-replacement window applies to trustee-ineligibility determinations. It is distinct from the separate 7-calendar-day rule for financial-security shortfalls below $75,000. Do not conflate these timelines.

### Fraud vs. Red-Flag Distinction
No scenario in this module should conclude with an unsupported fraud determination. Use VERIFY, HOLD, ESCALATE, DO NOT USE, or DO NOT BOOK based on verified facts and policy.

### Legal vs. Operational Classification
Broker authority is LAW/REGULATION; DNU lists are CARRIER POLICY. Keep this distinction visible.

### Cybersecurity Boundary
Do not teach forensic investigation, header analysis, IP tracing, or hacking techniques. The dispatcher recognizes the pattern, verifies through trusted channels, documents, and escalates.

### Factoring/Credit Boundary
Detailed factoring mechanics remain Module 14's territory. Module 06 only teaches how to interpret risk signals.
