# TWC-VR Self-Employment Project

This folder contains the vocational-rehabilitation and business-development work for the Texas Workforce Commission Vocational Rehabilitation self-employment project.

## Governing strategy

Disability-related employment barrier → appropriate self-employment outcome → specific occupational goal → required skills → required training → feasible business model → required tools/technology/supplies → revenue → sustainable employment.

## Research standard

- Use current authoritative sources for TWC-VR, Texas, federal VR, and Social Security rules.
- Do not represent any expenditure as approved unless TWC-VR has actually approved it.
- Classify proposed expenditures as expressly supported, potentially allowable with approval, unclear pending verification, probably not allowable, or not allowable.
- Separate the vocational goal from the company entity and from individual products/verticals.
- Build the company around commercial viability rather than around what VR might purchase.
