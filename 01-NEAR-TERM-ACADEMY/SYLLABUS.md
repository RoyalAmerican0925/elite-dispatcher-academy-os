# Elite Trucking Dispatcher Academy — Syllabus

## Program Goal
Prepare a beginner to understand and perform core trucking-dispatch functions through structured instruction, applied practice, assessments, and realistic decision scenarios.

## Program Sequence

1. Dispatcher Foundations and Industry Framework
2. Legal Boundaries, Roles, Authority, and Compliance Foundations
3. Trucking Operations, Equipment, Freight, and Operational Vocabulary
4. Carrier and Driver Onboarding
5. Load Boards, Freight Search, and Market/Lane Research
6. Broker and Load Verification, Risk Screening, and Fraud Awareness
7. Rate Math, RPM, Cost Awareness, and Load Profitability
8. Rate Negotiation and Broker Communication
9. Booking Loads, Rate Confirmations, and Dispatch Documentation
10. Trip Planning, Routing, Time, Fuel, and Appointment Management
11. Hours of Service and ELD Operational Awareness
12. Active Load Monitoring and Driver Communication
13. Delays, Detention, TONU, Breakdowns, Claims, and Exception Management
14. POD, Invoicing, Factoring Awareness, Recordkeeping, and Load Closeout
15. Dispatcher Business Operations, Client Service, Systems, and Capstone Practice

## Learning Methods
Student manuals, examples, guided practice, worksheets, templates, checklists, SOPs, flashcards, knowledge checks, quizzes, module exams, scenario exercises, case studies, simulations, and capstone practice.

## Assessment Model
Students should demonstrate both knowledge and application. Assessment may include quizzes, module exams, practical exercises, scenario decisions, and a final integrated assessment.

## Completion Standard
Final passing scores, remediation rules, required practical work, and certificate eligibility will be controlled by the assessment and publication standards in this repository.

## Compliance Note
The academy is educational. It must distinguish dispatcher functions from motor-carrier, broker, driver, shipper, and other regulated responsibilities and must not present educational content as legal advice.
