# Near-Term Course Specification

## Product
A complete self-paced trucking dispatcher academy designed to teach a beginner the operational knowledge, decision-making skills, documentation practices, calculations, communication, compliance awareness, and business foundations needed to perform dispatcher work.

## Required Product Components
- Course syllabus and module map.
- Student manuals.
- Instructor/reference materials.
- Worksheets, checklists, templates, SOPs, and flashcards.
- Quizzes, module exams, answer keys, rubrics, practical assessments, and final assessment.
- Case studies and realistic scenarios.
- Interactive practice and simulations.
- Student progress and completion standards.
- Certificate requirements.

## Core Competency Areas
- dispatcher role and workflow
- legal and professional boundaries
- trucking equipment and freight types
- carrier/driver onboarding
- load boards and market research
- broker/carrier verification
- rate-per-mile and profitability calculations
- negotiation and load booking
- rate confirmations and dispatch documentation
- trip planning and service management
- HOS/ELD awareness
- detention, TONU, lumper, delays, and exception handling
- invoicing and back-office documentation
- fraud prevention and risk recognition
- dispatcher business operations

## Product Standard
The academy must be usable as a standalone commercial product before the full EAOS/TKE software platform exists.
