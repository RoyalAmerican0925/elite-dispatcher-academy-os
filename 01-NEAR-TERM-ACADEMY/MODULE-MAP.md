# Authoritative Module Map

## Purpose
This map controls the instructional sequence for the near-term Elite Trucking Dispatcher Academy. Module titles may be refined as recovered source files are audited, but numbering and dependencies must not be changed casually.

## Core Sequence

Module 01 — Dispatcher Foundations and Industry Framework

Module 02 — Legal Boundaries, Roles, Authority, and Compliance Foundations

Module 03 — Trucking Operations, Equipment, Freight, and Operational Vocabulary

Module 04 — Carrier and Driver Onboarding

Module 05 — Load Boards, Freight Search, and Market/Lane Research

Module 06 — Broker and Load Verification, Risk Screening, and Fraud Awareness

Module 07 — Rate Math, RPM, Cost Awareness, and Load Profitability

Module 08 — Rate Negotiation and Broker Communication

Module 09 — Booking Loads, Rate Confirmations, and Dispatch Documentation

Module 10 — Trip Planning, Routing, Time, Fuel, and Appointment Management

Module 11 — Hours of Service and ELD Operational Awareness

Module 12 — Active Load Monitoring and Driver Communication

Module 13 — Delays, Detention, TONU, Breakdowns, Claims, and Exception Management

Module 14 — POD, Invoicing, Factoring Awareness, Recordkeeping, and Load Closeout

Module 15 — Dispatcher Business Operations, Client Service, Systems, and Capstone Practice

## Cross-Cutting Competencies
Fraud prevention, ethical boundaries, documentation discipline, communication, safety awareness, profitability, verification, and escalation should recur throughout the sequence rather than exist only in one module.

## Asset Expectation Per Module
Each module should ultimately have learning objectives, student manual, vocabulary, examples, guided practice, worksheet or operational tool where appropriate, knowledge check, quiz or assessment, answer key, scenario practice, source/provenance record, dependencies, and completion criteria.

## Control Rule
Recovered files must be mapped to this sequence by their actual content. A filename alone is not sufficient evidence that a module is complete or correctly numbered.
