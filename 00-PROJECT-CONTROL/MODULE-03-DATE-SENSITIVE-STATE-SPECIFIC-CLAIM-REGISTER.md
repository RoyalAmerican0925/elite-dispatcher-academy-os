## Why This Register Exists Separately

Module 03 relies heavily on distinguishing what's federally fixed from what varies by state, configuration, or fact pattern — that distinction is the module's core safety discipline (see the "four categories" framing in Part 02, Section 20). This register makes that distinction auditable in one place, separate from the stable-figure register, so a reviewer checking for outdated state or configuration claims doesn't have to re-read the entire module to find them.
