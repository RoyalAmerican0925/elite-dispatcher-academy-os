# Module 09 Regulatory / Legal Source Register

**Recheck date:** 2026-09-05  
**Scope:** Booking Loads, Rate Confirmations, Dispatch Documentation  
**Rule:** This register distinguishes actual federal authority from contract terms, carrier policy, platform workflow, and Academy training controls.

## Primary authority checked

### 15 U.S.C. § 7001 — E-SIGN general validity / retention
Primary source: U.S. House Office of the Law Revision Counsel, preliminary code text current in 2026:  
https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title15-section7001

Controlled teaching use:
- A signature, contract, or other record in an interstate/foreign-commerce transaction is not denied legal effect solely because it is electronic.
- The statute does **not** mean every electronic action proves authority, assent, authenticity, or agreement to every term.
- Where a legal record-retention requirement applies, §7001(d) addresses electronic retention through accurate, accessible, reproducible records.
- The Academy therefore teaches document integrity and authority control without requiring a particular commercial e-sign platform.

### 15 U.S.C. § 7006 — definitions
Primary source: U.S. House Office of the Law Revision Counsel:  
https://uscode.house.gov/view.xhtml?edition=prelim&req=granuleid%3AUSC-prelim-title15-chapter96

Controlled teaching use:
- Electronic signature includes an electronic sound, symbol, or process attached to or logically associated with a contract/record and executed/adopted with intent to sign.
- This definition does not grant a dispatcher authority the carrier did not supply.

### 49 CFR Part 371 / §371.3 — broker transaction records
Primary regulatory family: eCFR Part 371; FMCSA broker guidance summarizes §371.3 transaction-record requirements.  
https://www.ecfr.gov/current/title-49/subtitle-B/chapter-III/subchapter-B/part-371  
https://www.fmcsa.dot.gov/sites/fmcsa.dot.gov/files/docs/small-entity-comp-guide-broker-operations-508.pdf

Controlled teaching use:
- Broker recordkeeping rules are background context for brokered transactions.
- M09 does **not** convert broker regulatory recordkeeping duties into a fabricated universal carrier-side rate-confirmation form or universal dispatcher retention period.
