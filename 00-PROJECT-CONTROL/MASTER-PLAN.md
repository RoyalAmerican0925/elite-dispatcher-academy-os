# Elite Dispatcher Academy OS — Master Plan

## Purpose
Build a complete, professional, self-paced trucking dispatcher academy now while deliberately building toward the larger Elite Academy Operating System (EAOS) and Transportation Knowledge Engine (TKE).

## Two-Track Strategy

### Track One — Near-Term Commercial Academy
The first deliverable is a complete dispatcher training product that can teach a beginner the operational knowledge and decision-making required for trucking dispatch and dispatcher-business operations.

The academy will include curriculum, student manuals, instructor materials, worksheets, checklists, templates, SOPs, flashcards, quizzes, module exams, final examination, practical assessments, case studies, simulations, games, completion standards, and certificate requirements.

### Track Two — Ultimate EAOS/TKE Platform
The dispatcher academy is the first application of a larger reusable education and transportation-knowledge platform.

EAOS will ultimately coordinate curriculum production, assessments, simulations, student and instructor experiences, quality assurance, dashboards, AI-assisted production, versioning, and publication controls.

TKE will ultimately organize structured transportation knowledge, terminology, facts, sources, regulatory updates, dependencies, reusable data models, and the large asset manifest.

## Governing Rule
Near-term work must be commercially useful now without becoming throwaway work later. IDs, terminology, metadata, dependencies, source controls, and acceptance standards should be designed for eventual EAOS/TKE ingestion wherever practical.

## Build Phases

### Phase One — Recover and Control
Recover existing plans and assets. Establish repository structure, status tracking, terminology, IDs, dependencies, sources, and acceptance criteria.

### Phase Two — Complete Core Academy
Finish curriculum modules, assessments, student tools, instructor resources, operational templates, and supporting materials.

### Phase Three — Interactive Training
Build load-selection, rate-math, negotiation, broker-verification, trip-planning, fraud-recognition, exception-management, and dispatch simulations.

### Phase Four — Commercial Packaging
Complete onboarding, delivery, student progression, certificates, quality checks, product packaging, and launch requirements.

### Phase Five — EAOS Foundation
Turn production rules, metadata, assessment logic, simulations, QA, and content lifecycle into reusable software services and dashboards.

### Phase Six — TKE Expansion
Normalize transportation knowledge, source evidence, regulatory information, dependencies, terminology, and asset manifests into the broader Transportation Knowledge Engine.

## Status Model
An asset must be classified as Planned, Drafted, Reviewed, Verified, Production-Ready, or Published. Software and interactive assets also require functional testing before Production-Ready status.
