## Why This Register Exists Separately

Module 04 is unusually date-sensitive for a foundational module — two of its core teachings (Motus/legacy-L&I status and the NRII exemption) are tied to specific 2026 dates that are already in motion. This register isolates those two high-risk items so a reviewer can prioritize them without re-reading the full module.
