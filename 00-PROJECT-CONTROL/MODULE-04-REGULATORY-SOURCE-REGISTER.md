## Notes on Source Hierarchy

This module consistently distinguishes six categories, and content is labeled accordingly — matching the discipline established in Modules 02 and 03:

1. **FEDERAL REQUIREMENT** — binding federal statute/regulation.
2. **STATE-SPECIFIC REQUIREMENT** — varies by state (UCR administration, UCC enactments governing NOAs).
3. **INDUSTRY PRACTICE** — common convention, not a binding legal rule (e.g., broker UCR screening, which is not federally mandated).
4. **CURRENT-DATE MATERIAL** — active, time-sensitive systems/rules requiring reverification (Motus modernization, NRII exemption).
5. **CARRIER-SPECIFIC / DISPATCHER-SPECIFIC FACT** — must be verified per situation (COI details, W-9 entity relationship, equipment specifics).
6. **COURSE SAFETY PRACTICE** — this academy's own risk-control teaching (STOP/VERIFY/ESCALATE, payment-change callback protocol).
