## Important Timeline Correction — 7 Business Days vs. 7 Calendar Days

The current eCFR text at 49 CFR § 387.307(e)(5)–(6) states that, after FMCSA serves the broker with the written notice described there, the authority will be suspended within **7 business days** unless qualifying evidence is provided; failure to respond within **7 business days** results in suspension.

This must be distinguished from a separate rule in § 387.307(b): acceptable BMC-85 trust assets must be capable of liquidation to cash within **7 calendar days**.
