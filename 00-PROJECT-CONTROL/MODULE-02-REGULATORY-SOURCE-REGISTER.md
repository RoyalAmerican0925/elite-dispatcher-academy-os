## Notes on Source Hierarchy

This module consistently distinguishes three tiers, and every claim in the student manual is labeled with one of them:

1. **STATUTE** — the underlying federal law (49 U.S.C. citations).
2. **REGULATION** — codified federal regulations restating or implementing the statute (49 CFR citations).
3. **FMCSA GUIDANCE** — interpretive guidance (principally 88 FR 39368) that clarifies how FMCSA applies the regulation, but is not itself binding law in the same way as the statute or regulation.

The manual is deliberately careful not to attribute guidance-level recommendations (e.g., written agreements as a supporting factor) to the regulation's actual text (which requires only a preexisting agreement providing a continuing relationship, not a specified written format). See Part 01, Section 6 and Part 02, Section 16.
