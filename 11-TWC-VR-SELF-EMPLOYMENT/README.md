# TWC-VR Self-Employment Project

This area manages the vocational-rehabilitation and self-employment development work associated with Texas Workforce Commission Vocational Rehabilitation Services (TWC-VR).

## Project rule

The business is developed for commercial viability and sustainable employment. It is not designed around items that VR might purchase.

The working decision chain is:

Disability-related employment barrier → appropriate self-employment outcome → specific occupational goal → required skills → required training → feasible business model → required tools/technology/supplies → revenue → sustainable employment.

## Evidence standard

Current TWC-VR policy, Texas law/rules, federal VR regulations, and Social Security rules must be verified against authoritative current sources before being treated as controlling. Proposed expenditures must be classified as expressly supported, potentially allowable subject to approval, unclear pending verification, probably not allowable, or not allowable. No item is treated as approved unless TWC-VR actually approves it.

## Structure

- `01-INTERVIEW-PREPARATION/` — initial interview strategy, questions, functional/vocational statements, records checklist, and meeting notes.
- `02-ELIGIBILITY-IPE/` — eligibility, assessment, IPE development, employment outcome, and counselor requirements.
- `03-SELF-EMPLOYMENT-POLICY/` — current TWC/federal self-employment authority, policy research, forms, and process verification.
- `04-OCCUPATIONAL-GOAL/` — occupational-goal analysis and justification.
- `05-TRAINING-PLAN/` — skills-gap analysis, training research, providers, credentials, and vocational justification.
- `06-BUSINESS-MODEL/` — company architecture, customers, revenue models, products, services, and feasibility.
- `07-MARKET-RESEARCH/` — market evidence, competitors, pricing, customer validation, and demand analysis.
- `08-BUSINESS-PLAN/` — working and final business-plan materials, including any verified TWC-required forms.
- `09-EQUIPMENT-SOFTWARE-SUPPLIES/` — item-level necessity, policy authority, quotes, alternatives, and allowability status.
- `10-FINANCIALS/` — startup costs, operating expenses, pricing, revenue projections, cash flow, break-even, and assumptions.
- `11-SSA-BENEFITS-PLANNING/` — verified Social Security work-incentive and benefits-planning research relevant to self-employment.
- `12-COMPLIANCE-RISK/` — legal, tax, licensing, privacy, cybersecurity, insurance, regulatory, and operational risks.
- `13-TWC-COMMUNICATIONS/` — SARA communications, counselor requests, decisions, approvals, denials, and follow-up records.
- `14-SOURCE-LIBRARY/` — authoritative-source index and dated research notes; do not store unnecessary sensitive personal records here.
- `15-AI-TECH-COMPANY/` — core AI/automation/software company strategy and product-development roadmap.
- `16-DISPATCH-EDTECH-VERTICAL/` — trucking-dispatch education product as a company vertical; existing academy production remains in the established academy folders.
- `17-FASHION-TEXTILE-TECH/` — fashion/textile technology feasibility and integration analysis; equipment is not presumed justified.
- `18-PROCUREMENT-EVIDENCE/` — vendor research, quotes, specifications, comparable alternatives, and procurement justification.
- `19-DECISION-LOG/` — material decisions, assumptions, unresolved questions, and changes in strategy.
- `20-FINAL-SUBMISSION/` — only reviewed, verified materials intended for submission to TWC-VR.
- `99-ARCHIVE/` — superseded drafts retained for history.

## Privacy rule

Do not commit medical records, Social Security numbers, account credentials, private identifiers, or other unnecessary sensitive information to this repository. Working documents should describe vocationally relevant limitations without exposing more personal information than necessary.
