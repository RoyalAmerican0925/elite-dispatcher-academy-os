## Approved SIM-009 redesign
SIM-009 is **Booking Desk: Save the Load** — an occupational workdesk game, not a quiz presented as a simulation. The learner works from a dispatcher desktop with inbox/messages, document viewer, booking file/workbench, task/event queue, status controls, broker/driver/carrier interruptions, document/version handling, and consequence-driven workflow. Hidden competency scoring remains underneath the experience.

The game retains 12 progressive workflow stages, 24+ meaningful occupational actions, all 15 M09 competencies, exactly six critical-failure families, immutable first-attempt critical evidence, and materially different reassessment. Player-facing language must emphasize desk work, files, messages, corrections, authority, packet assembly, and load status rather than numbered test questions.

Assessments remain separate graded instruments; the simulation must not duplicate their test-like UX.

Integration target: `academy-master-architecture`. `main` is not authorized.
