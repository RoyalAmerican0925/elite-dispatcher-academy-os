# Module 05 — Premium Depth & Instructor Alignment Design

**Date:** 2026-09-01
**Branch:** `academy-master-architecture`
**Status:** APPROVED DESIGN
**Module:** 05 — Load Boards & Freight Sourcing

## Purpose
Raise Module 05 from its existing regular C-013 closeout state to the Academy premium-depth standard before SIM-005 is designed. Preserve the existing seven-part manual and validated operational chain where sound; repair and deepen rather than rewrite blindly.

## Premium Outcome
A learner completing Module 05 must be able to work a carrier-side load-search desk: translate carrier constraints into search criteria, source candidate freight, screen listings, identify missing or suspicious information, perform first-pass verification, compare viable loads, communicate professionally, review written terms for discrepancies, and route deeper identity/fraud/financial-security verification to Module 06.

## Locked Module Boundary
Module 05 owns:

**FIND → SCREEN → FLAG → FIRST-PASS VERIFY → COMPARE → COMMUNICATE → REVIEW WRITTEN TERMS → ROUTE FOR DEEPER VERIFICATION**

Module 06 owns deeper broker identity, authority, contact, fraud and payment-path verification. Module 05 must prepare the handoff without duplicating Module 06.

## Audit Standard
Audit all seven manual parts, all Module 05 tools, knowledge check, exam/key, practical/scoring, instructor answer material, source/currentness controls and master control against:

1. Google test — free first-page information alone must not replace the instructional value;
2. Employment test — learner must be able to perform observable dispatcher work;
3. Buyer test — module must feel like professional occupational training worth purchasing;
4. continuity test — Module 01–04 skills must transfer correctly and Module 06–10 prerequisites must be established without teaching future modules prematurely.

## Required Depth Areas
The premium pass must verify or add, where missing:
- carrier search-profile translation;
- equipment, geography, timing, appointment and operational constraints;
- load-board listing literacy and incomplete-information handling;
- deadhead and total-trip awareness without duplicating Module 07 profitability mathematics;
- first-pass broker/contact/listing verification boundaries;
- duplicate/reposted/changed listing handling;
- suspicious contact and communication-channel handling;
- professional broker-call preparation and information capture;
- load comparison based on operational fit and known/unknown/verify states;
- rate-confirmation discrepancy recognition without silently rewriting terms;
- authority to sign/process/escalate written terms;
- STOP/VERIFY/ESCALATE decision discipline;
- evidence logging and handoff to Module 06;
- realistic beginner failure modes and consequences;
- professional language expected at a dispatch desk.

## Existing Assets to Preserve and Synchronize
The current seven manual parts and nine operational tools are the baseline. Existing C-013 additions — entity-versus-contact verification and rate-confirmation discrepancy review — remain part of the chain unless the audit finds a factual or alignment defect.

The assessment chain must remain synchronized to any manual/tool changes. No premium additions may exist only in an exam or simulation.

## Instructor Product Layer
Build a full module-specific Instructor Guide conforming to `00-ACADEMY-MASTER/INSTRUCTOR-GUIDE-PRODUCT-STANDARD.md`. It must include instructor preparation, employment outcome/competency map, lesson-by-lesson teaching notes, professional-language coaching, activity/tool facilitation, assessment administration, critical-failure controls, diagnostic remediation, current-source notes, SIM-005 alignment contract, and module closeout.

An answer key or scoring guide is not a substitute.

## SIM-005 Unlock Rule
Do not design/build SIM-005 until the regular Module 05 learning chain and premium/instructor alignment are synchronized and independently checked. The later simulation must be based on the repaired Module 05 competency chain, not used to compensate for missing instruction.

## Release Control
Passing this premium/instructor gate does not make Module 05 `PUBLICATION_READY`. Final current-source, editorial, branding, accessibility, assembled-product and LMS/release QA remain separate controls.
