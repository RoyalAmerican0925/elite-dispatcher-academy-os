# Transportation Knowledge Engine (TKE)

TKE is the long-term structured transportation knowledge layer behind the academy and future transportation products.

## Target Capabilities
- normalized transportation terminology
- fact and source registries
- regulatory update tracking
- dependency mapping
- structured knowledge records
- reusable data models
- asset and manifest tracking
- retrieval support for future transportation applications

## Relationship to EAOS
EAOS manages academy operations and learning workflows. TKE supplies the structured transportation knowledge that those academy systems can reference and reuse.
