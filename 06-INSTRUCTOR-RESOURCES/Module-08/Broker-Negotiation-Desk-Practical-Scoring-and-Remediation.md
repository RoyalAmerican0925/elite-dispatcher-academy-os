- **0:** absent, materially wrong, or unsafe/unprofessional for the supplied role.

## Critical Failures
A critical failure overrides raw-score pass until remediated and reassessed:
1. knowingly accepts below supplied/revised floor without authorization;
2. fabricates leverage or a competing offer;
3. materially misrepresents truck/driver/equipment/load facts;
4. commits carrier outside supplied authority;
5. treats unresolved material term as agreed;
6. represents verbal negotiation/agreement-in-principle as completed BOOKED status before Module 09.

## Expected Event Logic
- Event 2: “everybody” remains broker statement; “2-ish” should be clarified.
- Event 4: learner must not invent a competing offer.
- Event 6: $2,425 is below original $2,450 floor; “should be quick” is not established guaranteed value.
- Event 7: detention requires four-part clarification; unresolved pieces stay visible.
- Event 8: $2,450 is floor, not target.
- Event 9: added stop is material; learner should VERIFY/re-evaluate. Instructor then supplies revised floor $2,525.
- Event 10: $2,500 is below revised floor; without further authorization learner cannot accept. Broker's later $2,525 explicitly establishes final rate movement.
- Event 11: $2,525 and confirmed stops/delivery may be agreed in principle; detention evidence/process remains unresolved. Untouched terms remain not established.
- Event 12: strongest supported proceeding status is AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL, subject to the learner accurately preserving unresolved items for Module 09.

## Professional Communication Standard
Full-credit communication is concise, truthful, specific, authority-aware and non-combative. Do not require one canned phrase. Equivalent professional language earns credit when the operational meaning is correct.

## First-Attempt Evidence
Instructor records before coaching:
- raw score;
- criterion scores;
- critical-failure events;
- learner artifacts;
- exact decision that triggered remediation.

Do not overwrite first-attempt evidence with corrected work.

## Remediation
1. Identify failed competency/decision pattern.
2. Assign exact manual section/tool review.
3. Require learner to explain why the first decision failed.
4. Give a materially equivalent but not identical reassessment event.
5. Record corrected decision separately.
6. Critical failure is cleared only when reassessment demonstrates the corrected behavior.
