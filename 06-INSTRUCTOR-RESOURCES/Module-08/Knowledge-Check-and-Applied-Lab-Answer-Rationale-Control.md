## Diagnostic Interpretation
Do not use a single raw percentage as the only remediation decision.

- Preparation/economics/position misses → return to Parts 01–02 and Tools 01–02.
- Truthful communication/discovery misses → return to Part 03 and Tool 03.
- Counter/trade misses → return to Part 04 and Tools 04–05.
- Term/authority misses → return to Parts 05–06 and Tools 05/07.
- Closeout/boundary misses → return to Part 06 and Tools 06–07.

Any response endorsing fabricated leverage, material misrepresentation, unauthorized carrier commitment, false agreement state, unauthorized below-floor acceptance, or premature BOOKED status requires targeted correction before applied practice.

# Part B — Applied Lab Instructor Control

## Stage 1 — Preparation
Strong evidence identifies carrier/truck, authority, target $2,500, floor $2,300, below-floor approval requirement, material unknown appointment/terms, truthful leverage only, and explicit VERIFY/ESCALATE/DECLINE triggers.

**Diagnostic failure:** learner treats opening rate as floor, invents market data, or ignores unknown appointment.

## Stage 2 — Discovery
Strong response does not accept “morning,” “everybody,” or “not much room” as independently verified facts. Learner asks a purposeful question about appointment and/or broker flexibility/constraint without accusation.

**Weak response:** “That's not true” or immediate concession to $2,100.

## Stage 3 — Counter
Counter should be defensible from the supplied position. Learner may be concise. Do not require disclosure of carrier cost structure. The number must not be random or supported by fabricated claims.

## Stage 4 — Resistance
Artificial urgency does not alter authority. Silence after a counter does not itself justify a concession. Strong learner remains professional and listens/questions/holds as appropriate.

## Stage 5 — Conditional Trade
$2,250 is below the supplied floor. “Supposed to be drop-and-hook” is not established. Learner must not accept below floor based on an unconfirmed add-on. A conditional response should make any movement dependent on actual confirmed value and remain inside authority.

## Stage 6 — Agreement-Specific Term
Full credit requires the learner to seek/record all four applicable elements:
1. trigger;
2. amount/method;
3. evidence;
4. process/timing.

Do not award full credit for “get detention in writing.” Do not impose an Academy-wide dollar amount or waiting period.

## Stage 7 — Floor
$2,300 equals floor, not target. Rate can be inside authority while another material term remains unresolved. Learner should continue closeout discipline rather than treating the number alone as the entire agreement.

## Stage 8 — Material Fact Change
The move from 10:00 AM to 6:00 AM is material. Correct status is VERIFY BEFORE DECISION until fit/economics/position are re-established. Continuing from the stale position without review is an occupational judgment failure.

## Stage 9 — Authority
$2,275 is below floor. Without authorization, accepting it is a critical failure. After the instructor supplies that floor remains $2,300 and 6:00 AM is workable, the broker's explicit return to $2,300 creates a coherent evidence chain to final closeout.

## Stage 10 — Three-Bucket Closeout
Expected from supplied progressive facts:

**AGREED IN PRINCIPLE:** $2,300 rate; confirmed 6:00 AM pickup; other facts only if the learner can point to actual establishment in the case.

**UNRESOLVED:** detention specifics.

**NOT DISCUSSED / NOT ESTABLISHED:** drop-and-hook remains not confirmed; any other untouched term stays here rather than being invented.

Critical error: upgrading unresolved/not-established terms to agreed.

## Stage 11 — Decision + Handoff
Expected status after final supplied event: **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL**, assuming learner's record contains no unresolved item that prevents proceeding under the supplied case.

The handoff must identify unresolved/document-sensitive terms for Module 09. It must NOT say BOOKED merely because verbal negotiation aligned.

# Remediation Rules
- Preserve the learner's first attempt.
- Mark the reasoning defect, not only the wrong answer.
- Assign the relevant manual/tool review.
- Require a corrected decision plus explanation of what changed.
- Do not erase a first-attempt critical-failure pattern because the learner later corrected it.
- This lab is formative; final graded competency is controlled by the later practical/SIM evidence.

# Critical-Failure Warnings
Escalate for targeted remediation if the learner:
1. knowingly accepts below floor without authorization;
2. fabricates leverage;
3. materially misrepresents carrier/truck/driver facts;
4. commits carrier outside supplied authority;
5. treats unresolved material term as agreed;
6. represents negotiation as completed booking before Module 09 control.
