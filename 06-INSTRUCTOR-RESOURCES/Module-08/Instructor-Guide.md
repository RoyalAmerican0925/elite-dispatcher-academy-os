# Module 08 — Instructor Guide
## Rate Negotiation and Broker Communication

**Product status:** Production build active. This guide supports instructional delivery and assessment; it does not make the module PUBLICATION_READY.  
**Employment outcome:** Learner can prepare a broker rate conversation, use supplied Module 07 economics, obtain material facts, negotiate professionally and truthfully within carrier authority, document the negotiation state, make an accept/decline/escalate decision, and hand an agreed-in-principle opportunity to Module 09 without falsely representing it as booked.

## 1. Instructor Control Header

### Required workflow
**PREPARE → VERIFY FACTS → SET POSITION → OPEN → QUESTION → LISTEN → COUNTER → TRADE → CONFIRM → DOCUMENT → ACCEPT / DECLINE / ESCALATE**

Do not teach this as a magic script. It is a decision sequence. Students may use different professional wording if their reasoning, facts, authority, and documentation are sound.

### Competencies
- M08-C01 build negotiation position from verified load/carrier facts
- M08-C02 convert supplied M07 economics into target/floor/walk-away controls
- M08-C03 distinguish asking/target/floor/walk-away
- M08-C04 open professionally and truthfully
- M08-C05 perform purposeful discovery of material unknowns
- M08-C06 listen/classify constraints, objections, assumptions and evidence
- M08-C07 make evidence-supported counters
- M08-C08 use truthful leverage; no fabrication
- M08-C09 make controlled conditional trades/concessions
- M08-C10 manage agreement-specific accessorial/operating terms
- M08-C11 handle resistance professionally
- M08-C12 remain inside carrier authority and escalate when required
- M08-C13 confirm agreed/unresolved/not-established terms accurately
- M08-C14 document and hand off without false booking completion
- M08-C15 decline professionally when warranted

### Six critical failures
1. knowingly accepting below supplied carrier minimum without authorization;
2. fabricating leverage or competing offers;
3. materially misrepresenting truck, driver, equipment, or load facts;
4. committing the carrier outside supplied authority;
5. treating an unresolved material term as agreed;
6. representing verbal negotiation as completed booking before Module 09 controls.

A learner can sound confident and still fail occupationally. Judge the decision, not charisma.

## 2. Prerequisites and Instructor Preparation
Before instruction, verify the learner has completed the required Module 07 profitability work or has been supplied equivalent exercise economics. Module 08 does not reteach profitability mathematics; it teaches how to use the resulting position during negotiation.

Instructor should review before class:
1. all six Module 08 Student Manual parts;
2. seven Module 08 operational tools;
3. Knowledge Check and rationale control;
4. progressive Broker Negotiation Applied Lab;
5. 30-item Final Exam/key;
6. Broker Negotiation Desk Practical/scoring control;
7. current SIM-008 curriculum contract/status before simulation facilitation.

Stage progressive exercises so later events are not visible early. Students must make decisions from information available at that moment.

## 3. Lesson Delivery — Part 01: Negotiation Foundations & Professional Positioning
### Teaching objective
Move the learner away from “get the highest rate” thinking toward disciplined representation of a carrier position.

Teach asking rate, target, floor and walk-away/approval threshold as different controls. A broker's opening number is the broker's current stated position; it does not become the carrier's economics.

### Demonstrate
Give a posted rate plus supplied target/floor. Ask the learner to identify each number and what authority attaches to it. Change the broker opening while leaving carrier economics unchanged. The student should see that a broker number does not rewrite the carrier file.

### Common reasoning defects
- treating broker asking/opening as market truth;
- confusing target with minimum;
- believing “close enough” permits below-floor acceptance;
- thinking urgency expands authority;
- believing aggressive language is negotiation skill.

### Coaching language
Weak: “That's way too low. You need to come up.”  
Stronger: concise position language that states the carrier's current position and invites useful movement without invented claims.

Do not force one sentence. Ask: **What fact, authority, or position supports what you just said?**

## 4. Part 02: Pre-Negotiation Intelligence
### Teaching objective
The learner must prepare five groups before bargaining: carrier position, load facts, economics, alternatives/leverage, and negotiation objective.

Use Tools 01 and 02. Require VERIFIED / BROKER STATED / UNKNOWN classification where appropriate. An unknown that can materially alter fit/economics is not administrative trivia.

### Instructor diagnostic
Remove one material fact from a case. Ask whether the learner can still set a defensible position. Strong students explain what can be prepared now and what must remain conditional.

### Correction cue
When a student jumps to a number too quickly, ask: **What changed in the facts that makes that number defensible?**

## 5. Part 03: Broker Call Discovery
### Teaching objective
Teach **OPEN → QUESTION → LISTEN → VERIFY → SUMMARIZE → POSITION** inside the larger Module 08 workflow.

Discovery is not interrogation. Questions should reduce uncertainty, expose constraints, or clarify terms that could affect the decision.

### Facilitation
Use Tool 03. Give statements such as “everyone runs it for this,” “easy load,” “morning pickup,” or “quick unload.” Require students to classify what was actually established versus merely stated.

### Key distinction
A broker statement may be relevant evidence of what the broker is saying without becoming an independently verified fact. Students should neither blindly accept nor automatically accuse.

### Negotiating against yourself
After a learner counters, pause. If the learner immediately lowers the number without new information/value, stop the exercise and diagnose why. Silence alone is not a concession request backed by new evidence.

## 6. Part 04: Counteroffers & Value-Based Negotiation
### Teaching objective
Build counters from prepared position and truthful facts, not random anchoring or fabricated leverage.

Use Tool 04 and the structure:
**POSITION → BASIS → BOUNDARY → QUESTION**

This is a thinking structure, not a mandatory speech. Evidence-grounded does not require disclosing internal costs or giving a long justification on every counter.

### Conditional trades
Use Tool 05. Teach IF/THEN exchanges. A concession given without receiving value or useful information is not automatically strategic.

### Fabrication drill
Invite the learner to claim a competing offer when none is supplied. The correct behavior is to refuse the false premise and negotiate from real position/leverage. Treat fabrication as a critical failure, not clever salesmanship.

## 7. Part 05: Terms Beyond Headline Rate
### Teaching objective
Prevent students from treating rate as the entire deal.

For agreement-specific terms use:
**TRIGGER → AMOUNT/METHOD → EVIDENCE → PROCESS**

Examples may include detention or other case-specific operating/accessorial terms, but do not teach a universal Academy dollar amount, waiting period, or documentation rule unless the actual agreement/source establishes it.

### Instructor drill
Say: “We take care of good carriers on detention.” Ask what is missing. Full reasoning should seek the applicable trigger, amount/method, evidence requirement, and process.

“Get it in writing” alone is incomplete reasoning because it does not identify what must actually be established.

## 8. Part 06: Closing, Documentation & Difficult Conversations
### Teaching objective
Teach an accurate evidence state instead of a cosmetically complete file.

Use three closeout buckets:
- **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE**
- **UNRESOLVED**
- **NOT DISCUSSED / NOT ESTABLISHED**

Use Tools 06 and 07. Require chronological evidence when broker positions or material facts change.

### Material fact change
If appointment, stops, equipment requirement, or another material fact changes, the student must recognize that a previously prepared position may be stale. Correct status may become **VERIFY BEFORE DECISION** until the position is re-established.

### Module 09 boundary
Module 08 may conclude with **AGREED IN PRINCIPLE / PENDING BOOKING CONTROL**. Do not let students label verbal alignment BOOKED. Module 09 owns booking/rate-confirmation/document control.

## 9. Seven-Tool Facilitation Map
1. **Negotiation Preparation Sheet** — establish carrier/load/economic/evidence state before bargaining.
2. **Target/Floor/Walk-Away Worksheet** — prevent number and authority confusion.
3. **Broker Discovery Call Sheet** — capture purposeful questions and statement-vs-fact distinctions.
4. **Counteroffer Builder** — create defensible concise counters.
5. **Concession & Trade Log** — preserve movement and conditional exchanges.
6. **Negotiation Call Record** — reconstruct chronology and three-bucket closeout.
7. **Accept/Decline/Escalate Decision Record** — force a supported status and Module 09 next action.

Do not have students fill tools mechanically after the case. Their value is strongest when used at the point in the workflow where the decision occurs.

## 10. Knowledge Check Administration
The 15-item Knowledge Check is diagnostic, not the final exam. Group misses by competency family. Do not rely only on raw percentage.

When correcting an answer, require the learner to explain the reasoning defect: unsupported assumption, authority error, evidence-classification error, concession error, false agreement state, or boundary error.

Critical-pattern answers require targeted correction before the progressive lab.

## 11. Progressive Applied Lab Facilitation
Release the Broker Negotiation Applied Lab events sequentially. Do not preview later broker movement or fact changes.

Instructor should observe:
- preparation before opening;
- purposeful discovery;
- response to silence/resistance;
- whether conditional value is actually established;
- four-part term control;
- response when appointment materially changes;
- response to below-floor pressure;
- three-bucket closeout;
- Module 09 handoff.

Do not coach the correct decision during the first attempt. Preserve evidence, then remediate.

## 12. Final Exam Administration
- 30 items
- pass = 27/30
- critical items = 8, 14, 20, 24, 27, 29

A learner who scores 27–30 but misses a critical item requires targeted remediation. Preserve first-attempt result and reassessment separately.

Do not give the instructor answer key as student study material.

## 13. Broker Negotiation Desk Practical
### Standard
30 points. Pass = 24/30 **and zero unresolved critical failures**.

The practical is an integrated occupational performance task. The learner must use facts, economics, discovery, countering, terms, authority, changing conditions, documentation, and handoff together.

### Administration rule
Release events progressively. Do not tell the learner a later broker offer is coming. Do not rescue the learner from silence or urgency. Observe what they do.

### Communication scoring
Do not score accent, personality, or one preferred script. Score whether communication is concise, truthful, professional, specific, and authority-aware.

### Critical failure handling
Stop or flag the event according to assessment procedure, preserve the exact first-attempt decision, and assign targeted remediation. A corrected second answer does not erase the first critical event.

## 14. Diagnostic Remediation
Use this sequence:
**IDENTIFY DEFECT → TRACE COMPETENCY → REVIEW MANUAL/TOOL → EXPLAIN WHY → REASSESS WITH EQUIVALENT NEW EVENT → RECORD RESULT**

Examples:
- fabricated leverage → Part 04 + C08 + Counteroffer/Trade tools;
- below-floor acceptance → Parts 01/06 + C12 + Target/Floor and Decision tools;
- vague detention marked agreed → Part 05 + C10/C13 + Trade/Call Record;
- premature BOOKED status → Part 06 + C14 + Call/Decision records.

Remediation should change the reasoning, not teach memorization of the previous answer.

## 15. SIM-008 Facilitation Contract
SIM-008 should test the same curriculum under a more realistic progressive environment. Before facilitation, confirm the current runtime/build has been synchronized to the locked M08 curriculum and current critical-failure set.

The simulation must not reward:
- invented leverage;
- false truck/driver facts;
- unauthorized commitments;
- below-floor acceptance without authority;
- false agreement-state classification;
- premature booking claims.

Simulation evidence supplements, not replaces, the module assessment record. Runtime/release QA status is separate from curriculum completion.

## 16. Source and Currentness Controls
Module 08 primarily teaches operational judgment and communication, but instructors must distinguish durable reasoning principles from changeable external facts.

Do not present a market rate, broker policy, accessorial amount, waiting period, platform practice, or legal/regulatory requirement as universal merely because it appeared in an example. When instruction depends on a current external rule or policy, use the Academy's approved current-source control and verify it before release/teaching.

Do not turn a broker's statement into a verified regulatory, contractual, or market fact.

## 17. Module Closeout
Before progression, record:
- Knowledge Check diagnostic/remediation state;
- Final Exam first attempt and any critical-item remediation;
- Practical first-attempt score and critical failures;
- reassessment evidence where required;
- SIM-008 competency evidence/status when applicable;
- M08-C01–C15 competency state;
- unresolved instructional concerns.

The learner should be able to explain the difference between a good negotiated opportunity and a completed booking.

### Required handoff statement
**NEGOTIATION RESULT → DOCUMENTED STATUS → MODULE 09 BOOKING/RATE-CONFIRMATION CONTROL**

## 18. Instructor Closeout Questions
Before marking instructional competency achieved, ask:
1. Can the learner build a position without letting the broker opening rewrite carrier economics?
2. Can the learner discover material facts rather than negotiate from assumptions?
3. Can the learner counter without fabricated leverage or unnecessary cost disclosure?
4. Can the learner trade conditionally instead of conceding reflexively?
5. Can the learner clarify agreement-specific terms without inventing universal rules?
6. Can the learner stop/re-evaluate after a material fact change?
7. Can the learner remain inside carrier authority under pressure?
8. Can the learner preserve agreed, unresolved and not-established terms accurately?
9. Can the learner decline professionally?
10. Can the learner hand the opportunity to Module 09 without falsely calling it booked?

If the answer to a material competency is no, the module requires remediation regardless of how polished the learner sounds.
