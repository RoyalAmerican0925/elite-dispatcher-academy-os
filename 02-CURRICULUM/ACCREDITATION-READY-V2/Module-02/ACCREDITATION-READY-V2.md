# Module 02 — Dispatcher Role & Legal Boundaries
## Accreditation-Ready Version 2

**Status:** Accreditation-readiness revision; not an accreditation claim.
**Source body:** Existing Module 02 student manual and instructor answer guide remain the instructional content body unless expressly superseded by a later V2 revision.
**Version:** 2.0-AR
**Review date:** 2026-08-15

## Module purpose
Develop the learner's ability to distinguish carrier-side dispatch activity from freight brokerage and to recognize when facts require verification, escalation, or qualified legal guidance.

## Measurable learning outcomes
By the end of this module, the learner will be able to:
1. Distinguish motor-carrier, broker, and carrier-side dispatcher functions using federal definitions and the facts of a scenario.
2. Explain the bona fide agent concept and the significance of a preexisting continuing carrier relationship.
3. Identify allocation-of-traffic facts that increase brokerage risk.
4. Analyze multiple-carrier scenarios without relying on unsupported numerical safe harbors.
5. Distinguish regulatory text from FMCSA guidance and industry practice.
6. Identify compensation, money-flow, shipper-solicitation, and agreement facts that may affect the broker/agent analysis.
7. Recognize when a situation exceeds dispatcher competence and requires hold/escalation rather than an unsupported legal conclusion.
8. Explain the existence of separate broker-registration and financial-security requirements without treating this module as broker-licensing instruction.

## Required student evidence
- Scored legal-boundary scenario set mapped to Outcomes 1–8.
- At least one multi-carrier allocation-of-traffic case analysis.
- At least one source-classification exercise distinguishing statute, regulation, guidance, and practice.
- Written explanation of when the correct response is VERIFY, HOLD, or ESCALATE.

## Assessment alignment
The Module 02 Instructor Answer Guide is the controlled answer-key source. Scenario scoring must reward identification of controlling facts and calibrated escalation, not merely selection of a label such as 'dispatcher' or 'broker.'

## Completion criteria
Learner must meet the academy's approved passing standard and successfully complete the required applied legal-boundary scenarios. Any reassessment must use an equivalent but not identical scenario set.

## Evidence retained
Retain assessment score, scenario completion, remediation/reassessment history, module/assessment version, and any rubric-based competency result.

## Source and revision controls
All federal legal citations, broker financial-security requirements, penalty amounts, and FMCSA guidance must be reverified at publication and on the academy's regulatory-review schedule. The manual's legal-advice disclaimer remains mandatory.
