## Module purpose
Establish the learner's foundational map of the trucking/freight ecosystem, the principal parties, federal transportation entities, registration concepts, load lifecycle, and the carrier-side dispatcher role.

## Measurable learning outcomes
By the end of this module, the learner will be able to:
1. Identify and distinguish shipper, receiver/consignee, motor carrier, driver, dispatcher, freight broker, freight forwarder, and factoring company roles in a freight scenario.
2. Explain the relationship between DOT and FMCSA.
3. Distinguish a USDOT number from operating authority and identify why the distinction matters operationally.
4. Classify basic freight movements as interstate or intrastate using the broader movement facts rather than state-line geography alone.
5. Trace a standard load lifecycle from shipper need through carrier payment documentation.
6. Explain where a carrier-side dispatcher fits in the load lifecycle and distinguish that role from carrier and broker functions.
7. Correct common beginner misconceptions using the terminology and principles taught in the module.

## Required student evidence
- Knowledge assessment mapped to Outcomes 1–7.
- At least one freight-party identification scenario.
- At least one load-lifecycle sequencing exercise.
- At least one registration/authority distinction exercise.
- At least one interstate/intrastate reasoning item.
