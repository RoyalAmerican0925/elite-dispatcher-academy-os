# Module 05 — Load Boards & Freight Sourcing
## Accreditation-Ready Version 2

**Status:** Accreditation-readiness revision; not an accreditation claim.
**Source body:** Existing Module 05 student manual and instructor answer guide remain the instructional content body unless expressly superseded by a later V2 revision.
**Version:** 2.0-AR
**Review date:** 2026-08-15

## Module purpose
Develop the learner's ability to source freight systematically, screen load candidates against the carrier/driver/equipment profile, identify missing information, and route qualified candidates into the correct next-stage workflow.

## Measurable learning outcomes
By the end of this module, the learner will be able to:
1. Identify major freight-sourcing channels and distinguish spot, contract, and dedicated freight at a beginner operational level.
2. Build a load-board search from verified carrier, driver, equipment, geography, and availability criteria.
3. Read a load posting and classify key fields as KNOWN, MISSING, UNCLEAR, or VERIFY.
4. Screen load candidates for equipment fit, carrier/driver restrictions, origin deadhead, timing, and special requirements.
5. Explain why posting age and duplicate-looking freight are signals requiring judgment rather than automatic conclusions.
6. Verify load availability before treating a posting as an available load.
7. Apply the Module 02 legal boundary when considering shipper-direct sourcing.
8. Route a qualified candidate to the correct next stage: broker/fraud screening, rate evaluation, negotiation, booking, trip planning, or HOS feasibility.

## Required student evidence
- Completed load-search setup exercise based on a carrier profile.
- Load-candidate screening worksheet covering multiple postings.
- Missing-information identification and verification exercise.
- At least three scenario decisions with written justification.
- Knowledge assessment mapped to Outcomes 1–8.

## Assessment alignment
The Module 05 Instructor Answer Guide is the controlled answer-key source. Applied scoring must measure whether the learner can eliminate bad fits, identify verification gaps, and route viable opportunities appropriately—not merely recognize terminology.

## Completion criteria
Learner must meet the approved passing standard and achieve a satisfactory result on the load-candidate screening exercise. Material failures involving ignored carrier restrictions, obvious equipment mismatch, or unsupported assumptions require remediation.

## Evidence retained
Retain assessment score, screening exercise/rubric result, remediation/reassessment history, completion status, and exact module/assessment version.

## Source and revision controls
Platform names, features, interfaces, pricing, or functionality are current-date material and must be reverified before publication. Platform-specific information must remain clearly separated from law, regulation, carrier policy, and academy workflow.
