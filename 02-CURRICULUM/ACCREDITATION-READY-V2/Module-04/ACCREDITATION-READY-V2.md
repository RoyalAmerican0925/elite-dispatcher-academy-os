# Module 04 — Carrier & Driver Onboarding
## Accreditation-Ready Version 2

**Status:** Accreditation-readiness revision; not an accreditation claim.
**Source body:** Existing Module 04 student manual and instructor answer guide remain the instructional content body unless expressly superseded by a later V2 revision.
**Version:** 2.0-AR
**Review date:** 2026-08-15

## Module purpose
Develop the learner's ability to build an operationally complete carrier/driver dispatch profile while preserving the distinction between dispatcher readiness tasks and motor-carrier compliance responsibilities.

## Measurable learning outcomes
By the end of this module, the learner will be able to:
1. Distinguish dispatcher operational-readiness tasks from motor-carrier compliance responsibilities.
2. Identify the carrier registration, authority, insurance, tax/payment, driver, equipment, ELD/HOS, and operational-preference information needed for dispatch onboarding.
3. Distinguish a dispatcher operational profile from a driver qualification file.
4. Apply secure payment-change and trusted-channel verification procedures.
5. Identify expiration, mismatch, and reverification issues and assign an appropriate status.
6. Apply carrier and driver onboarding checklists to a realistic case file.
7. Identify when an onboarding issue requires VERIFY, ESCALATE, or DO NOT DISPATCH status.

## Required student evidence
- Completed simulated carrier onboarding file.
- Completed simulated driver operational profile.
- Expiration/reverification tracker exercise.
- Payment-instruction change verification scenario.
- Knowledge assessment mapped to Outcomes 1–7.

## Assessment alignment
The Module 04 Instructor Answer Guide is the controlled answer-key source. Practical onboarding evidence must be evaluated with a rubric that checks completeness, accuracy, role boundaries, security controls, and escalation decisions.

## Completion criteria
Learner must meet the approved assessment passing standard and achieve a satisfactory rubric result on the onboarding case file. A learner who incorrectly assumes carrier compliance authority or approves an unresolved high-risk payment/identity mismatch must remediate before completion.

## Evidence retained
Retain assessment result, onboarding-case rubric, completion status, remediation/reassessment record, and exact content/assessment version.

## Source and revision controls
Current FMCSA registration-system information, medical-certificate transition information, insurance requirements, and other time-sensitive regulatory material must be reverified immediately before publication and on the academy's regulatory-review schedule.

V2 adds measurable outcomes, a required performance-based onboarding artifact, rubric expectations, completion criteria, evidence retention, and formal time-sensitive source controls while retaining the existing substantive manual.
