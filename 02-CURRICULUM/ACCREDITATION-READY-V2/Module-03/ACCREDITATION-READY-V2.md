# Module 03 — Trucks, Trailers, Freight & Equipment

**Status:** Accreditation-readiness revision; not an accreditation claim.

**Version:** 2.0-AR
**Review date:** 2026-08-15

## Module purpose
Develop dispatcher-level equipment and freight-fit judgment so learners can identify when a load can be screened confidently and when missing, specialized, state-specific, or safety-sensitive facts require verification or escalation.

## Measurable learning outcomes
By the end of this module, the learner will be able to:
1. Distinguish major tractor, trailer, and specialized-equipment categories relevant to dispatching.
2. Match freight characteristics to equipment requirements using actual dimensions, loading method, temperature, weight, and configuration facts.
3. Explain the difference among GVW, GVWR, GCWR, tare weight, payload, gross weight, and axle-weight concepts.
4. Identify why gross weight alone does not establish legal loadability.
5. Recognize when state-specific size/height/permit requirements or specialized freight rules require additional verification.
6. Identify unsafe or unsupported assumptions involving nominal trailer dimensions, reefer capacity, open-deck fit, hotshot operations, food transport, hazmat, and specialized equipment.
7. Classify a freight/equipment scenario as FIT, NOT FIT, VERIFY, or ESCALATE and justify the decision.

## Required student evidence
- Equipment-identification assessment.
- Freight/equipment fit matrix exercise.
- At least three applied screening scenarios requiring justification.
- One missing-information exercise requiring the learner to identify facts that must be verified before booking.

## Assessment alignment
The Module 03 Instructor Answer Guide is the controlled answer-key source. Scoring must distinguish factual recall from applied equipment-fit judgment.

## Completion criteria
Learner must meet the approved passing standard and demonstrate acceptable performance on the applied screening exercise. Failure to identify a material safety/legal verification need requires remediation before module completion.

## Evidence retained
Retain assessment score, applied-exercise result, remediation/reassessment history, and exact module/assessment version.

## Source and revision controls
Federal size/weight rules, specialized freight references, and any state-specific or configuration-specific examples must be source-classified and reviewed at publication. V2 preserves the manual's repeated rule against booking from weight or nominal dimensions alone.
