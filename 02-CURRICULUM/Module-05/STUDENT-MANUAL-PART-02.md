**FACT-M05-006.** Before pursuing any candidate, run the fit screen using what you already know from Modules 03 and 04: equipment type, weight, dimensions, loading method, carrier restrictions, lane restrictions, driver availability, home time, hazmat, temperature, team/solo status, and origin deadhead.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not pitch a load to a carrier or driver that obviously fails this screen. Screening out clear bad fits before they reach a human being is the core time-saving skill this module builds.

# 13\. Carrier / Driver Profile Screen

Every candidate needs to be checked specifically against the profile you built in Module 04 — not against a generic idea of "good freight." A load that would be an excellent fit for one carrier can be a clear rejection for another, purely based on that carrier's own restrictions and preferences.

# 14\. Missing Information

**FACT-M05-008.** Required workflow when a posting is incomplete: do not assume → identify the gap → verify → document → continue or reject.

Common categories of missing information: exact origin, exact destination, commodity, weight, dimensions, stop count, loading method, unloading method, pickup hours, delivery hours, appointment/FCFS details, temperature, hazmat, special handling, equipment requirements, team/solo status, and accessorial expectations.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not make students or yourself guess at missing information to keep a deal moving. A gap gets verified or the load gets set aside — it doesn't get filled in with an assumption.

# 15\. Posting Age

**FACT-M05-004.** Posting age is a priority signal — it helps you decide what to look at first. It is NOT proof that a load is available or unavailable.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not teach universal age thresholds like "under 15 minutes is good" or "14 hours is stale" or "24 hours means unavailable." Platform refresh and removal behavior varies by platform and by poster — a load's actual availability always has to be verified directly.

# 16\. Duplicate / Reposted Freight

**FACT-M05-005.** Similar-looking listings from multiple sources can have many different explanations.

* Separate legitimate freight that simply looks similar.
* Authorized co-brokering between two legitimate parties.
* A shipper tendering the same freight through multiple channels at once.
* A reposted or duplicated platform record with no ill intent.
* Unauthorized brokerage or fraud — a real possibility, but only one of several.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not diagnose double brokering just because two postings look alike. Flag it, verify the basic details, and route it to Module 06 for fraud screening if something remains genuinely unresolved.

# 17\. Availability Verification

**FACT-M05-009.** FOUND does not equal AVAILABLE.

A posting must be confirmed through the posting party or the applicable platform booking process before it's treated as currently available. Do not assume a load is still open simply because the posting remains visible.

# 18\. Broker Information at the Sourcing Stage

At this early stage, you may reasonably note: the broker/company name, MC number when displayed, a contact method, any internal Do-Not-Use restriction your company already maintains, and whatever basic information the platform itself provides.

**IMPORTANT:** Do not perform a complete fraud investigation at the sourcing stage. Identity verification, authority verification, fraud-indicator analysis, double-brokering investigation, suspicious contact-change detection, broker impersonation, and payment/credit risk assessment are Module 06 territory.

# 19\. Shipper-Direct Sourcing — the Module 02 Boundary

**FACT-M05-010.** A dispatcher operating as a qualifying carrier-side agent may seek freight for the carrier they represent — including, where appropriate, directly from a shipper.

**IMPORTANT:** This does not license everything that might be called "shipper-direct sourcing." Do not teach independent dispatchers to solicit freight generally for an undefined network, to secure freight first and find a carrier for it afterward, to allocate shipper freight among multiple competing represented carriers, or to hold themselves out as a general transportation arranger.

# 20\. Search Workflow

A repeatable workflow for sourcing:

1. Carrier ready
2. Driver ready
3. Equipment ready
4. Define search parameters
5. Search sources
6. Filter results
7. Remove obvious bad fits
8. Shortlist candidates
9. Review postings
10. Identify missing information
11. Verify availability / details
12. Route qualified candidate to next stage

Possible next stages: Module 06, 07, 08, 09, 10, or 11 depending on what the candidate needs.

**IMPORTANT:** Real company workflows may overlap these steps or run them in a different order — this sequence is a teaching model, not a rigid procedure every dispatch desk follows identically.

# 21\. Common Beginner Mistakes

* Treating a posting as guaranteed available because it's still showing on the board.
* Assuming FCFS means the facility is always open.
* Filling in missing information with a guess instead of verifying it.
* Jumping to a fraud conclusion from a duplicate-looking posting instead of flagging and verifying.
* Pitching a load that obviously fails the carrier's own stated restrictions.
* Treating posting age as a hard cutoff rather than a priority signal.

# 22\. Scenario Lab

### S-05-01: Equipment Mismatch — Van vs. Flatbed

A carrier has a dry van available. A posting explicitly requires flatbed/open-deck equipment with a 4-foot tarp requirement.

**Classification:** BAD FIT / REJECT.

This is a straightforward equipment-type mismatch.

### S-05-02: Deadhead Exceeds Carrier Policy

The carrier's profile caps origin deadhead at 100 miles. The candidate load's pickup is 350 miles from the truck's current position.

**Classification:** BAD FIT / REJECT based on carrier policy.

### S-05-03: Restricted Lane

The carrier's profile states "no Northeast." The candidate load delivers to Brooklyn, New York.

**Classification:** BAD FIT / REJECT.

### S-05-04: Missing Commodity

A posting shows origin, destination, weight, and equipment type, but no commodity description.

**Classification:** NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY.

### S-05-05: FCFS Without Facility Hours

A posting lists delivery as FCFS but does not state the facility's operating hours.

**Classification:** NEEDS MORE INFORMATION / VERIFY.

### S-05-06: Older Posting Still Visible

A posting that appears to be several hours old is still showing as active on the board.

**Classification:** VERIFY AVAILABILITY / lower priority depending on alternatives.
