# Appendix A: Module 05 — Terminology Ledger Candidates

### Load Board

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** A digital freight-sourcing or freight-matching environment where users can locate available freight or capacity.

**CLASSIFICATION:** INDUSTRY / PLATFORM TERM

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 05, Section 4 (FACT-M05-001)

**RELATED TERMS:** Freight Matching, Broker Portal

**NOTES:** A posting is a lead, not a guarantee of availability or a binding offer.

### Freight Matching

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Digital or automated pairing of available freight with available capacity.

**CLASSIFICATION:** INDUSTRY / PLATFORM TERM

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 05, Section 2

**RELATED TERMS:** Load Board

**NOTES:** Mechanics vary by platform — [PLATFORM-SPECIFIC].

### Spot Market

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** The market for freight priced/tendered outside a longer-term contracted arrangement, often load-by-load.

**CLASSIFICATION:** INDUSTRY TERM

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 05, Section 3 (FACT-M05-002)

**RELATED TERMS:** Contract Freight, Dedicated Freight

**NOTES:** Not synonymous with "emergency freight" — see Section 3 qualification.

### Contract Freight
