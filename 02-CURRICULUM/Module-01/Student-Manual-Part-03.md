Appendix A: Module 01 Glossary
Term
Definition
Shipper
The party that owns the freight and needs it moved from an origin point to a destination.
Receiver / Consignee
The party who receives the freight at its destination.
Motor Carrier
An entity that provides motor-vehicle transportation of property for compensation.
Driver
The individual who physically operates the commercial motor vehicle.
Dispatcher
In the carrier-side dispatch model taught in this academy, the individual who plans, coordinates, and manages freight movement and driver activity on behalf of a motor carrier.
Freight Broker
An entity that arranges or offers to arrange transportation by authorized motor carriers for compensation, without itself transporting the freight.
Freight Forwarder
An entity that performs additional transportation-related functions and assumes responsibility for the freight from receipt to destination, as defined under federal law.
Factoring Company
A company that purchases a carrier's outstanding invoices at a discount, providing the carrier faster access to cash.
USDOT Number
An identifier assigned to a regulated entity for FMCSA safety and registration purposes.
Operating Authority
Federal registration authorizing a carrier or broker to conduct specified regulated transportation operations, historically identified by docket numbers such as MC, FF, or MX.
Interstate Commerce
Transportation that crosses state or international boundaries, or that is one leg of a larger movement that does.
Intrastate Commerce
Transportation that occurs entirely within a single state and is not part of a broader interstate movement.
For-Hire Carrier
A motor carrier that transports property belonging to others in exchange for compensation.
Private Carrier
A motor carrier that generally transports its own property as part of its own business operations.
FMCSA
Federal Motor Carrier Safety Administration — the federal agency within the U.S. Department of Transportation focused on commercial motor vehicle safety, registration, and oversight.
