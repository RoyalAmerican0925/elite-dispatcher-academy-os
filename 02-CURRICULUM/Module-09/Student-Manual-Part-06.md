# Module 09 Student Manual — Part 06
## Booking Exceptions, Closeout & M10 Handoff

### Employment outcome
The learner can decide when the booking file is ready, when it must stop, and how to create a traceable closeout without pretending later operational work is complete.

## 1. Final M09 states
- **BOOKED / DISPATCH PACKET READY** — all required M09 controls are satisfied.
- **HOLD** — identified evidence/control must be satisfied before advancement.
- **CORRECT** — a controlling document/evidence defect requires correction.
- **ESCALATE** — supplied authority/evidence is insufficient for dispatcher decision.
- **DECLINE/CANCEL PER AUTHORITY** — only when supplied authority/process supports that action.

## 2. Readiness gate
Before BOOKED/READY, confirm:
1. correct load/carrier/document identity;
2. current controlling confirmation;
3. all material terms extracted;
4. verbal/written reconciliation complete;
5. material discrepancies resolved or authorized;
6. acceptance/signature within supplied authority;
7. document integrity/version history preserved;
8. booking record complete;
9. dispatch packet current and internally consistent;
10. later-workflow issues clearly handed off rather than silently assumed.

If one required control is unresolved, do not use a false completed status.

## 3. Exception examples
### Broker will not correct a material mismatch
Document the request/response. Use HOLD/ESCALATE or decline/cancel only within supplied authority. Do not edit the confirmation yourself.

### Carrier approves the changed term
Record who approved, source/time/scope, reconcile the controlling version, and continue if all other controls pass.

### Broker sends a third version after acceptance
Do not automatically replace the controlling record. Identify what changed, whether acceptance must be revisited, and whether authority/correction is required.

### Driver asks for route and whether legal hours work
Record the booked appointments; hand detailed route/time/HOS feasibility to M10/M11. Do not guess.

## 4. Worked closeout
Fictional case ends with RC v3 matching the supplied approved terms. Carrier instruction authorizes dispatcher acceptance when all material terms match. Dispatcher records acceptance at 15:22, marks v1/v2 superseded, builds packet from v3, and records a flag: "M10 verify trip feasibility against booked Tue 14:00 / Wed 10:00 appointments."

Correct final M09 state: **BOOKED / DISPATCH PACKET READY — HAND OFF TO M10**.

This does not certify the trip plan, HOS, delivery completion, POD, or invoice.

## 5. Critical failures are history, not erasers
If a learner commits the carrier outside authority, uses a known obsolete document, fabricates evidence, or falsely marks a load booked, correcting the original screen does not make the event disappear. The learner must complete a materially different reassessment for that critical family. The original trigger remains in the competency record as resolved or unresolved.

## 6. Coached practice
You have a clean current confirmation, but the carrier's supplied policy says the owner must approve any pickup-time change. The latest version contains a changed pickup time and no owner approval is recorded.

Correct final state: **HOLD / ESCALATE**, not BOOKED. The document may be internally clean and still fail the authority gate.

## 7. Professional closeout record
Record:
- final status;
- controlling document/version;
- acceptance/approval evidence;
- unresolved flags, if any;
- packet issued/time/recipient;
- correction notices sent;
- M10 handoff facts;
- critical/reassessment history when this is a training record.

### Module 09 operating standard
**A load is not ready because someone wants it to be ready. It is ready when the booking evidence, authority, document version, and handoff controls support that conclusion.**
