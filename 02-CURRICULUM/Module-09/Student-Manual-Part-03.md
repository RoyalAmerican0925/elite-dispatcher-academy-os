# Module 09 Student Manual — Part 03
## Verbal vs Written Reconciliation & Discrepancy Control

### Employment outcome
The learner can compare the controlled M08 handoff with written booking evidence, classify differences, and run a correction/escalation loop without guessing.

## 1. Reconciliation matrix
For every material field record:
**TERM → M08/SOURCE EVIDENCE → WRITTEN RC EVIDENCE → STATUS → MATERIALITY → REQUIRED ACTION → RESOLUTION EVIDENCE**

Statuses:
- MATCH
- MISMATCH — MATERIAL
- MISMATCH — NONMATERIAL / CLERICAL
- UNKNOWN / MISSING
- LATER-WORKFLOW ISSUE

## 2. Material discrepancy examples
Depending on supplied facts:
- $2,450 negotiated vs $2,250 written;
- no driver assist negotiated vs driver assist written;
- Tue 14:00 pickup vs Tue 08:00 written;
- Carrier A negotiated vs Carrier B named;
- dry van negotiated vs reefer written;
- one load/reference number negotiated vs another confirmation attached.

Do not accept the document and "remember" the verbal version. The controlled file must show what was corrected or authorized.

## 3. Nonmaterial/clerical does not mean invisible
Example: broker contact's first name is misspelled but entity/contact identity is otherwise verified and the typo changes no obligation. Record it as clerical. Correct when appropriate. Do not let a clerical label become an excuse to ignore an identity ambiguity.

## 4. Unknown/missing
If M08 says "delivery appointment pending" and RC is blank, you do not manufacture a time. Record UNKNOWN/MISSING and determine whether M09 can proceed under supplied policy or must HOLD/ESCALATE.

## 5. Correction loop
**STOP → CITE THE CONFLICT → REQUEST CORRECTION/CLARIFICATION → RECEIVE RESPONSE → VERIFY NEW EVIDENCE → VERSION IT → RECONCILE AGAIN → DOCUMENT RESOLUTION**

A broker saying "it's fine" may be useful communication evidence, but if the controlled workflow requires a corrected written document for a material term, the dispatcher must obtain that evidence or escalate.

## 6. Worked case
M08: $2,450, no driver assist, Tue 14:00 pickup.  
RC v1: $2,450, driver assist required, Tue 14:00.

Matrix:
- rate: MATCH;
- pickup: MATCH;
- driver assist: MISMATCH — MATERIAL → CORRECT/HOLD.

Broker sends RC v2 removing driver assist but changing pickup to Tue 12:00 without explanation.

Do **not** assume v2 is now clean. Version it, extract it again, and identify the new pickup mismatch. One correction can introduce another problem.

## 7. Later-workflow issue
Suppose booked delivery is Wednesday 10:00 and the driver facts suggest a possible time-feasibility concern. M09 records the booked appointment accurately and flags **HAND OFF TO M10/M11 FOR FEASIBILITY**. M09 does not invent a routing/HOS conclusion.

## 8. Coached practice
Evidence:
- M08 rate $2,600.
- RC v1 rate $2,600 but says "team required."
- Carrier profile supplied to M09 does not establish a team.

Classify: MATCH on rate; UNKNOWN/MATERIAL OPERATING REQUIREMENT on team availability; HOLD and verify/escalate. Do not dispatch a solo driver based on hope.

## 9. Common failures
- choosing verbal over written without controlled resolution;
- choosing written over verbal without noticing a conflict;
- correcting only the first discrepancy;
- failing to re-review a revised document;
- calling missing evidence "probably okay";
- treating every difference as equally critical instead of classifying consequence.

### Part 03 operating rule
**A revised document is new evidence, not automatic proof that every old problem is fixed. Reconcile the whole controlling version.**
