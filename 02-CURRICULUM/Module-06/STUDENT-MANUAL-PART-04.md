**CLASSIFICATION:** CURRENT-DATE MATERIAL / FEDERAL SYSTEM

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 4 (FACT-M06-002)

**RELATED TERMS:** SAFER, Legacy L&I

**NOTES:** Reverify before publication.

**CLASSIFICATION:** FEDERAL SYSTEM

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 4

**RELATED TERMS:** Motus

**NOTES:** Verifies entity, not individual contact.

### Legacy L&I

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Historical-record review system as of May 14, 2026; current Motus filings are not reflected there.

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 4

**RELATED TERMS:** Motus

### BMC-84

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** FMCSA form evidencing a surety bond satisfying broker financial security requirements.

**CLASSIFICATION:** LAW / REGULATION

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 6 (FACT-M06-003)

**RELATED TERMS:** BMC-85

**NOTES:** 49 CFR § 387.307; alternative to BMC-85.

### BMC-85

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** FMCSA form evidencing a trust fund satisfying broker financial security requirements.

**CLASSIFICATION:** LAW / REGULATION / CURRENT-DATE MATERIAL

### BOC-3

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** FMCSA form designating a process agent for a regulated entity.

**CLASSIFICATION:** REGULATION

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06 (referenced)

**RELATED TERMS:** Broker Authority

**NOTES:** Introductory mention only.

### BEC (Business Email Compromise)

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** A fraud pattern in which a legitimate email account is compromised and used to send fraudulent requests.

**CLASSIFICATION:** CYBERSECURITY / OPERATIONAL BEST PRACTICE

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 10 (FACT-M06-005)

**RELATED TERMS:** ATO, Callback Verification

**NOTES:** Do not investigate forensically — verify and escalate instead.

### ATO (Account Takeover)

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Unauthorized control of a legitimate account by a third party.

**CLASSIFICATION:** CYBERSECURITY / OPERATIONAL BEST PRACTICE

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 10

**RELATED TERMS:** BEC

**NOTES:** Related to, sometimes used interchangeably with, BEC.

### Typosquatting

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Registering a domain name deceptively similar to a legitimate one to mislead recipients.

**CLASSIFICATION:** CYBERSECURITY TERM

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 9

**RELATED TERMS:** Display-Name Spoofing

**NOTES:** One of several email/domain risk patterns to recognize.

### Display-Name Spoofing

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Presenting a familiar display name attached to an unfamiliar or fraudulent underlying email address.

**CLASSIFICATION:** CYBERSECURITY TERM

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 9

**RELATED TERMS:** Typosquatting

**NOTES:** Requires checking the actual address, not just the displayed name.

### Callback Verification

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Confirming a request or change by calling a previously trusted, independently verified contact number.

**CLASSIFICATION:** OPERATIONAL BEST PRACTICE

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 11

**RELATED TERMS:** Trusted Contact

**NOTES:** The core defense against BEC and payment-redirection fraud.

### Trusted Contact

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** A verified contact channel established independently of the message currently being evaluated.

**CLASSIFICATION:** OPERATIONAL BEST PRACTICE

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 8

**RELATED TERMS:** Callback Verification

**NOTES:** Never derived from the suspicious message itself.

### Rate Confirmation

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** A document confirming the agreed terms of a load, including rate, parties, and shipment details.

**CLASSIFICATION:** INDUSTRY DOCUMENT

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 13

**RELATED TERMS:** Document Cross-Checking

**NOTES:** Mismatches require VERIFY, not automatic fraud conclusion.

### Co-Brokering

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** An authorized arrangement in which more than one broker is knowingly and legitimately involved in arranging a shipment.

**CLASSIFICATION:** LEGAL / INDUSTRY TERMINOLOGY — QUALIFIED

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 14 (FACT-M06-006)

**RELATED TERMS:** Double Brokering

**NOTES:** Not synonymous with double brokering.

### Double Brokering

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Commonly used industry term for unauthorized re-brokering, including a carrier transferring freight to another carrier without the original broker's/shipper's knowledge.

**CLASSIFICATION:** LEGAL / INDUSTRY TERMINOLOGY — QUALIFIED

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 06, Section 14

**RELATED TERMS:** Co-Brokering

**NOTES:** Not provable from duplicate listings alone.

### Unauthorized Brokerage

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Brokering activity conducted without required FMCSA broker authority.

**CLASSIFICATION:** LAW / REGULATION

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02 (established), referenced here

**RELATED TERMS:** Broker Authority

**NOTES:** See Module 02 for consequences framework.
