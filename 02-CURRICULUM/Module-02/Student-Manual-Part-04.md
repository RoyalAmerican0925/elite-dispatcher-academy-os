# Appendix A: Module 02 — Terminology & Formula Ledger Candidates

No formula entries are applicable to Module 02\.

### Broker

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** A person who, for compensation, arranges or offers to arrange transportation of property by an authorized motor carrier, and who is not a motor carrier or qualifying employee/bona fide agent of one.

**CLASSIFICATION:** STATUTE / REGULATION

**FACT ID:** FACT-M02-001

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 5

**RELATED TERMS:** Bona Fide Agent, Motor Carrier, Broker Authority

**NOTES:** Core definitional term; 49 U.S.C. § 13102 / 49 CFR § 371.2(a).

### Bona Fide Agent

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** A person who is part of the normal organization of a motor carrier, performing duties under the carrier's direction pursuant to a preexisting agreement providing a continuing relationship, and precluded from exercising discretion in allocating traffic.

**CLASSIFICATION:** REGULATION

**FACT ID:** FACT-M02-002

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 6

**RELATED TERMS:** Broker, Allocation of Traffic, Preexisting Agreement

**NOTES:** 49 CFR § 371.2(b); do not add a "written contract required" element to the regulatory text itself — see Section 6 qualification.

### Dispatch Service

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** An industry-functional description, not a defined federal legal category. Activities may fall under broker or bona fide agent treatment depending on nature and scope.

**CLASSIFICATION:** FMCSA GUIDANCE (interpretive, not binding law)

**FACT ID:** N/A

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 7

**RELATED TERMS:** Broker, Bona Fide Agent

**NOTES:** No numerical carrier limit and no separate "dispatcher license" framework exists — see Myths 2, 3, 8 in Section 20\.

### Allocation of Traffic

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Any exercise of discretion on an agent's part in deciding which represented motor carrier receives a given load.

**CLASSIFICATION:** FMCSA GUIDANCE (interpreting REGULATION)

**FACT ID:** N/A

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 9

**RELATED TERMS:** Bona Fide Agent, Overlapping Operations

**NOTES:** Central concept of this module — see Section 9 and Scenario Lab, Section 22\.

### Preexisting Agreement / Continuing Relationship

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** The regulatory requirement that a bona fide agent operate under an agreement made in advance that establishes an ongoing (not one-off) relationship with the carrier.

**CLASSIFICATION:** REGULATION

**FACT ID:** FACT-M02-002

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 8

**RELATED TERMS:** Bona Fide Agent, Written Agreements

**NOTES:** 49 CFR § 371.2(b) text; written form is a guidance-recommended factor, not a stated regulatory requirement.

### Broker Authority

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** Federal operating authority, obtained through FMCSA registration, that authorizes a person to lawfully conduct brokerage of property transportation.

**CLASSIFICATION:** REGULATION

**FACT ID:** FACT-M02-006

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 18

**RELATED TERMS:** Broker, BMC-84, BMC-85, BOC-3

**NOTES:** Introductory-level only in Module 02; full broker compliance is out of scope for this module.

### BMC-84

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** FMCSA form used to evidence a surety bond satisfying a broker's or freight forwarder's financial security requirement.

**CLASSIFICATION:** REGULATION

**FACT ID:** FACT-M02-006

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 18

**RELATED TERMS:** BMC-85, Broker Authority

**NOTES:** 49 CFR § 387.307; alternative to BMC-85 trust filing.

### BMC-85

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** FMCSA form used to evidence a trust fund satisfying a broker's or freight forwarder's financial security requirement.

**CLASSIFICATION:** REGULATION

**FACT ID:** FACT-M02-006

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 18

**RELATED TERMS:** BMC-84, Broker Authority

NOTES: Updated trustee-eligibility requirements took effect January 16, 2026\. Three distinct timelines apply and must not be interchanged: a 30-day cure window for trustee ineligibility, a 7-business-day replenishment window after an FMCSA financial-security-shortfall notice, and a separate 7-calendar-day trust-asset-liquidity standard the trust must meet on an ongoing basis. Current rule verified August 27, 2026; recheck at the final publication gate because this is date-sensitive regulatory material.

### BOC-3

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** FMCSA form used to designate a process agent for a regulated motor carrier, broker, or freight forwarder.

**CLASSIFICATION:** REGULATION

**FACT ID:** FACT-M02-006

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 18

**RELATED TERMS:** Broker Authority

**NOTES:** Introductory mention only; full process-agent mechanics out of scope for Module 02\.

### Principal

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** In this module's context, the motor carrier on whose behalf a bona fide agent acts.

**CLASSIFICATION:** INDUSTRY / REGULATORY CONCEPT

**FACT ID:** N/A

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 6

**RELATED TERMS:** Agent, Bona Fide Agent

**NOTES:** Used descriptively; not independently defined by a specific cited regulation in this module.

### Agent

**APPROVED DEFINITION:** In this module's context, a party (such as a bona fide agent) acting on behalf of and under the direction of a principal carrier.

**CLASSIFICATION:** INDUSTRY / REGULATORY CONCEPT

**FACT ID:** N/A

**FIRST-USE LOCATION:** Module 02, Section 6

**RELATED TERMS:** Principal, Bona Fide Agent

**NOTES:** Used descriptively alongside "Bona Fide Agent," the specifically defined regulatory term.
