## Controlling Purpose

Module 02 develops the student's ability to remain on the lawful side of freight-dispatch operations. It is not a slogan-level “dispatcher versus broker” lesson. The graduate must be able to examine a real freight arrangement, identify who represents whom, determine the source and scope of authority, distinguish carrier-side agency activity from activity that may constitute brokerage, recognize fraud and contract traps, identify missing facts, document the analysis, and stop or escalate before participating in an unauthorized transaction.

**Academy principle: SUBSTANCE OVER LABEL.** Job title, contract title, marketing language, tax classification, and industry custom do not independently determine regulatory status. Actual conduct, relationships, authority, control, freight source, carrier-selection discretion, and money flow must be examined together.

## Primary Authority Stack

1. **49 U.S.C. § 13102** — statutory definitions, including broker and motor carrier concepts.
2. **49 U.S.C. §§ 13901–13906** — registration and operating-authority framework.
3. **49 U.S.C. § 14916** — unlawful brokerage activities, civil penalty framework, and private cause of action.
4. **49 CFR Part 371**, especially **§ 371.2** — broker and bona fide-agent definitions.
5. **FMCSA Final Guidance, 88 FR 39368 (June 16, 2023), Docket FMCSA-2022-0134** — fact-specific guidance addressing broker/bona fide-agent definitions and dispatch services.
6. **49 CFR Part 387**, especially **§ 387.307** — broker/freight-forwarder financial responsibility.
7. **Current FMCSA broker/freight-forwarder financial-responsibility guidance** — 2026 implementation and provider rules.
8. **49 CFR Part 386, Appendix B** — current civil-penalty schedule where applicable.

Students must distinguish **statute → regulation → controlling judicial authority where relevant → official agency guidance → contract → industry interpretation/training material**. Contract terms can allocate commercial rights and duties between parties but cannot authorize conduct prohibited by controlling law.

## Required Competency Model

The Module 02 professional operating sequence is:

**RECOGNIZE → ANALYZE → VERIFY → DOCUMENT → DECIDE → ESCALATE**

The operational decision chain is:

**STOP → IDENTIFY → TRACE → CHECK → CLASSIFY → ACT → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE**

Permitted outcomes include:

- **PROCEED / PERMITTED AGENT ACTIVITY**
- **HOLD**
- **VERIFY / INSUFFICIENT FACTS**
- **ESCALATE**
- **DO NOT PROCEED AS PROPOSED**
- **BROKER AUTHORITY REQUIRED / POTENTIAL BROKERAGE ACTIVITY**, when supported by the verified fact pattern and appropriate level of instruction

Students are never required to guess when a material fact is unknown.

## Red-Flag Doctrine

A red flag is not automatically proof of wrongdoing. It triggers:

**STOP → VERIFY → DOCUMENT → ESCALATE**

Examples include requests to use another carrier's MC or insurance, conceal the actual hauling carrier, obtain freight before a represented carrier is identified, collect shipper funds and redistribute carrier payments, sign broad agreements without authority, move a committed load to another independent carrier without proper authorization, hide rate confirmations from the carrier owner, or rely on an active MC number as proof that the contacting person is legitimate.

## Employment Outcome

A successful graduate can operate within defined dispatcher authority; identify the represented carrier and freight source; analyze carrier-selection discretion; distinguish bona fide-agent indicators from brokerage indicators; recognize missing facts; verify basic broker authority, financial responsibility, and identity indicators; identify unauthorized rebrokering and misrepresentation risks; review authority-related contract clauses; document decisions; and refuse or escalate transactions that exceed delegated authority or require concealment or misrepresentation.

The graduate is not trained to act as an attorney or issue final legal opinions. The graduate is trained to recognize, analyze, verify, document, decide, and escalate.
